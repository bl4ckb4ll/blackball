# The Philosopher's Wife — source register

**Verified / accessed as of 18 October 2026.** This is a provenance map, not a claim that full books have been read. Distinguish the **person who witnessed or wrote** from the **editor**, **interviewer**, **biographer**, and **webpage reproducing the account**. Two websites reprinting the same interview do not count as two witnesses.

**Grades:** **A** = participant-authored primary text in inspected scholarly edition or edited transcription; **B** = first-person participant's testimony as printed in an inspected interview; **C** = archive/catalogue entry, direct evidence that an item exists and how to retrieve it, not proof of its full contents; **D** = inspected secondary biography review/analysis, including quotations reproduced from other books; **Q** = identified, not read. *A* grades are direct author testimony but may be translated, edited or retrospective and are not automatic proof of independent truth. Partial access and dependence on intermediaries are noted.

## Material that must still be acquired

**First priority:** critical text of Davidson pp. 97/140/285/287/425–26; Barss's original wife/sons interviews and source notes; Einstein *Collected Papers* vol. 8; Sofia's 2017 **two-way letters**; Russell's complete 1934 sworn affidavit *and* Dora's corresponding court record; Jane's original and revised memoir; Jenny's MECW volume facsimiles. Each acquisition should specify edition, page, passage date and witness. Treat a catalogue description as a **lead**, not proof of every sentence in the unretrieved letter.

**Method:** biographical appearance of one misbehaving man can be compelling writing. It is not a controlled comparison; this register is meant to stop the evidence from disappearing beneath the story.
