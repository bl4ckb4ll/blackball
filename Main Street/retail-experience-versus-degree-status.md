## Comparison to preserve

A person who enters retail directly may accumulate four years of:

- reliable attendance evidence;
- customer and conflict experience;
- knowledge of store operations;
- scheduling and staffing experience;
- inventory and loss-control knowledge;
- familiarity with the employer’s systems;
- references and internal sponsorship;
- eligibility for lead and management openings;
- earnings rather than tuition expense.

A graduate entering the same workplace four years later may possess other knowledge and longer-run options. Do not assume either path dominates. Compare the paths at the moment they converge in the same workplace.

Measure who supervises whom, pay, promotion timing, debt, performance, retention, and whether the degree changes the actual work. Also record status beliefs: people without degrees may continue to feel inferior even when they have greater responsibility, better employer evaluations, and a stronger promotion record.

That mismatch between **credential rank** and **demonstrated workplace rank** is itself an outcome of education marketing and social classification.
