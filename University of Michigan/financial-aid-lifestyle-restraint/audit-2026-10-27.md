# Evidence and arithmetic audit — 2026-10-27

This audit retains the research and calculator introduced by [PR #182](https://github.com/bl4ckb4ll/blackball/pull/182), which addresses [issue #85](https://github.com/bl4ckb4ll/blackball/issues/85). It distinguishes the university's published advice from Blackball's model and does not promote assumptions to observations.

## Attribution and cost scale

- **U-M** publishes the Office of Financial Aid's *Your Money, Your Life* advice, including the 'live like a pauper' remark and the 'Not at all' answer to whether students must always forgo movies and eating out. The latter expressly contradicts a stronger attribution that movie tickets supposedly pay tuition: https://sites.google.com/a/umich.edu/your-money-your-life/home/personal-finance-topics/peer-pressure-at-college
- **U-M** publishes 2026–27 **fall/winter** LSA undergraduate annual resident lower/upper COA **$40,194/$42,566**, and nonresident **$88,394/$93,096**, composed of tuition/fees and living/other allowances: https://finaid.umich.edu/getting-started/estimating-costs
- **Blackball**, not U-M, hypothesizes discretionary cuts totaling $2,980 per academic year ($11,920 in four years). Its arithmetic is $11,920/$165,520 = **7.20%** resident and $11,920/$362,980 = **3.28%** nonresident. Neither the cut amounts nor the unchanged-cost four-year multiplication are U-M projections.
- **Four-year period** assumes exactly two lower- and two upper-division fall/winter budgets at fixed 2026–27 prices. Summer, price growth, financing interest, exact net price, state-specific scholarship eligibility and actual expenses are not incorporated.

## Verification scope and remaining questions

The companion Bash tests check normal outputs, malformed inputs, numeric-domain boundaries, concurrent writers to a file via atomic rename, and note assertions. **They do not implement HTTP endpoints, `200` statuses, financial transactions, rejection/queuing of competing writers, or double-spend prevention.** A last-writer-wins atomic rename demonstrates whole-file replacement, not mutual exclusion.

The full Bash suite and the committed independent Python-to-calculator integration oracle were **not executed in the current audit environment**; execution against the exact revised branch is required for a green receipt. The `bc` and independent Decimal **spot checks above were executed**, but they are not a substitute for end-to-end tests.

Open evidence: live O'Reilly pay at the specified store, a signed apartment/utility quote, student-specific aid/net price, historical snapshots and dates of the Google Sites financial-literacy pages, the remembered stronger tuition/movie comparison, and the separate family-capital/internship access research question. Maintain explicit uncertainty rather than filling these gaps with model outputs.
