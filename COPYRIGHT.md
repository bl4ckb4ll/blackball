# Copyright and redistribution policy

Blackball distinguishes **being readable somewhere** from **being legal to redistribute here**.

## May be mirrored

A full text may be committed under `texts/` only when at least one affirmative basis has been verified:

1. the work is public domain in the relevant jurisdiction; or
2. the rights holder released the text under an explicit license permitting redistribution (for example, an appropriate Creative Commons license); or
3. another explicit grant of redistribution rights applies.

Record the evidence beside the local copy.

## Link only

Do not copy a scan merely because Internet Archive has it. In particular, treat the following as link-only unless separate rights evidence exists:

- `Access-restricted-item: true`
- `printdisabled`
- Open Library `Borrow` or `Preview Only`
- controlled digital lending items
- an `lcpdf`/`lcp` identifier
- a scan that exposes metadata or OCR but no affirmative open license

`No suitable files to display here` is also not evidence of an open license.

## What we can still record for restricted books

For a restricted or in-copyright book, Blackball can still record:

- bibliographic metadata;
- the exact Internet Archive/Open Library identifier;
- lawful links to the lender, publisher, catalog, and reviews;
- our own summary and analysis;
- short quotations within applicable limits;
- acknowledgments, contributor names, and citation/provenance data obtained from lawful public sources.

## Current result

The initial Roger L. Geiger and William Deresiewicz Internet Archive copies located on 2026-08-30 are controlled/preview/print-disabled copies. **No full book PDF from those records has been copied into this repository.**
