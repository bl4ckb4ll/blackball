# Adecco mobile service-technician message: job-advertisement receipt

Capture received: **2026-10-15**, from a job seeker's Indeed conversation screenshots.
Source type: **user-supplied screenshots of a recruiter message and attached job-opportunity card**. This is a direct observation of what an applicant was shown, not an independent verification of the vacancy, employer, recruiter, or work.
Source owner as displayed: Adecco Staffing / its recruiting intermediary. The private-message correspondent's name and contact address are intentionally omitted from this public transcript.

## What is visible

The message describes a **direct-hire** first-shift technician position in the **Ann Arbor/Detroit area**, Monday–Friday with occasional Saturdays. Compensation is described as **“paying competitively, based on experience”**, without a number.

The attached card is headed **“Service Technician”** and labeled **“Adecco Finance and Accounting”**. Its opening describes an unidentified **“premier mobile mechanic service company”** needing a full-time mobile technician.

The message says the technician will repair and maintain **trash compactors, recycle balers, truck docks, and other material-handling equipment**. It calls for strong skills in **electrical controls and hydraulics**, plus some knowledge and experience in **structural welding**.

In the visible requirements near the bottom of the card:

> Exceptional communication and customer service skills are necessary, as you operate in the public eye without the buffer of a traditional service desk.

A **good driving record** is also requested. Visible benefits include health/vision/dental insurance with an advertised 75% premium coverage upon hire, paid time off, an advertised “25% 401K Match” after one year, an employee stock ownership program after one year, and uniforms/boot allowance. The full job-card text is **not visible** across the supplied captures; do not infer that these excerpts exhaust its terms.

## Observations distinguished from inferences

1. **Observed:** A card labeled “Finance and Accounting” describes mechanical/electrical/hydraulic service work. **Not established:** that an accounting firm employs the technician, or whether Indeed, an Adecco posting system, or a human selected the label.
2. **Observed:** The actual hiring company's identity and a numeric wage are absent **from the displayed portions**. **Not established:** that they were unavailable elsewhere in the complete listing or on request.
3. **Observed:** The same outreach uses an explicit *communication skills* criterion while giving only vague pay and employer information and showing a mismatched card label. **Not established:** that the recruiter is unqualified, the message was automated, or that actual workplace communication is unimportant.
4. **Observed:** Tasks and several technical skill areas are named. **Not established:** actual daily work, required proficiency, welding frequency, training offered, compensation, safety practices, travel radius, or the worker's eventual experience.
