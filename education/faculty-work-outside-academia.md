## Decision and evidence

- **Any outside work.** Count affirmative evidence that the person carried out a role or task beyond academic study, teaching, research, publishing, or service. This can include one shift, a placement while studying, a side job, paid or volunteer work, and the clinical component of a university appointment. A clearly named consulting client under a consulting heading is enough to document consulting. The sponsor of an academic grant, an authorization to use data, or a meeting venue supplies a different kind of evidence.
- **Sustained outside responsibility.** Look for explicit recurrence or an operational role with a documented span of responsibility. Preserve the actual period and cadence. The role may be seasonal or concurrent. A date range on a consulting entry gives the reported span of that entry. With cadence, scope, and deliverables unstated, the amount of sustained responsibility stays open. An expressly one-time task supports a limited affirmative answer. Neither rule estimates full-time equivalent years from calendar years.
- **Coverage.** Record whether the relevant CV pages were readable. A positive finding survives gaps elsewhere. For a negative finding, distinguish absence in the reviewed CV from incomplete coverage. Keep the date of the CV and its version separate from the person's lifetime history.
