## Why this branch matters to a teachers’-union graph

Tyack is not primarily a union history. Leroux cites him because teacher organization developed inside a school system whose authority structure was itself changing. His sources lead backward into the professionalization and centralization of administration, NEA superintendency institutions, women’s access to leadership, equal-pay politics, and the ideology of efficiency. Those institutional changes help explain what teachers were organizing **against, within, and sometimes alongside**.
