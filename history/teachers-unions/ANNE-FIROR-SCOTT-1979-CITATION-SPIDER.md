## What this generation adds

The recursion has now moved from teacher organization into the **institutional genealogy of women’s teacher education**. Scott’s notes expose a dense primary-source archive at the Emma Willard School, early teacher-improvement associations, correspondence with Henry Barnard, nineteenth-century professional journals, Almira Phelps’s pedagogy, and the argument that female seminaries helped train women for intellectual and paid work rather than functioning only as finishing schools.
