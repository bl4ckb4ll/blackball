## Why Tyack remains in the union graph

Tyack is a history of superintendency rather than unionism. Leroux uses it to locate teacher organization inside the changing authority structure of public schooling. The citation trail therefore documents the professional and administrative hierarchy with which teacher organizations negotiated, resisted, and sometimes identified.
