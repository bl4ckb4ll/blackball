# Mike Duncan — *Revolutions*, Season 9: The Mexican Revolution

Mike Duncan's Season 9 is a useful narrative map of the Mexican Revolution, but this directory treats it as an **entry point into sources**, not the end of the source trail. The main research object is therefore the season **plus the bibliography Duncan used**.

These arrows are research cross-references. They become strict citation edges only where Duncan's episode notes, bibliography, or explicit acknowledgements establish the source relation.

## Provenance rule

Do not treat a platform's copied episode description, a YouTube repost, a Reddit recommendation, or a standard scholarly work on the Revolution as evidence that Duncan used a particular book. The [bibliography spider](REVOLUTIONS-BIBLIOGRAPHY-SPIDER.md) records confidence separately and preserves the recovery frontier.
