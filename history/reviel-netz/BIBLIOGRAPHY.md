# Reviel Netz bibliography and source ledger

Status key:

- **verified** — bibliographic identity checked against an author/institutional, publisher, or project source.
- **verified project bibliography** — appears in the Archimedes Palimpsest project's own bibliography.
- **queue** — relevant item or bibliography exists, but individual citation edges have not yet been checked.

The point of the status labels is to avoid treating topical relevance as a citation edge.

## Books by Netz

### Greek mathematics and cognitive history

- **verified** — Reviel Netz, *The Shaping of Deduction in Greek Mathematics: A Study in Cognitive History*. Cambridge University Press, 1999. *Ideas in Context* 51. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1017/CBO9780511543296. Cambridge describes the book as a study of Greek mathematical practice, the emergence of deduction, and cognitive history, beginning from geometry and the lettered diagram.
  - Publisher: https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/the-shaping-of-deduction-in-greek-mathematics/6801E135656F4401979F431F6FF48A28

- **verified** — Reviel Netz, *The Transformation of Mathematics in the Early Mediterranean World: From Problems to Equations*. Cambridge University Press, 2004 (later digital/paperback publication metadata varies by edition). DOI: https://doi.org/10.1017/CBO9780511720000. Follows an Archimedean problem through Eutocius and the Arabic mathematical tradition to Omar Khayyam, using the sequence to study a change from geometric problem to equation.
  - Publisher: https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/transformation-of-mathematics-in-the-early-mediterranean-world/F8B69BB197D898C3B0830CF200C93699

- **verified** — Reviel Netz, *Ludic Proof: Greek Mathematics and the Alexandrian Aesthetic*. Cambridge University Press, 2009. Cambridge describes it as a study of scientific writing style in relation to contemporary literary style.
  - Publisher: https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/ludic-proof/2C2B680721D6AB9B4D76A808DCC79831
  - Bibliography landing page: https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/ludic-proof/bibliography/7865F9CB3ADF27C52D28405C7140B8B3

- **verified** — Reviel Netz, *A New History of Greek Mathematics*. Cambridge University Press, 2022. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1017/9781108982801. Chapters run from Near Eastern antecedents and early Greek mathematics through Archimedes, astronomy, canonization, and the legacy into modern science.
  - Publisher: https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/a-new-history-of-greek-mathematics/8FF4CD50DEBDD5E0ABF75334027E79AB

### Archimedes

- **verified** — Archimedes; Reviel Netz, ed. and trans., *The Works of Archimedes, Volume 1: The Two Books On the Sphere and the Cylinder: Translation and Commentary*. Cambridge University Press, 2004.
  - Confirmed on Stanford's publication/profile pages and in the Archimedes Palimpsest bibliography.

- **verified** — Archimedes; Reviel Netz, ed. and trans., *The Works of Archimedes, Volume 2: On Spirals: Translation and Commentary*. Cambridge University Press, 2017. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1017/9781139019279.
  - Publisher: https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/the-works-of-archimedes/364D32635C6E0E6515C010188516C3E7

- **verified** — Reviel Netz and William Noel, *The Archimedes Codex*. Weidenfeld & Nicolson, 2007; later editions under expanded subtitles. Popular account of the rediscovery, conservation, imaging, transcription, and mathematical significance of the Archimedes Palimpsest.
  - Project bibliography: https://archimedespalimpsest.org/links/bibliography.php

- **verified** — Reviel Netz, William Noel, Natalie Tchernetska, and Nigel Wilson, eds., *The Archimedes Palimpsest*, 2 vols. Cambridge University Press, 2011. Volume 1 contains catalogue/commentary; Volume 2 contains facsimile/transcription.
  - Stanford: https://classics.stanford.edu/publications/archimedes-palimpsest-vols-1-and-2
  - Project bibliography: https://archimedespalimpsest.org/links/bibliography.php

### Literary culture and the ancient book world

- **verified** — Reviel Netz, *Scale, Space and Canon in Ancient Literary Culture*. Cambridge University Press, 2020. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1017/9781108686945. Its quantitative chapter explicitly models audience size, numbers of authors, and the scale of the ancient “bibliosphere.”
  - Cambridge book/contents: https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/scale-space-and-canon-in-ancient-literary-culture/02A4542A77B1F57801F1A6B8F564FE0E

- **verified** — Reviel Netz, *Why the Ancient Greeks Matter: The Problematic Miracle that was Greece*. Cambridge University Press, 2025. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1017/9781009505604. The book explicitly revisits the “Greek miracle,” Greek science, canon formation, and the afterlife of Greek intellectual culture.
  - Publisher: https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/why-the-ancient-greeks-matter/6C08DD345E62246E02BC0AAD89C3D37C

### Modern technology, ecology, and coercive space

- **verified** — Reviel Netz, *Barbed Wire: An Ecology of Modernity*. Wesleyan University Press, 2004; later paperback edition. Studies barbed wire from cattle and colonization of the American West through concentration camps and the Gulag, using restriction of movement as its organizing problem.
  - Publisher: https://www.weslpress.org/9780819569592/barbed-wire/

## Institutional author anchor

Stanford currently lists Netz as Suppes Professor in Greek Mathematics and Astronomy, Professor of Classics, and by courtesy Professor of Philosophy. The profile characterizes his main field as the history of pre-modern mathematics and his broader research as the history of cognitive practices, including visual culture, history of the book, literacy, and numeracy.

https://classics.stanford.edu/people/reviel-netz
