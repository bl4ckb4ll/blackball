## Why keep this material with the rest of Netz

The poetry is not an incidental biographical curiosity. Netz's scholarship repeatedly pays attention to literary form, repeated formulae, style, canon, and the relationship between mathematical and poetic practices. *Ludic Proof* in particular explicitly situates Hellenistic mathematical writing alongside Alexandrian literary aesthetics.

That relationship should be investigated rather than assumed. A later spider should distinguish:
