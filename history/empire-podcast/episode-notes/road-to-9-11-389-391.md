# Empire episodes 389–391 — Road to 9/11

**Public-feed cutoff:** 30 August 2026. Episode 392 is visible on Apple as subscriber early access on 2 September and is not treated as a generally released episode here.

## 389 — Origin of al-Qaeda

William Dalrymple is joined by journalist Jason Burke. The published description traces intellectual and organizational roots of radical Islamism through the Muslim Brotherhood, Sayyid Qutb, Egyptian prison torture and Ayman al-Zawahiri.

Book trail:

- Jason Burke, *Al-Qaeda: The True Story of Radical Islam*.
- Jason Burke, *The Revolutionists*.

Blackball angle: distinguish ideology from organizational capacity. Record prisons, state repression, funding, recruitment networks, travel, safe havens and armed organizations separately rather than treating "radicalization" as a single variable.

## 390 — Soviet-Afghan War

Burke returns for the Afghan-war setting, Abdullah Azzam and the institutional formation of al-Qaeda. The episode explicitly asks about the common claim that the CIA funded Osama bin Laden.

This is exactly the sort of claim that should be source-audited. Separate U.S./Saudi/Pakistani support to Afghan mujahideen from direct organizational or financial support to bin Laden/al-Qaeda, and require evidence for each link.

## 391 — rise of Osama bin Laden

Anita Anand and William Dalrymple are joined by Steve Coll, author of *The Bin Ladens: An Arab Family in the American Century*. The description focuses on Mohammed bin Laden's construction fortune, the family's relationship to the Saudi state, Osama's upbringing and his political formation.

This episode adds a class/institutional dimension often lost in biographies of terrorists: bin Laden was not simply an isolated ideologue; he emerged from an extraordinarily wealthy contracting family embedded in Saudi state development.
