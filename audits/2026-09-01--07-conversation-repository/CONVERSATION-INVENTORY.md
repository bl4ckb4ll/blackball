The audit passes also recovered **17 substantive in-window discussions/captures outside that card snapshot**; those are retained below rather than discarded. Titles/snippets alone were used only to route obviously operational conversations or to identify source names for repository searches.

Every in-window project card appears below. A ledger code means the substantive content is consolidated into that item in [LEDGER.md](LEDGER.md). `OP` means repository/PR/merge administration, recorded in [REPOSITORY-STATE.md](REPOSITORY-STATE.md) but not counted as a distinct intellectual request. `X` means the card did not contain a Blackball research item (for example, an emotional check-in). This prevents both omission and double counting.
