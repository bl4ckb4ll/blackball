# Nickelodeon opportunity map — first pass, 2026-09-30

Nickelodeon needs to be modeled as a brand/studio labor market inside Paramount's wider shared corporate, streaming and consumer-products organization.

## Nickelodeon Animation

Nickelodeon Animation's own careers page describes studios in Los Angeles and New York and explicitly names work by:

- artists;
- storytellers;
- writers;
- production teams;
- voice actors;
- other animation specialists.

It links to a studio job board and runs a full-time paid, yearlong Nickelodeon Writing Program with classes, executive mentorship, networking and writers-room exposure.

Sources:
- https://www.nickanimation.com/work-here/?hl=en-US
- https://www.nickanimation.com/

## Paramount recruiting surface

Paramount Careers has a dedicated **Nickelodeon Animation** category and a brand field that can expose Nickelodeon-branded requisitions. As of 2026-09-30 the dedicated Nickelodeon Animation category reports no matching open positions, while the broader Paramount inventory has recently shown Nickelodeon-branded marketing work.

Source:
- https://careers.paramount.com/go/Nickelodeon-Animation/8711300/

Paramount's wider shared functions include Production, Creative, Marketing, Consumer Products, Technology, Research, Finance/Accounting, Sales and General Business. Do not attribute a Paramount shared-function role to Nickelodeon unless the brand, requisition or reporting structure supports that edge.

Source:
- https://careers.paramount.com/go/All-Current-Job-Opportunities/8710000/

## Entry gates

The Nickelodeon Writing Program is a concrete paid development route for writers. Other early-career, internship and production-assistant routes need a dated census rather than assumption from the parent company.
