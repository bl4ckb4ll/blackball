# Consulting-employer map — first pass, 2026-09-30

The useful object is not "consulting." These firms expose different internal structures and buy different kinds of labor.

## Booz Allen Hamilton

Public structure is unusually customer/mission-oriented.

Current company reporting describes major customer markets including:

- Defense;
- Civil;
- Intelligence;

and technology capability areas including:

- artificial intelligence;
- cybersecurity;
- data;
- software/digital engineering;
- cloud and infrastructure;
- advanced engineering;
- quantum and other emerging technologies.

Booz Allen states that it operates as a single profit-and-loss center, so its public customer markets and technical communities should **not** be misrepresented as separate legal profit centers.

Current careers:
- https://careers.boozallen.com/jobs/search/

Current company reporting:
- https://www.boozallen.com/content/dam/home/docs/corp/reports/2026-annual-report-on-form-10-k.pdf

### Mapping target

```text
Booz Allen
→ customer market (Defense / Civil / Intelligence / commercial where sourced)
→ contract / mission / client organization
→ technical capability
→ delivery team
→ program / contract leadership
→ requisition
```

Because contract work can determine clearance, geography, technology, and budget, the **contract/client attachment** may matter more than a generic corporate department name.

## McKinsey & Company

McKinsey publishes both **capability practices** and **industry practices**.

Current capability/practice surface includes at least:

- Operations;
- Strategy & Corporate Finance;
- Risk & Resilience;
- People & Organizational Performance;
- Growth, Marketing & Sales;
- transformation;
- technology, including McKinsey Technology;
- QuantumBlack, AI by McKinsey;
- Leap by McKinsey / business building.

Industry leadership includes groupings such as:

- Advanced Industries;
- Energy & Materials;
- Financial Services;
- Life Sciences;
- Private Equity & Principal Investors;
- Social, Healthcare & Public Entities;
- Technology, Media & Telecommunications;
- Travel, Logistics & Infrastructure.

McKinsey explicitly publishes global practice leadership. That gives Black Ball a better starting point for authority mapping than generic partner biographies.

Sources:
- https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities
- https://www.mckinsey.com/industries
- https://www.mckinsey.com/about-us/overview/our-leadership/practice-leadership

The Operations Practice also exposes specialist structures such as the Advanced Operations Group, with front-line consultants, industry experts, and implementation coaches.

## Bain & Company

Bain's career surface separates ordinary management consulting from several integrated teams.

Current visible internal work areas include:

- Management Consulting;
- Technology & Engineering;
- AI, Insights & Solutions (AIS);
- Product, Practice & Knowledge (PPK);
- Analytics, Data & Research;
- Product Management & Innovation;
- Business Operations.

The PPK team is particularly useful because it exposes internal work usually invisible behind the "consultant" label: knowledge systems, practice/product/sector expertise, commercialization of intellectual property, go-to-market work, reporting, analytics, and operations.

Bain's Technology & Engineering area explicitly includes software, data, ML, QA, automation, front-end, systems engineering, design, and product management.

Sources:
- https://www.bain.com/careers/work-with-us/integrated-teams/product-practice-knowledge-ppk/
- https://www.bain.com/careers/work-with-us/integrated-teams/ai-insights-solutions-ais/
- https://www.bain.com/careers/work-with-us/our-work-areas/technology-engineering/
