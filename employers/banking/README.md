# Bank-employer map — first pass, 2026-09-30

"Bank" is too coarse to be a useful job category. Each large bank contains separate consumer, commercial, markets, wealth, technology, operations, risk, finance, product, data, and control organizations.

## JPMorganChase

Current public business areas:

- Asset & Wealth Management;
- Commercial & Investment Banking;
- Consumer & Community Banking;
- Corporate Functions;
- Technology.

Source:
- https://www.jpmorganchase.com/careers/explore-opportunities

JPMorganChase says its technology organization contains roughly 65,000 technologists. Do not treat Technology as detached from the business lines: future mapping should show which technology organizations serve Card, Payments, Markets, Wealth, Consumer Banking, etc.

A particularly useful structural source is the Chase Associate Program. Its rotations expose subunits inside Consumer & Community Banking:

- Auto;
- Business Banking;
- Card Services;
- Connected Commerce;
- Consumer Banking;
- Data & Analytics;
- Digital Product & Experience;
- Finance;
- Home Lending;
- J.P. Morgan Wealth Management;
- Marketing & Communications;
- Controls;
- Operations;
- Risk Management;
- Strategy.

Source:
- https://www.jpmorganchase.com/careers/explore-opportunities/programs/cl-fulltime-associate

That is closer to the desired department map than simply searching "banking jobs."

## Capital One

Capital One's early-career programs expose durable professional tracks:

- Business Analyst;
- Technology;
- Product;
- Project Management / Management;
- Finance.

The Product Development Program explicitly places work across **Tech, Banking, Card and other areas**, which suggests the next useful hierarchy:

```text
Capital One
→ Card / Banking / Enterprise
→ Technology / Product / Data / Analysis / Finance / Operations
→ team
→ hiring authority
```

Sources:
- https://www.capitalonecareers.com/en/get-ahead-with-early-career-programs-for-students
- https://www.capitalonecareers.com/design-develop-and-deliver-products-with-the-pdp-students-tech

A current Product Director posting also exposes a horizontal **Finance Data** product team serving lines of business, regulators, and investors. This is evidence that horizontal product/data organizations cross the business-line structure.

## Why the comparison matters

A worker who says "I want a bank job" could mean at least:

- branch/consumer operations;
- credit;
- fraud/risk;
- commercial lending;
- payments;
- markets;
- wealth management;
- software/infrastructure;
- cybersecurity;
- data science;
- product management;
- finance/accounting;
- controls/compliance;
- operations/project management.

Those routes have different managers, different evidence, and different entry gates.
