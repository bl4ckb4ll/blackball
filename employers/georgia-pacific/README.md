# Georgia-Pacific opportunity map — first pass, 2026-09-30

Georgia-Pacific is a useful household-name case because a familiar consumer/manufacturing brand hides large plant, engineering, maintenance, supply-chain and corporate labor markets.

Georgia-Pacific states that it is part of Koch, Inc., has headquarters in Atlanta, and operates facilities in nearly 30 U.S. states.

Source:
- https://www.gp.com/careers

## Public career functions

Georgia-Pacific's current careers page explicitly exposes:

- Accounting & Finance;
- Early Career;
- Engineering;
- Environmental Health & Safety;
- Information Systems & Technology;
- Maintenance & Skilled Trade;
- Marketing;
- Operations & Manufacturing;
- Sales;
- Supply Chain.

### Engineering

Named engineering disciplines include:

- chemical;
- electrical;
- mechanical;
- process safety;
- project;
- reliability/safety.

### Maintenance and skilled trades

The careers page explicitly names:

- maintenance electricians and technicians;
- E&I technicians;
- electromechanical technicians.

### Information systems and technology

Examples include:

- technology leadership;
- mobile development;
- advanced analytics/data engineering;
- ERP analysis.

### Operations and supply chain

Plant work includes plant management, production, maintenance management and utilities. Supply-chain examples include replenishment analysis, demand planning and logistics.

## Entry gates

Georgia-Pacific has an explicit Early Career recruiting surface for recent graduates. Exact gates need to be measured by requisition and plant rather than generalized from the career-area description.

The skilled-trade route should be measured separately from engineering/new-graduate recruiting because plant maintenance may have very different credential and experience requirements.

## Next measurements

4. Record degree, trade, apprenticeship and experience gates by requisition.
5. Map plant controller, EH&S, process engineering, maintenance and operations authority at facility level where public evidence permits.
