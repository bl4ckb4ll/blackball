# IBM opportunity map — first pass, 2026-09-30

IBM is useful because its current public career structure spans software, hardware, infrastructure, consulting, research, support, sales, and corporate operations, with explicit entry gates that are not all conventional college recruiting.

## Public career areas

IBM currently publishes these career areas:

- Consulting;
- Data & Analytics;
- Enterprise Operations;
- Infrastructure & Technology;
- Product Management;
- Project Management;
- Research;
- Sales;
- Security;
- Software Engineering.

Source:
- https://www.ibm.com/careers/areas

Infrastructure & Technology explicitly includes roles such as:

- Storage Specialist;
- Systems Administrator;
- Mainframe Technical Specialist;
- Technical Support;
- Site Reliability Engineer;
- Networking & Infrastructure.

Source:
- https://www.ibm.com/careers/infrastructure-and-technology

## Explicit entry gates

### Software Engineer Apprentice — Armonk, New York

Current IBM posting 107883:

- approximately 12-month registered Department of Labor apprenticeship;
- high school diploma/GED required;
- Associate degree preferred, not required;
- basic knowledge of one listed programming language;
- no prior professional experience listed as required;
- nationally recognized credential on completion;
- eligible to apply to available IBM full-time roles after graduating from the apprenticeship;
- onsite;
- no visa sponsorship or relocation.

Source:
- https://careers.ibm.com/en_US/careers/JobDetail/Software-Engineer-Apprentice/107883

### Entry Level Back End Developer 2026 — IBM AI on Z

Current posting 73787 sits in Infrastructure & Technology and explicitly attaches the work to the **AI on Z** team.

Advertised work includes:

- Telum / Spyre accelerators;
- PyTorch/TensorFlow;
- compiler/toolchain work;
- Linux on Z / LinuxONE / z/OS;
- open-source contribution;
- C/C++/Java/Go/Python.

The posting says BS in a related field **or equivalent experience**.

Source:
- https://careers.ibm.com/en_US/careers/JobDetail/Entry-Level-Back-End-Developer-2026/73787

## Why IBM matters

IBM demonstrates three quite different access routes inside one corporation:

```text
registered apprenticeship
entry-level software / compiler / AI systems
entry-level hardware / mechanical / electrical systems
```

The next task is to map those gates onto IBM's actual product/business organizations rather than leave them as generic career categories.
