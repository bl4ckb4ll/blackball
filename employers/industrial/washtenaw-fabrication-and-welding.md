# Fabrication and welding work around Washtenaw Community College

Evidence reviewed November 27, 2026. See [the regional employer-demand study](https://github.com/bl4ckb4ll/blackball/issues/195). The addresses below identify company sites. Campus drive times for these sites have yet to be measured. Taylor is included as a wage comparison.

## Quantum Signal AI — Saline

**Company → site → work.** Ford Motor Company subsidiary at 200 N. Ann Arbor St., Saline. It develops vehicle and mobile-robotics systems. The company describes four functions. Software builds sensing, embedded and interface systems. Simulation builds tools for testing intelligent vehicles. Algorithms turn sensor data into decisions. Prototyping builds and tests integrated hardware and software, including custom mechatronics and electronics. [Company functions and site](https://quantumsignalai.com/)

**What the fabrication shop needed done.** A July 14, 2026 Fabrication Technician posting describes an in-person R&D shop that needs someone to build and install prototype parts, review CAD and choose ways to make the parts, use hand and machine tools to cut, bend and weld, run FDM and SLA printers, finish parts, solder and crimp harnesses, and maintain lab equipment. The posting calls for two to five years of related experience, CAD proficiency and an associate degree or equivalent experience. CNC and CAM are listed as pluses. The posting gives no starting rate. The company’s careers page provides a general application. The July role was absent from that page at review. [Dated posting preserved by Ann Arbor SPARK](https://jobs.annarborusa.org/companies/quantum-signal-ai-llc-2/jobs/86208122-fabrication-technician) · [Company careers](https://quantumsignalai.applytojob.com/)

**Who got the work and how.** Mike Rudisill says Quantum Signal’s CEO went to WCC instructors for a shortlist. The company hired him to expand internal fabrication and prototyping. Independent reporting places him at Quantum Signal by March 2020. WCC reused the hire in Winter 2024 *Launch*, March 2024 *On the Record*, its April 2026 anniversary series, and its mechatronics recruiting page. The reviewed records identify one WCC hire. The shortlist size, other WCC hires, first-shift wage, who screened applicants for the July 2026 job and whom it hired remain unknown. The July posting shows an advertised need for fabrication work. It provides no placement count. [Original WCC profile](https://www.wccnet.edu/news/articles/2023-11-14-alumni-rudisill.php) · [March issue](https://www.wccnet.edu/news/index-otr.php?tags=March+2024) · [Anniversary profile](https://www.wccnet.edu/news/articles/2026-04-15-rudisill60.php) · [Recruiting page](https://catalog.wccnet.edu/current/programs/apmras.php) · [March 2020 reporting](https://tollbit.forbes.com/sites/samabuelsamid/2020/03/05/michigans-quantum-signal-on-the-hunt-for-more-engineers/)

## Watson Engineering — Taylor

**Company → site → work.** The posting names the shop at 16455 Racho Blvd., Taylor. Watson makes prototype and production sheet-metal and tubular parts. Its advertised capabilities include cutting and forming, manual and robotic welding, CNC machining, fixtures and tooling, coating, and assembly. Its Taylor directory identifies HR, machining, quality, materials/logistics and sales/product development. The welding hiring manager and final authority over headcount are unknown. [Taylor directory](https://www.watsoneng.com/taylor/) · [Fabrication work](https://www.watsoneng.com/metal-fabrication/) · [Fixtures and tooling](https://www.watsoneng.com/home-mosaic/fixtures-and-tooling/) · [Company careers](https://www.watsoneng.com/careers/)

**What the weld shop advertised.** Manual MIG at **$20–$23/hr**, manual TIG at **$23–$26/hr**, and production robot welding at **$18–$20/hr**. The ad ties starting pay to weld-test scores, adds $2/hr for afternoon and midnight shifts, and describes contract-to-hire contingent on attendance and performance. It lists a physical, drug screen and background check. It lists no college credential. The Indeed ad has expired. Archived copies give different minimum contract periods (30 versus 45 days). The rate after conversion, actual conversions, shift-level order volumes and which parts were assigned to new welders remain unknown. [Indeed ad](https://www.indeed.com/viewjob?jk=e0c2bb4366c6a81d) · [Career.com copy](https://www.career.com/job/watson-engineering/welders-needed-manual-mig-tig/j202610161421090932222)

## Heat Treat Equipment — Canton

**Company → site → work.** At 42056 Michigan Ave., Canton, the company sells used and refurbished heat-treatment equipment and offers installation/startup and rigging coordination. Its small fabrication shop advertised someone to build industrial furnaces and components, work independently, and operate basic shop machinery. The shop asks for a practical MIG test on steel and stainless steel and prefers some stick welding. The ad lists **$22–$28/hr** in the pay field and no degree requirement. An image on the same listing shows **$21–$26/hr**, so the actual offer rate needs verification. The company publishes a general contact. It does not identify who administers the test or sets pay. [Company and services](https://heattreatequip.com/about_us) · [Welder/Fabricator posting](https://www.ziprecruiter.com/c/heat-treat-equipment/Job/Welder-Fabricator/-in-Canton,MI?jid=34066b85af7f7118)

## Unnamed Dexter manufacturing shop

A staffing ad, now expired, put full-time production MIG at **$22–$24/hr**. The work included setting up gas shielding, fitting parts into weld fixtures, welding to print, and basic welder maintenance. It required at least one year of welding experience. The ad does not identify the employer or give a shop address. The pay and tasks belong in the local evidence. An employer attribution would require another source. [Dexter posting](https://www.indeed.com/viewjob?jk=34f886d7dad12be0)

## What the records establish

These wage figures are advertised rates for specified work. Actual starting pay and how long workers stay at those rates are unmeasured. For Quantum Signal, a public count of hires through WCC by year, with starting pay and whether WCC training was required, would establish whether Rudisill’s route recurs. The school’s multiple publications all point to the same documented hire.
