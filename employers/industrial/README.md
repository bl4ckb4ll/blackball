## Stryker

Stryker's current careers surface exposes much more than generic "medical device engineering."

Engineering / Technology / Innovation includes:

- electrical engineering;
- mechanical engineering;
- manufacturing engineering;
- process engineering;
- quality engineering;
- software engineering;
- engineering management;
- product management;
- Research & Development;
- Information Systems;
- cybersecurity / cyber risk / IT compliance;
- Project and Program Management;
- Digital, Robotics and Enabling Technologies (DRE).

Other current student/graduate material exposes:

- manufacturing;
- advanced operations;
- supply chain;
- finance;
- sales;
- marketing;
- clinical support;
- customer service;
- regulatory/quality functions depending on geography.

Sources:
- https://careers.stryker.com/engineering
- https://careers.stryker.com/students-and-graduates

US recent-graduate rotational programs explicitly include Engineering and Finance/Accounting.

### Next Stryker map

```text
Stryker
→ product/business division
→ R&D / manufacturing / quality / supply chain / sales / IS / DRE
→ site or plant
→ department manager
→ requisition
```

The division/site attachment matters because a Kalamazoo manufacturing engineering role and a software/robotics role are not one labor market merely because both say Stryker.

## Cummins

Cummins publishes explicit entry surfaces across:

- internships and co-ops;
- apprenticeships;
- entry-level roles;
- returnships;
- professional development programs;
- School-to-Work roles.

Current functional/career surfaces include:

- engineering;
- manufacturing;
- supply chain;
- systems / information technology;
- finance;
- service;
- sales and marketing;
- quality;
- production and technician work.

Sources:
- https://www.cummins.com/en-na/careers
- https://cummins.jobs/jobs/

Cummins explicitly describes apprentices training alongside technicians, and its professional development programs include Finance and Manufacturing rotations. Current job inventory includes production associates, test technicians, controls technicians, supply-chain roles, engineers, IT solution roles, and finance roles.
