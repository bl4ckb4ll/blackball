# Walmart corporate opportunity map — first pass, 2026-09-30

Walmart should not be represented as "retail jobs." Its corporate organization contains large finance, technology, merchandising, supply-chain, real-estate, data, product, audit, and operations labor markets.

Current corporate and program material exposes at least:

### Operating segments

- Walmart U.S.
- Sam's Club
- International
- Corporate / enterprise functions

## Explicit entry gates

### Accounting & Finance Development Program internship

The current Summer 2027 AFDP internship feeds a two-year rotational program.

The Finance & Strategy track can place participants in Commercial Finance supporting:

- Strategy;
- Operations;
- Merchandising;
- Supply Chain;
- Marketing;
- Real Estate;
- FP&A.

The Accounting track rotates through:

- Controllership;
- Audit.

This is an unusually useful public map of internal corporate functions.

### Merchandising internship

The current merchandising internship describes supporting functions including:

- Private Brands;
- Sourcing;
- Planning;
- Business Analytics;
- Replenishment.

## Technology is attached to operations

A current Supply Chain Technology Operations leadership posting explicitly connects:

- Supply Chain Operations;
- Fulfillment;
- Transportation;
- Product;
- Engineering;
- Data & AI;
- Enterprise Technology.

That relation is more useful than simply cataloging "software engineer at Walmart."
