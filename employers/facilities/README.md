# Facilities, real estate, and material-handling employers — first pass, 2026-09-30

These employers are easy to miss because much of their work occurs **inside somebody else's building**. The employer name may be less visible than the client site, even when the company controls hiring and payroll.

- Corporate;
- Digital & Technology;
- Building Operations & Management;
- Investment Management;

- Sales & Consulting;

### Critical structural point

CBRE often hires into a **service line + client account + site**. Therefore the useful authority graph is often:

```text
CBRE
→ GWS / Project Management / D&T / other service line
→ client account
→ regional/site organization
→ site leader / engineering manager
→ technician/engineer/project role
```

Current data-center Operating Engineer / Critical Facilities Engineer postings require roughly two years of critical-facility experience and work with UPS, generators, switchgear, HVAC/chillers, CRAC/CRAH, CMMS, vendor management, and emergency procedures.

This makes JLL another employer whose technical workers may be physically embedded at client facilities rather than at something visibly branded "JLL."

## Daifuku Services America / ELS recruiting surface

The current employer-hosted recruiting system remains at:

- https://careers-els-daifuku.icims.com/

Current postings identify the employer as Daifuku Services America Corporation / DSUS while the recruiting URL retains ELS-Daifuku naming. Preserve both names until the corporate history is separately documented.

A current **DSUS Jr. Technician** posting is a concrete low-barrier technical gate:

- high school diploma/GED;
- one year prior mechanical or baggage-handling/passenger-boarding-bridge experience **or ability to pass a mechanical aptitude test**;
- assists senior technicians with scheduled/unscheduled maintenance;
- basic mechanical/electrical adjustments and jam clearing;
- airport badge and driver's-license constraints;
- current postings in multiple airports.

Example:
- https://careers-els-daifuku.icims.com/jobs/21816/dsus-jr.-technician/job

The service surface includes baggage-handling systems, passenger boarding bridges, conveyors/material handling, mechanical/electrical maintenance, and higher controls/automation work.

### Why this group belongs in Black Ball

CBRE, JLL, and Daifuku/ELS make visible a class of middle-income technical/corporate work that disappears if research starts only from famous consumer brands. A hyperscale data center, airport, warehouse, or office campus can contain workers employed by several different companies with different ladders and hiring authorities.
