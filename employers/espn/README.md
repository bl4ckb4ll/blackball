# ESPN opportunity map — first pass, 2026-09-30

ESPN is a household-name brand with its own visible recruiting surface inside Disney, but some of its technology organization is shared with Disney's broader media portfolio.

## Brand-level work visible now

The current ESPN jobs page exposes categories including:

- Creative;
- Data Science and Analytics;
- Marketing and Digital Media;
- Operations;
- Production.

Recent/current inventory includes work such as:

- programming coordination;
- crewing and resource coordination;
- event production;
- remote camera, replay and video operation;
- encoder technician work;
- statistics analysis;
- design;
- media planning.

Source:
- https://jobs.disneycareers.com/espn-jobs

## Shared product and technology organization

Disney also recruits through **Disney Entertainment and ESPN Product & Technology**, a global media technology organization containing engineering, product, design, data and operations work. Current postings expose product software engineering, machine learning, data product management, media systems, reliability, delivery operations and distribution/platform work.

Sources:
- https://www.disneycareers.com/en/job/glendale/sr-mgr-product-software-engineering/391/99352768464
- https://jobs.disneycareers.com/search-jobs?acm=26715&orgIds=391-28648

Do not assume every role in that shared organization is ESPN-specific. Attach requisitions to ESPN only when the posting or team description supports the edge.

## Why ESPN is useful to map separately

A brand-level view exposes labor markets mostly hidden by the word "technology":

- programming and scheduling;
- statistics/data;
- product/software;
- advertising/media planning;
- creative/design.
