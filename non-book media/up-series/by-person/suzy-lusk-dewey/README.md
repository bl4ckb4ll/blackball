# Suzanne “Suzy” Lusk / Dewey

Participation through *63 Up*: `7 new · 14 new · 21 new · 28 new · 35 new · 42 new · 49 new · 56 new · 63 archive`

Suzy comes from a wealthy background and is one of the four girls in the continuing fourteen. Her parents' divorce and her own skepticism toward the series become recurring parts of her story.

At twenty-one she is doubtful about marriage; by twenty-eight she is married with children. She repeatedly expresses discomfort with being turned into a seven-yearly public object. She appears through *56 Up* but declines a new interview for *63 Up*, appearing only in archival material.

Her trajectory is a useful warning that statements at seven, fourteen, or twenty-one are not destiny and that economic security does not eliminate family disruption or resistance to publicity.
