# John Brisby

Participation through *63 Up*: `7 new · 14 new · 21 new · 28 archive · 35 new · 42 archive · 49 new · 56 new · 63 new`

John is one of the three Kensington boys. His childhood confidence and knowledge of elite institutions make him one of the original class thesis's strongest cases. He later studies at Oxford and becomes a barrister.

John periodically refuses participation, notably at twenty-eight and forty-two, then returns. He also challenges the simplification of his childhood as effortless privilege; later accounts note his father's early death and scholarship/support details that complicate the visual shorthand of "posh school equals uncomplicated life."
