# Charles Furneaux

Participation through *63 Up*: `7 new · 14 new · 21 new · 28 absent · 35 archive · 42 archive · 49 archive · 56 archive · 63 archive`

Charles is one of the three Kensington boys. He attends elite schools, goes to Durham University, later undertakes postgraduate study at Oxford, and becomes a television journalist, producer, and documentary filmmaker.

After *21 Up*, his most important role in the series is absence. He stops giving new interviews and becomes critical of continued archival reuse. His case therefore raises the strongest internal question about ownership of a longitudinal persona: once childhood footage exists, how much control does the adult retain over repeated reuse?

Pre-broadcast reporting about *70 Up* must not be converted into completed participation before transmission.
