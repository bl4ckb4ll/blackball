# Bruce Balden

Participation through *63 Up*: `7 new · 14 new · 21 new · 28 new · 35 new · 42 new · 49 new · 56 new · 63 new`

Bruce begins at boarding/preparatory school and is remembered for missing his father, who was serving abroad in Rhodesia. As a child he imagines a life of service, including missionary aspirations.

He studies mathematics at Oxford, teaches, works for a period in Bangladesh, then teaches in a London comprehensive and later at an independent school in St Albans. He marries and has sons.

Bruce complicates a crude class reading: his education demonstrates substantial institutional advantage, while his adult choices repeatedly orient toward teaching and service rather than high-paying finance or law. His friendship with Neil also makes him part of another participant's causal story.
