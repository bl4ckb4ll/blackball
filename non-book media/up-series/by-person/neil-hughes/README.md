# Neil Hughes

Participation through *63 Up*: `7 new · 14 new · 21 new · 28 new · 35 new · 42 new · 49 new · 56 new · 63 new`

Neil begins as one of the most cheerful and imaginative seven-year-olds, saying he wants to become an astronaut. He attends the University of Aberdeen but leaves, works various jobs, and by *28 Up* is living precariously and at times homeless in Scotland.

Later films document continued mental-health struggles, writing/performance interests, movement around Britain, and eventually a more stable civic life. By *42 Up* he has completed an Open University degree and serves as a Liberal Democrat councillor in Hackney; later he is involved in local politics in Cumbria and lay religious work.

No single installment should be treated as the definitive Neil. The archive's emotional power comes partly from repeatedly cutting adult distress against the exuberant child.
