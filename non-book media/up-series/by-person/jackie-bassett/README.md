# Jackie Bassett

Participation through *63 Up*: `7 new · 14 new · 21 new · 28 new · 35 new · 42 new · 49 new · 56 new · 63 new`

Jackie is introduced with Lynn and Sue as an East End girl. Across the series her life includes marriage, divorce, three sons, multiple jobs, moves, illness, and disputes over disability-related support.

One of her most important contributions is methodological: she challenges Apted for repeatedly asking the women narrow questions about marriage and domestic life while giving men more room to discuss work, politics, and ideas. By *56 Up* and *63 Up*, her health, benefits, family losses, and resilience bring the welfare state directly into the longitudinal story.

ITV/Apple identify Jackie as a central subject in *63 Up* part 2.
