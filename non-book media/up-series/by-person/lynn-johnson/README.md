# Lynn Johnson

Participation through *63 Up*: `7 new · 14 new · 21 new · 28 new · 35 new · 42 new · 49 new · 56 new · 63 tribute`

Lynn is introduced with Jackie and Sue in the East End. Her adult life becomes strongly associated with children's education and libraries; she works for decades as a children's/school librarian.

Later films show austerity entering an individual biography as cuts and changes to library services affect her work. She marries Russ, has children and grandchildren, and remains closely connected to the other East End women.

Lynn develops a serious brain condition and dies in May 2013, after *56 Up* and before *63 Up*. She is the first of the tracked fourteen to die; *63 Up* includes a tribute and family material.
