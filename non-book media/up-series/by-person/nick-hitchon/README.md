# Nicholas “Nick” Hitchon

Participation through *63 Up*: `7 new · 14 new · 21 new · 28 new · 35 new · 42 new · 49 new · 56 new · 63 new`

Nick grows up in a Yorkshire Dales farming family and is the cohort's rural child. At seven he wants to learn about the Moon; as a teenager his interest in science is already more concrete.

He studies physics at Oxford, earns a doctorate, moves to the United States in 1982, and spends roughly four decades at the University of Wisconsin–Madison working on plasma/nuclear-fusion-related engineering, becoming a professor and department chair.

By *63 Up* he is seriously ill with cancer. He retires from Wisconsin in 2022 and dies on 23 July 2023 at age 65, so any *70 Up* appearance can only be archival.

Obituary by his brother: https://www.theguardian.com/environment/2023/aug/27/nick-hitchon-obituary
