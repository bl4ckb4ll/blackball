# Symon Basterfield

Participation through *63 Up*: `7 new · 14 new · 21 new · 28 new · 35 absent · 42 new · 49 new · 56 new · 63 new`

Symon is the only mixed-race child among the continuing fourteen and begins in the same children's home as Paul. Public accounts describe a difficult family background, including his mother's depression and lack of a relationship with his father.

He marries young, has five children, divorces, later remarries, and becomes involved in fostering. His work includes freight/industrial employment. His later foster parenting gives the care-home origin a striking longitudinal echo without requiring a claim that age seven predicted it.

The reunion with Paul in *63 Up* is one of the series' strongest comparisons between shared childhood institution and divergent adult geography.
