# Susan “Sue” Davis / Sullivan

Participation through *63 Up*: `7 new · 14 new · 21 new · 28 new · 35 new · 42 new · 49 new · 56 new · 63 new`

Sue is the third of the East End schoolfriends Jackie, Lynn, and Sue. She later works in university administration. She marries young, has children, divorces by the period around *35 Up*, and later forms a long-term relationship.

Her biography is useful against two simplifications: a working-class childhood does not imply one occupational outcome, and social mobility does not require becoming culturally unrecognizable from childhood.

In a 2019 Radio Times interview she says she is proud of the record the series made while also describing the fear of saying something irreversible because subjects do not control the final edit.
