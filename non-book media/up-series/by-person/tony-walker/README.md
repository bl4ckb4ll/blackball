# Tony Walker

Participation through *63 Up*: `7 new · 14 new · 21 new · 28 new · 35 new · 42 new · 49 new · 56 new · 63 new`

At seven Tony is the energetic East End boy who wants to be a jockey. He races professionally at a small scale but does not establish a long-term racing career, and becomes a London taxi driver.

Tony is one of the series' clearest examples of a childhood desire surviving without producing the predicted occupation. Horses remain important while adult livelihood becomes taxi work. Later films include property, family life, acting-extra work, Spain, grandchildren, marital difficulty, changing East End demographics, and controversy over remarks in *56 Up*.
