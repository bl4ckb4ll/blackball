# Paul Kligerman

Participation through *63 Up*: `7 new · 14 new · 21 new · 28 new · 35 new · 42 new · 49 new · 56 new · 63 new`

Paul is first seen in a children's home with Symon. Soon after the first film his family emigrates to Australia, so the series follows a life that leaves Britain geographically while remaining tied to the original cohort.

His adult work includes manual trades such as bricklaying and later practical/maintenance work. He marries and has children. His relationship with Symon becomes especially important in *63 Up*, whose third part centers an emotional reunion in Australia between the two men who had been filmed together in care at seven.
