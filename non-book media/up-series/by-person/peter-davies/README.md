# Peter Davies

Participation through *63 Up*: `7 new · 14 new · 21 new · 28 new · 35 absent · 42 absent · 49 absent · 56 new · 63 new`

Peter is introduced in a Liverpool suburban school alongside Neil. His adult route includes university, teaching, later legal work, music, marriage, and children.

His most important relation to the project is his long withdrawal. In *28 Up* he criticizes the Thatcher government; hostile tabloid coverage follows, and he declines *35 Up*, *42 Up*, and *49 Up*. He returns in *56 Up* after roughly twenty-eight years away.

This is a strong internal example of selection effects: if a participant disappears after a politically costly appearance, the later series is not a neutral panel. His return also shows that participation is negotiated and can serve participants' own purposes.
