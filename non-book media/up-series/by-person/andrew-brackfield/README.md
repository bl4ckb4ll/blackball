# Andrew Brackfield

Participation through *63 Up*: `7 new · 14 new · 21 new · 28 new · 35 new · 42 new · 49 new · 56 new · 63 new`

Andrew is one of the three boys chosen from a wealthy Kensington pre-preparatory school. At seven the trio can already name a sequence of elite schools and universities, making them the original programme's clearest illustration of inherited institutional knowledge.

His trajectory broadly follows that expectation: Cambridge (Trinity College), then a legal career as a solicitor, marriage, and children. He is the only one of the three Kensington boys who participates actively in every installment through *63 Up*.

The useful interpretation is not supernatural childhood foresight. The family/school system had already made an elite educational route predictable enough for a child to recite it. Later installments also make him useful for separating structural advantage from claims about personal vice or virtue.
