# Age 28 — *28 Up* — 1984

- Director: Michael Apted
- Original air dates: 20–21 November 1984
- Approximate total length: 136 minutes
- Charles declines active participation; John is represented through archive rather than a new interview.

Marriage, children, career, unemployment, geographic movement, and mental health have made the biographies too complex to fit the original binary class scheme cleanly. The series becomes recognizably about lives rather than predictions.

Neil's precarious living situation and periods of homelessness are among the most consequential developments in the project. Peter's criticism of Thatcher-era Britain later generated hostile tabloid attention and contributed to his long withdrawal. The installment therefore supplies evidence both about life-course instability and about the reputational costs of documentary participation.
