# Age 14 — *7 Plus Seven* / *14 Up* — 1970

- Director: Michael Apted
- Original air date: 15 December 1970
- Approximate length: 53 minutes

At fourteen the children can compare their seven-year-old answers with adolescence. School sorting is more visible: some are already moving along the educational paths predicted at seven, while others are closer to leaving school and work.

This installment establishes a central device of the series: the archive argues with the present. Apted can replay a childhood prediction and ask what happened to it. That gives unusually strong longitudinal evidence but also considerable rhetorical power to the editor, who chooses which old statement the present-day participant must confront.

The subjects are also beginning to understand that recorded remarks can embarrass them later. From this point onward the project is studying people who know that earlier selves can be replayed to them.
