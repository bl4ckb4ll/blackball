# Age 56 — *56 Up* — 2012

- Director: Michael Apted
- Original air dates: 14, 21, 28 May 2012
- Approximate length: 144 minutes
- Peter returns after a long absence.

Retirement planning, grandchildren, illness, austerity, redundancies, and disputes about the original class premise become central. Lynn's experience with cuts affecting her library career and Jackie's confrontation over disability and benefit policy put institutions directly into the biographies.

Peter's return after decades away is evidence of participant agency: the project cannot simply summon subjects on schedule; participation has costs and is negotiated.
