# Age 7 — *Seven Up!* — 1964

- Director: Paul Almond
- Broadcaster: ITV / Granada, *World in Action*
- Original air date: 5 May 1964
- Approximate length: 40 minutes

The film begins as a deliberate class polemic. Children from sharply different social settings are asked about school, money, work, marriage, race, religion, leisure, and their future. The privileged Kensington boys can already name educational institutions far into the future; working-class children speak from a different horizon of expectation.

The strongest evidence is institutional rather than prophetic: family and school systems have already made some pathways ordinary knowledge at age seven. The London Zoo/playground material also stages class contact physically by bringing children from normally separate institutions together for the camera.

Methodological limit: the children were selected to illustrate social differences. This is not a random sample and should not be used to infer numerical mobility rates.
