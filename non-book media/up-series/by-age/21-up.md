# Age 21 — *21 Up* — 1977

- Director: Michael Apted
- Original air date: 9 May 1977
- Approximate length: 100 minutes
- All fourteen continuing subjects appear.

At twenty-one, childhood class forecasts can be compared with university, training, jobs, relationships, and emerging adult identity. Some privileged predictions look almost mechanically accurate; other lives are already diverging from what the seven-year-old imagined.

The useful interpretation is that class supplies probabilities, resources, and institutional routes rather than a complete script. Education, confidence, family help, and networks matter, but so do temperament, relationships, health, and decisions.

This is Charles Furneaux's last active installment before his long withdrawal, making continuing consent an explicit methodological issue.
