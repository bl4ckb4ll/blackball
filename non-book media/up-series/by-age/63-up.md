# Age 63 — *63 Up* — 2019

- Director: Michael Apted
- Original air dates: 4, 5, 6 June 2019
- Approximate length: 150 minutes
- Lynn had died in 2013 and is represented through tribute/family material.
- Suzy does not give a new interview.
- Charles remains archive-only.

Mortality becomes explicit. Nick is living with cancer and reflects on death. Retirement and aging move from distant future to immediate circumstance.

ITV's episode descriptions identify Tony and Nick in part 1; Bruce, Jackie, and Lynn's story in part 2; and Paul and Symon's reunion in Australia plus Neil's later life in part 3. The Paul/Symon reunion is especially useful for comparing two people who began in the same children's home and then lived on different continents.
