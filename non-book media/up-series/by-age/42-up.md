# Age 42 — *42 Up* — 1998

- Director: Michael Apted
- Original air dates: 21–22 July 1998
- Broadcaster: BBC One
- Approximate length: 133 minutes
- John declines a new interview; Charles remains absent.

By forty-two there is enough historical footage to show how misleading any single installment can be. A person who looked permanently lost at twenty-eight may be more stable; a stable marriage may have ended; a career may have changed.

Neil has completed Open University study and is involved in local politics; Bruce at one point gives him a place to stay. That friendship matters because the project itself has created relationships across class divisions it originally set out to observe.
