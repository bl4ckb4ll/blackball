# Age 49 — *49 Up* — 2005

- Director: Michael Apted
- Original air dates: 15 and 22 September 2005
- Approximate length: 134 minutes
- John returns; Peter and Charles remain absent.

At forty-nine participants are openly capable of analyzing the series that analyzes them. They criticize Apted's questions, editing, and tendency to fit them back into the original class thesis. This reflexivity is one of the most valuable parts of the later project.

Career and class remain present, but the film gives increasing weight to marriage, children, prejudice, disappointment, and the meaning of ordinary stability.
