# Age 35 — *35 Up* — 1991

- Director: Michael Apted
- Original air date: 22 May 1991
- Approximate length: 116 minutes
- Charles remains out; Symon and Peter do not take part in new filming.

At thirty-five the project is increasingly about family formation, divorce, career consolidation, parenthood, and the participants' relationship to their own childhoods. Attrition matters: absence may reflect dislike of the project, publicity costs, competing obligations, or a wish not to let Apted define another seven-year chapter.

The installment also sharpens a recurring gender problem. The original design gave much more space to boys, and women's lives were often forced into repetitive questions about marriage, children, and domestic happiness. Jackie later challenges Apted directly about this pattern.
