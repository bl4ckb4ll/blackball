# Rights and mirroring decisions

Checked: 2026-08-31.

## *Born Rich* (2003)

**Decision: do not mirror the film.**

Apple's current U.S. catalog page identifies the film as © 2003 Wise and Good Film, LLC. That is affirmative copyright information, not an orphan-work situation.

Sources:

- Apple TV: https://tv.apple.com/us/movie/born-rich/umc.cmc.7e0x2cpkhg3hwh9y1gst7qxzv
- Tubi: https://tubitv.com/movies/503586/born-rich
- Current U.S. availability aggregator: https://www.justwatch.com/us/movie/born-rich

As of late August 2026, lawful U.S. routes include Tubi (free with ads), Hoopla where a participating library offers it, and Apple TV / Amazon Video purchase or rental routes. Availability changes, so the JustWatch record is retained as a dated discovery aid rather than treated as permanent.

## The *Up* series

**Decision: do not mirror any installment.**

There is no verified public-domain basis for *Seven Up!* (1964) or any sequel. Age alone is not enough. ITV actively distributes the collection, and Apple identifies *63 Up* as © ITV Studios Limited 2019. ITV's own Press Centre terms state that its programme material is protected by copyright and prohibit unauthorized duplication.

Sources:

- Official ITVX collection: https://www.itv.com/watch/the-7-up-collection/3a0317
- Apple TV UK *63 Up* / collection page: https://tv.apple.com/gb/show/63-up/umc.cmc.3e4h1qtxu3ieitfpc9lqv50m6
- ITV rights/Press Centre terms: https://www.itv.com/presscentre/terms-service
- Current U.S. availability: https://www.justwatch.com/us/tv-show/the-up-series

Library catalog records that say a particular DVD includes public-performance rights do **not** mean the underlying film is public domain or may be uploaded to GitHub.

## What would change the decision

Before mirroring a future copy, preserve all of the following:

1. the source URL and source owner;
2. the exact license or public-domain statement;
3. the date checked;
4. a local text/PDF snapshot of the rights statement when legally reproducible;
5. checksum and provenance for the mirrored file;
6. a note explaining why the license covers redistribution, not merely viewing or public performance.

Until then, use lawful external viewing links and do not ingest video bytes into the repository.
