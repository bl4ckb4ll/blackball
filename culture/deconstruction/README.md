# Deconstruction

This subject area treats arguments as arguments, with their sources, counterarguments, and historical applications kept visible.

## Source reviews

- [Gayatri Chakravorty Spivak — “The Post-modern Condition: The End of Politics?” (1984)](gayatri-chakravorty-spivak-1984.md) — her account of narrative exclusion, the need to narrate despite limits, and the objections raised by Ron Aronson, John Dunn, and Geoffrey Hawthorn.

## Research question

Does attending to what a political or historical narrative omits produce a more accurate or useful account? Compare concrete examples using evidence, causal claims, and the best available alternatives. Do not treat “incomplete” as synonymous with “false,” or “criticized” as synonymous with “refuted.”
