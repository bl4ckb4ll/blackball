# Apple employment map

Captured: 2026-09-30.

Primary source:
- https://jobs.apple.com/en-us/search

## Public technical surface

Apple's careers search exposes distinct work across:

- Software and Services, including operating systems;
- machine learning and AI;
- hardware engineering;
- sensing, imaging and camera systems;
- silicon and low-level systems;
- operations and supply chain;
- information systems and technology.

Current search pages show hundreds of postings in operating systems and machine-learning/AI categories. These counts are dated search-result counts, not headcount or hires.

## Entry gates

Apple has a Students category with internships. Current US-facing results include:

- Software Undergrad Engineering Internships;
- Machine Learning and Artificial Intelligence Undergrad Internships;
- other technical internships across information systems and hardware-related areas.

The student gate must be kept separate from ordinary experienced-hire demand.

## Why this case matters

Apple is a useful bridge between software, electrical engineering, computer engineering, mechanical/product engineering, silicon, sensors, manufacturing and operations.

The catalog should therefore classify work by what the posting describes rather than by a single "software" label.

## Next measurements

1. Freeze current US student, operating-system, ML/AI, hardware and operations requisitions.
2. Separate internships from new-graduate and experienced-hire roles.
3. Record whether mechanical, electrical or computer-engineering degrees are named and whether equivalent experience is accepted.
4. Identify technician, lab, validation, manufacturing and operations roles that may have different entry gates from design engineering.
5. Track geographic concentration by team.
