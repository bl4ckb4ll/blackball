## Entry gates

Apple has a Students category with internships. Current US-facing results include software, ML/AI, information-systems and hardware-related internships.

The student gate must be kept separate from ordinary experienced-hire demand. A separate census is still needed for genuine new-graduate, technician, lab, validation, manufacturing and support entry routes.

## Why this case matters

Apple is not one software labor market. Its published team map connects software, electrical engineering, computer engineering, mechanical/product engineering, silicon, sensors, validation, manufacturing, supply chain, finance, marketing, retail and support.

For Black Ball, this is closer to the desired unit:

```text
Apple
→ public team / specialty
→ narrower group named by requisition
→ manager or budget authority where supported
→ work and entry gate
```
