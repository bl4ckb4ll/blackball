## What to measure next

The next useful unit is not another employer description. It is an **entry-gate census** across these employers.

For every current technical requisition, classify:

```text
employer
job_id
title
job_family
level
entry_gate = internship | new_graduate | early_career | residency | technician | experienced_hire | unknown
school_enrollment_required
degree_required
equivalent_experience_allowed
years_experience_min
independently_buildable_evidence
location
work_arrangement
first_seen
last_seen
source_url
```

That will let Blackball answer questions such as:

- Which employers actually have non-experienced technical openings this month?
- Which gates require current student status?
- Which employers explicitly accept equivalent practical experience?
- Which technical areas have technician/operations routes rather than only degree-gated engineering routes?
- Which "entry level" postings quietly require prior professional experience?
- How quickly do the gates open and close?
