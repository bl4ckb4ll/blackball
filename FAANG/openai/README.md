# OpenAI employment map

Captured: 2026-09-30.

Primary sources:
- https://openai.com/careers/
- https://openai.com/careers/emerging-talent/
- https://openai.com/residency/

## Public work surface

Current OpenAI postings span:

- research engineering and research science;
- applied engineering and product;
- distributed systems and platform infrastructure;
- data infrastructure;
- compute infrastructure;
- security;
- data science;
- data-center, hardware, manufacturing, supply-chain, power/cooling and operations work.

The compute surface is particularly useful because OpenAI explicitly describes work across software, hardware, facilities, operations and engineering disciplines rather than treating AI employment as only model research.

## Explicit entry gates

OpenAI's Emerging Talent page states that it offers full-time roles in research, applied engineering and product for people with **0–3 years of experience**, and explicitly includes recent graduates and self-taught learners.

The six-month OpenAI Residency is a separate pathway for researchers and engineers from AI or closely related quantitative/scientific fields. The 2026 Residency is currently closed.

These are direct employer claims about entry routes. They are not evidence of acceptance rates.

## Geography

Many technical roles remain centered in San Francisco. Current infrastructure roles also name New York City, Seattle, Mountain View, London and some US-remote arrangements. Exact location must be attached at the requisition level.
