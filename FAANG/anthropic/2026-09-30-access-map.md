# Anthropic worker-access map — 2026-09-30

This note applies the Black Ball rule that prestige is not actionable opportunity information. It records current, concrete gates into Anthropic, the evidence a worker is asked to produce, the cost of testing the route, and what is still unknown.

Sources are dated where the underlying posting supplies a date. A live posting is evidence of advertised demand, not evidence that an applicant will be hired.

## Three explicit entry gates

### 1. Anthropic Fellows Program

Primary source:
- https://job-boards.greenhouse.io/anthropic/jobs/5183051008

Status on 2026-09-30: accepting applications on a rolling basis for a cohort expected to start in January 2027.

Anthropic describes the program as funding and mentoring promising technical talent "regardless of previous experience." The current ML Systems & Reinforcement Learning posting says:

- four months, full-time;
- direct mentorship from Anthropic researchers;
- public empirical output is the goal, such as a paper submission;
- US stipend: $3,850/week, plus benefits;
- compute funding: about $15,000/month;
- remote fellows are allowed in the US, UK, or Canada;
- participants must already have work authorization in one of those countries;
- Anthropic does not currently sponsor visas for Fellows;
- fluent Python is required;
- the employer reports that 25–50% of Fellows in previous cohorts received a full-time Anthropic offer, while explicitly stating that no offer is guaranteed.

Treat the 25–50% conversion figure as an employer-reported historical outcome, not an independently verified rate and not a promise for the January 2027 cohort.

This is a materially different object from generic advice to "do AI research." It has a current application, a defined duration, pay, compute, mentors, an output target, an interview process, and a stated historical conversion range.

The cost is also visible: four months of full-time work. Even though it is paid, it is not a nights-and-weekends program that can simply be layered onto an ordinary full-time job.

### 2. Associate Applied AI, Rotational Program — London

Primary source:
- https://job-boards.greenhouse.io/anthropic/jobs/5425724008

Posted: 2026-09-16.

This is an explicit early-career role rather than a senior posting with vague "equivalent experience" language.

The posting says:

- 0–2 years of software engineering, technical consulting, or solutions-architecture experience;
- 0–2 years of customer-facing technical experience such as sales engineering, technical support, consulting, or teaching;
- six-month rotation through Applied AI segments and functions;
- hands-on work building agents, Skills, demos, integrations, and customer solutions using Claude's SDK and API;
- intended transition possibilities include Solutions Architecture, Product Engineering, or other Applied AI functions;
- bachelor's degree or equivalent education/training/experience;
- £115,000 annual salary;
- London;
- rolling applications with no stated deadline.

This is a particularly useful Black Ball gate because the employer names the evidence it wants: functional applications, tools, APIs/SDKs, integration work, troubleshooting, technical communication, and customer-facing technical experience.

The geography is a real constraint. The posting is London-based and Anthropic's ordinary hybrid rule applies, though the company says it sponsors visas for eligible full-time roles and will make reasonable efforts after an offer.

### 3. Research Engineer, Takeoff Intel

Primary source:
- https://job-boards.greenhouse.io/anthropic/jobs/5416882008

Posted: 2026-09-08.

Takeoff Intel is part of the Anthropic Institute. Its posting explicitly says:

> We're hiring at both junior and senior levels.

The team measures AI R&D capability growth and internal AI-assisted development. The posting asks for evidence such as:

- shipping an evaluation, data product, or research library end to end;
- running experiments on large language models;
- handling large, messy data;
- rapid prototyping;
- building evaluation harnesses or benchmark infrastructure;
- building tools or libraries used by researchers;
- identifying failures in AI-written code.

Location is listed as Remote-Friendly (Travel Required) or San Francisco. The posting uses Anthropic's bachelor's-or-equivalent wording.

The advertised compensation range is $350,000–$850,000, but the same requisition spans junior and senior levels. Do not infer a junior-specific salary from the full range.

This is a concrete test of what "junior" means at a frontier lab: the gate is junior relative to Anthropic's internal ladder, but the evidence requested is still substantial.

## No internship route

Anthropic's current careers FAQ says:

> We don't currently offer internships.

Source:
- https://www.anthropic.com/careers

That negative fact matters. A college student or career changer should not silently assume that the standard large-technology-company internship ladder exists here.

Anthropic does permit re-application after 12 months, or sooner if something materially changes about the applicant's experience or skills. It says it does not provide feedback on resumes or interviews. Those two facts make independent feedback loops especially important.

## Concrete evidence loops

The current postings expose work products that can be checked before someone spends years accumulating another credential.

| Gate | Employer-visible evidence named in the source | Fastest falsifiable question |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fellows | empirical project; Python; public research output; ML systems/RL work depending on stream | Can the applicant produce a serious empirical project and pass the technical/research selection process? |
| Applied AI rotation | functional applications/tools; API and SDK integrations; agents; troubleshooting; technical/customer communication | Can the applicant build and explain a working customer-shaped Claude/API system? |
| Takeoff Intel | eval harness/data product/research library; LLM experiments; analysis tooling; rapid prototypes | Can the applicant ship an evaluation/measurement artifact that a research team could actually use? |

This does not establish that any one artifact is sufficient for hiring. It does replace the much weaker instruction to acquire prestige and hope.

## A useful counterexample: mentors are not automatically hiring contacts

The Fellows posting names potential mentors, then explicitly tells applicants that all applications must go through the official form and **not** through the mentors.

That is exactly the distinction this project needs:

```text
important person in the work
!=
correct hiring channel
!=
person with authority over every hiring decision
```

The map should record those relations separately.

## Worker cost and uncertainty

Current Anthropic access is not "easy" merely because explicit gates exist.

- Fellows requires four months full-time.
- The London rotation has a geographic and hybrid-work constraint.
- Takeoff Intel calls some hiring junior but still asks for unusually strong shipped technical evidence.
- Anthropic currently has no internship program.
- Anthropic says it does not provide rejected applicants with application/interview feedback.
- Requisition existence does not reveal applicant counts, interview-pass rates, offer rates, or whether equivalent-experience language is honored in practice.

The value of the map is not to turn those unknowns into optimism. It is to expose them before a worker sinks years into a route.
