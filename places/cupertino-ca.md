# Cupertino, California

## Why this place is here

Cupertino is a particularly strong comparison case because extraordinary household income is paired with high-performing public schools, Silicon Valley employment, intensive college preparation, and proximity to Stanford and several major public universities.

## Public economic anchor

U.S. Census QuickFacts reports for 2020-2024:
- median household income: **$234,707**;
- per-capita income: **$110,519**;
- poverty: **4.1%**.

Source:
- https://www.census.gov/quickfacts/fact/table/cupertinocitycalifornia

Census Reporter provides the same ACS frame:
- https://censusreporter.org/profiles/16000US0617610-cupertino-ca/

This places Cupertino well above national and California household-income levels, but the dossier still needs distributions rather than only a median.

## Public-school / college pipeline

The City of Cupertino highlights both local public schools and proximity to Stanford, UC Berkeley, UC Santa Cruz, Santa Clara University, and San Jose State.
- https://www.cupertino.gov/Our-Community/Education/Schools

Monta Vista High School describes itself as a nationally recognized comprehensive high school in the heart of Silicon Valley, with a dedicated College and Career Advisor alongside school counselors.
- https://mvhs.fuhsd.org/about-us/accountability/school-profile

Cupertino High School publishes annual school profiles containing:
- college choices for graduates;
- college-admission test scores;
- demographics;
- curriculum information.

Source:
- https://chs.fuhsd.org/about-us/accountability/school-profile

These are strong primary institutional sources for reconstructing the local college pipeline without relying on rankings sites.

## Questions to investigate

- College destinations from Monta Vista, Cupertino High, Homestead, and private schools.
- College admissions representatives visiting each campus.
- Academic course loads, AP/honors enrollment, tutoring, Olympiad/research programs, and college consulting.
- Summer research, coding, internship, music, sports, and international-travel programs.
- Parent occupations by tract and links to major technology employers.
- Housing prices and the effect of school attendance boundaries on residential markets.
- Childcare, nanny, after-school, and domestic-service markets.
- Student stress, sleep, mental-health, and achievement-pressure evidence from defensible surveys rather than anecdotes.
- Counterevidence concerning economic diversity, immigrant family trajectories, public-school access, and community-college pathways.

## Evidence boundary

Cupertino's extraordinary median income and academic infrastructure are public facts. They do not establish that every family pursues elite admissions, that every child is overscheduled, or that parental affection is conditional on achievement.

Those are separate empirical questions.
