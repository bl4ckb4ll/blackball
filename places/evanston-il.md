# Evanston, Illinois

## Why this place is here

Evanston belongs in the comparison because it combines affluent neighborhoods, Northwestern University, a highly developed college-oriented school infrastructure, and substantial racial and economic diversity.

It should **not** be treated as a homogeneous wealthy enclave.

## Public economic anchor

Census Reporter, using ACS 2024 1-year data, reports approximately:
- population: **75,994**;
- median household income: **$93,016**;
- per-capita income: **$65,357**.

Source:
- https://censusreporter.org/profiles/16000US1724582-evanston-il/

U.S. Census QuickFacts similarly reports 2020-2024 median household income around $96,434 and poverty around 11.8%.
- https://www.census.gov/quickfacts/fact/table/evanstoncityillinois/HSD410224

The combination of high per-capita income and nontrivial poverty is itself an important warning against summary by median alone.

## University and school infrastructure

The City of Evanston describes Northwestern University as a central educational institution and lists a large set of public and private schools.
- https://www.cityofevanston.org/explore/education.php

Evanston Township High School says it serves more than 3,600 students and reports **63 languages** spoken by students. It also says 74% of students participate in extracurricular activities.
- https://www.eths202.org/about/about-eths

Its college-application infrastructure includes:
- dedicated College & Career Services;
- counselor recommendations;
- school profiles automatically sent with applications;
- transcript management;
- Common App / Coalition / SRAR guidance;
- financial-aid and application timelines.

Source:
- https://www.eths202.org/beyond-eths/college-applications

This is useful evidence of a mature college-application bureaucracy, while the school's diversity makes it a poor candidate for assuming one common family culture.

## Questions to investigate

- Separate affluent lakefront / university-professional neighborhoods from city-wide averages.
- College representative visits to ETHS and private schools.
- College destinations by income, race, course track, or school where public.
- Northwestern employee households and university-linked local networks.
- Tutoring, private counseling, summer programs, sports, arts, and enrichment markets.
- Property taxes and school funding.
- Domestic and care labor markets and commuting.
- How local inequality appears across neighborhoods that share one city and high school.

## Evidence boundary

Evanston is valuable precisely because it frustrates the easy category. It contains wealthy professional households and elite-university infrastructure, but also substantial poverty and a highly diverse public-school population.
