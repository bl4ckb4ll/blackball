# Skaneateles, New York

## Why this place is here

Skaneateles is useful as a small-town / lakefront comparison with large affluent suburbs. It should be measured at both the **village** and **town** level because the two units differ substantially.

## Public economic anchors

Census Reporter, using ACS 2024 5-year data, gives the **village** approximately:
- population: **2,543**;
- median household income: **$125,625**;
- per-capita income: **$98,872**.

Source:
- https://censusreporter.org/profiles/16000US3667510-skaneateles-ny/

For the larger **town**, the U.S. Census Bureau reports 2020-2024:
- median household income: **$103,545**;
- per-capita income: **$74,811**.

Source:
- https://www.census.gov/quickfacts/fact/table/skaneatelestownonondagacountynewyork/PST045223

The difference is exactly why the geographic unit needs to remain explicit.

## Research directions

- Map lakefront versus village versus remainder-of-town property values.
- Document school-district college counseling, admissions visits, AP/advanced coursework, sports, arts, and student travel.
- Identify private-school use outside the district, including Syracuse-area schools.
- Document second-home and seasonal-residence patterns where public data permit.
- Compare local service wages, housing costs, and commuting with lakefront wealth.
- Inventory yacht/boating, club, philanthropy, summer-camp, arts, and tourism institutions without treating participation as universal.
- Look for local alumni / notable-person self-presentation only when it adds evidence about institutional networks rather than celebrity trivia.

## Evidence boundary

Skaneateles should not be summarized merely as "a rich lake town." The village and town differ; older residents are unusually prominent in the population; and tourism / seasonal wealth may not be captured by resident household-income measures alone.

The dossier should make those distinctions visible rather than erase them.
