# Ken Burns

## Empire connection

Filmmaker Ken Burns is interviewed by Anita Anand in the July 2026 American Revolution / U.S. empire material. The published episode asks about Native land, George Washington, the Civil War, Vietnam and the transformation of the United States into a global power.

Episode catalogue: https://podcastaddict.com/empire-world-history/episode/227970251

## Work

Burns's main bibliography is a **filmography**, not a conventional sole-authored-book corpus. Major documentary projects include *The Civil War*, *Baseball*, *The War*, *The Vietnam War* (with Lynn Novick), *The U.S. and the Holocaust* and *The American Revolution*.

Official site: https://kenburns.com/

## Blackball use

Burns is useful as a source on mass historical memory: what archival images, narrators, experts and individual stories are selected to construct a national narrative. The documentaries should be treated as secondary historical works with their own source choices, not as neutral containers of footage.
