# Raja Shehadeh

## Empire connection

Palestinian lawyer and writer Raja Shehadeh appears in Empire bonus material discussing everyday life under occupation.

## Selected books

- *Where the Line Is Drawn*.
- *We Could Have Been Friends, My Father and I*.
- *Occupation Diaries*.

Author site: https://www.rajashehadeh.com/

## Blackball use

Shehadeh is useful for connecting legal/administrative structures to ordinary movement through space: permits, roads, land access, courts, settlement, property and physical barriers. Treat memoir and legal observation as qualitative evidence and separately collect systematic data on the institutions described.
