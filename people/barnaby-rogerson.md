# Barnaby Rogerson

## Empire connection

Barnaby Rogerson joins Empire's Iran series for the Safavid conversion of Iran to Shi'a Islam.

## Selected books

Rogerson has written travel, biography and narrative history across North Africa and the Islamic world. Relevant titles include:

- *The Heirs of the Prophet Muhammad*.
- *The Prophet Muhammad: A Biography*.
- *The Last Crusaders*.
- *The House Divided: Sunni, Shia and the Making of the Middle East*.

## Blackball use

The Safavid material is useful for distinguishing state-building from anachronistic claims about permanent sectarian identities. Ask which institutions promoted religious conformity, how clerical authority was organized, and how policy reached local populations.
