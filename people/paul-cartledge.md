# Paul Cartledge

## Empire connection

Classicist Paul Cartledge joins Empire's Greek-Persian-war material, including Thermopylae.

## Selected books

- *Thermopylae: The Battle That Changed the World*.
- *The Spartans*.
- *Alexander the Great*.
- *Ancient Greece: A History in Eleven Cities*.
- *Democracy: A Life*.

## Blackball use

Use Cartledge for Greek political and military history while resisting the later ideological habit of turning Thermopylae into a timeless "West versus East" myth. The Persian Empire and Greek poleis need to be reconstructed as their own institutions before applying modern geopolitical categories.
