# Lena Epstein

## Why Blackball is recording her

Lena Epstein is a Michigan business executive and Republican political candidate who sought election to the University of Michigan Board of Regents in 2022 and is again a nominee in 2026. A regent exercises public authority over a public university. Her education, business succession, institutional affiliations, stated university policies, and claims of qualification are therefore appropriate subjects for documented public scrutiny.

This note distinguishes verified biography from interpretation. It does not treat candidacy as permission to collect irrelevant private information.

## Verified educational and business sequence

- Epstein studied economics at Harvard University from 1999 to 2003 and received a bachelor's degree.
- Vesco Oil Corporation's own corporate history says that she joined Vesco in 2003.
- She received an MBA from the University of Michigan Ross School of Business in 2008.
- Vesco was founded by her grandfather Eugene Epstein in 1947.
- Her father, Donald Epstein, led Vesco as CEO from 1995 to 2020. He had also attended Harvard and received a law degree from the University of Michigan.
- Public company and campaign accounts describe Lena Epstein as a third-generation co-owner, board member, and executive of Vesco.
- She and other family members continued to own and govern the company after her father's retirement.

The order matters:

> Harvard BA → entry into the family company → Michigan MBA → continued executive and ownership roles

The available chronology does **not** support the simpler story that an MBA enabled Epstein to enter Vesco. She entered a company founded by her grandfather and then led by her father before receiving the MBA.

## Question raised by the MBA

The obvious criticism is that Epstein could have entered her father's company without an MBA; according to the public chronology, she did. The degree therefore should not be presented without qualification as the cause of her access to the business.

That does not make the MBA useless or fraudulent. It raises a different question about what an MBA can provide to someone who already possesses an inherited place in a substantial enterprise.

A person expecting education to produce a first job asks whether the degree opens an otherwise closed occupational door. A person already positioned to own or govern a family company can use business school for another purpose: learning how other enterprises handle succession, acquisitions, finance, governance, taxation, family ownership, executive hiring, charitable foundations, and the conversion of private wealth into civic or political authority. These are important problems, but they are problems that relatively few families ever have occasion to face.

The classmates may also matter. Studying business beside heirs, financiers, executives, consultants, and future public officials may provide comparison cases, trust, introductions, and relationships useful over decades. This is the same distinction developed in [Reading beside future authority figures](../READING-BESIDE-FUTURE-AUTHORITY-FIGURES.md): a degree can be sold to one student as a route to wages and used by another family as preparation for preserving and exercising an already existing institutional position.

This remains a hypothesis. An MBA curriculum and a shared classroom do not establish that Epstein obtained any particular advantage, relationship, appointment, or business result from them.

## Public-authority trajectory

Epstein's publicly documented political and civic roles include:

- co-chair of Donald Trump's 2016 Michigan presidential campaign;
- 2018 Republican nominee for Michigan's 11th Congressional District;
- 2022 Republican nominee for the University of Michigan Board of Regents;
- membership on Donald Trump's National Finance Committee in 2020;
- finance chair of the Michigan Republican Party in 2024;
- another Republican nomination for University of Michigan regent in 2026;
- board roles with the Detroit Regional Chamber of Commerce, Community Foundation for Southeast Michigan, and Detroit Historical Society;
- a leadership role in the Marjory and Donald Epstein Family Charitable Foundation.

These positions form a documented path among family business ownership, philanthropy, civic boards, party finance, electoral candidacy, and attempted authority over a major public institution. They do not by themselves establish that Harvard or Michigan caused the trajectory.

## Questions for investigation

1. What did Ross teach about family-enterprise succession, closely held firms, acquisitions, boards, foundations, and political or civic leadership during Epstein's enrollment?
2. Who studied with Epstein at Harvard and Ross, and which classmates later occupied significant business, governmental, philanthropic, or university positions?
3. Is there sourced evidence that any educational relationship was later activated through hiring, investment, appointment, fundraising, endorsement, or board service?
4. Which roles followed from family ownership, which required independent selection, and which combined the two?
5. How does Epstein publicly describe the value of her MBA, and does that description differ when addressing voters, students, business audiences, or other wealthy families?
6. When she argues that Michigan should connect a degree to a Michigan job, how does that claim compare with her own sequence, in which access to the family company preceded the MBA?
7. What useful knowledge can an MBA supply specifically to people managing inherited enterprises, and how openly do universities describe that purpose?
8. What comparison group would allow Blackball to distinguish education, inherited access, peer relationships, political affiliation, wealth, and individual performance?

## Source discipline

Campaign and institutional biographies are useful for identifying claims, but they are interested sources. Claims about revenue, business growth, leadership responsibility, philanthropy, or the effects of education require independent checking where possible.

No acquaintance or causal connection should be inferred merely from overlapping attendance dates. Computer-generated criticism should challenge every causal interpretation in this note and propose evidence that could falsify it.
