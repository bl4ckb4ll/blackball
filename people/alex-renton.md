# Alex Renton

## Empire connection

Alex Renton appears in the slavery/reckoning material, where family wealth and inherited historical responsibility are central subjects.

## Book

- *Blood Legacy: Reckoning With a Family's Story of Slavery*.

Author/publisher search starting point: https://www.alexrenton.com/

## Blackball use

Renton's work is useful because it makes the balance sheet of slavery personal without reducing the problem to personal guilt. The stronger empirical questions are: which estates and firms generated the wealth, how ownership transferred across generations, what compensation owners received at abolition, and which later assets or institutions can be traced to those transfers.
