# Campbell Price

## Empire connection

Egyptologist Campbell Price appears in Empire's Ancient Egypt material, including discussion of mummies, representation and later reception of Egypt.

## Book

- *Golden Mummies of Egypt: Interpreting Identities from the Graeco-Roman Period*.

Manchester Museum profile: https://www.museum.manchester.ac.uk/about/people/campbell-price/

## Blackball use

Price is useful for material culture and for the history of how Egypt was collected, displayed and interpreted in Britain. That creates two separate dossiers: ancient Egyptian society and the modern imperial/museum system that acquired and categorized Egyptian objects.
