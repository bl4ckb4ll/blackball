# Nandini Das

## Empire connection

Nandini Das joins Empire for the Thomas Roe/Mughal court episode, centered on early English diplomatic and commercial contact with Mughal India.

## Book

- *Courting India: England, Mughal India and the Origins of Empire*.

University of Oxford profile: https://www.english.ox.ac.uk/people/professor-nandini-das

## Blackball use

Das is valuable precisely because this is **before** the East India Company became a territorial sovereign. It gives a baseline for measuring how weak and dependent English merchants initially were at the Mughal court, and therefore how much institutional power changed over the following century and a half.
