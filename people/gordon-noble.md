# Gordon Noble

## Empire connection

Archaeologist Gordon Noble appears in Empire episode 386 on the Picts and northern Britain beyond the Roman frontier.

## Selected book

- With Nicholas Evans, *Picts: Scourge of Rome, Rulers of the North*.

University of Aberdeen profile: https://www.abdn.ac.uk/people/g.noble

## Blackball use

Noble's archaeology is useful for reconstructing political organization from forts, settlements, monuments and material remains rather than relying entirely on Roman descriptions of peoples outside imperial control. This is a good case for asking who produced the surviving description and what interests shaped the label.
