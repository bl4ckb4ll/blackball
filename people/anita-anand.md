# Anita Anand

## Why Blackball is recording her

Anita Anand is a journalist, broadcaster, author and co-host of **Empire: World History**. Her books and broadcasts repeatedly recover people who sit awkwardly inside simple imperial/national stories: Sophia Duleep Singh, the Koh-i-Noor's chain of possession, and Udham Singh.

## Blackball use

Anand is useful for connecting elite imperial institutions to individual lives that cross class, race, gender and national categories. *Sophia* also belongs beside Blackball material on access to elite circles: Duleep Singh could move near royalty while also becoming a militant suffrage campaigner.

*The Patient Assassin* gives a route into the Jallianwala Bagh massacre, colonial policing and the long aftermath of state violence through the life of Udham Singh.

## Source discipline

As with Dalrymple, distinguish host narration from independent corroboration. For episodes built around one of her books, trace the book's footnotes and cited archives rather than treating the podcast retelling as the evidentiary endpoint.
