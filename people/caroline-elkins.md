# Caroline Elkins

## Empire connection

Caroline Elkins appears in Empire's twentieth-century Palestine material and is a major source for the podcast's broader subject of coercion under British colonial rule.

## Books

- *Imperial Reckoning: The Untold Story of Britain's Gulag in Kenya* (published in Britain as *Britain's Gulag*).
- *Legacy of Violence: A History of the British Empire*.

Harvard profile: https://history.fas.harvard.edu/people/caroline-elkins

## Blackball use

Elkins is directly relevant to **delegated coercion / intermediary enforcers**. Her work on counterinsurgency should be mined for chains of command, detention systems, screening/interrogation, local auxiliaries, documentation practices and the distance between policymakers and the people who carried violence out.

## Source discipline

Her work has also been part of strong historiographical disputes. Preserve archival claims and critics' specific objections rather than treating either praise or controversy as a substitute for checking evidence.
