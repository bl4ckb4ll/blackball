**Research topics:** deconstruction, postcolonial criticism, Marxism, feminism, the politics of interpretation.

## First-person intellectual source

- [Spivak in the 1984 *Post-modern Condition: The End of Politics?* discussion](../culture/deconstruction/gayatri-chakravorty-spivak-1984.md) — a source-checked account of how she explains deconstruction, narration, and excluded voices, alongside substantive challenges from Ron Aronson, John Dunn, and Geoffrey Hawthorn.
- **Printed primary record:** *The Post-Colonial Critic: Interviews, Strategies, Dialogues*, ed. Sarah Harasym (Routledge, 1990), 17–34. Publisher: https://www.routledge.com/The-Post-Colonial-Critic-Interviews-Strategies-Dialogues/Harasym-Spivak/p/book/9780203760048

## Evidence boundary

The 1984 debate provides direct evidence for arguments made in that discussion, not a complete judgment of Spivak's published work, classroom conduct, or later intellectual positions. Favorable and unfavorable reputations require independent evidence; neither substitutes for reading what she argues.
