# Daniel Immerwahr

## Empire connection

Historian Daniel Immerwahr appears in several Empire episodes on U.S. territorial and overseas power, including "How To Hide An Empire," Theodore Roosevelt, the Philippines and later Greenland/U.S. strategic power.

## Books

- *Thinking Small: The United States and the Lure of Community Development*.
- *How to Hide an Empire: A History of the Greater United States*.

Author site: https://danielimmerwahr.com/

## Blackball use

Immerwahr is particularly useful for making territorial administration measurable. The phrase "United States" can hide colonies, territories, bases and possessions whose residents have different political rights. Track population, citizenship, voting rights, military rule, taxation, land ownership and strategic infrastructure separately.

This is also a useful corrective to maps that visually erase non-state territory.
