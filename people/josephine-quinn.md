# Josephine Quinn

## Empire connection

Ancient historian Josephine Quinn appears at the start of Empire's long Gaza sequence to discuss Philistines and ancient Mediterranean identities.

## Selected books

- *In Search of the Phoenicians*.
- *How the World Made the West: A 4,000-Year History*.

University of Oxford profile: https://www.classics.ox.ac.uk/people/professor-josephine-quinn

## Blackball use

Quinn's work is useful against retrospective nationalist stories that turn ancient cultural labels into modern peoples with uninterrupted identities. Treat trade, language, religion and political organization as separately changing variables rather than assuming one bounded civilization produced "the West."
