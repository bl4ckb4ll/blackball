# Bettany Hughes

## Empire connection

Historian and broadcaster Bettany Hughes appears in the Ottoman series, including material on women, slavery and the imperial harem.

Empire guest index: [link](../history/empire-podcast/guest-index.md)

## Selected books

- *Helen of Troy: Goddess, Princess, Whore*.
- *The Hemlock Cup: Socrates, Athens and the Search for the Good Life*.
- *Istanbul: A Tale of Three Cities*.
- *Venus and Aphrodite*.
- *The Seven Wonders of the Ancient World*.

## Blackball use

Hughes is useful where court, household and gender history complicate a ruler-and-battles narrative. For the Ottoman material, distinguish the harem as an institution of dynasty, reproduction, education, patronage, slavery and political influence from its later European sexualized stereotype.
