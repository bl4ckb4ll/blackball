## Empire connection

## Blackball use

Patrick's life is useful for separating **social status from ability**. Enslavement tells us what power others held over him, not his underlying intelligence, skill or potential. The early-medieval material also requires unusually careful chronology because later hagiography can overwhelm sparse contemporary evidence.
