# Simon Sebag Montefiore

## Empire connection

Simon Sebag Montefiore joins the Russia/Catherine the Great material. His work is heavily biographical and court-centered, making him useful for the elite decision layer of empire but not sufficient by itself for labor or ordinary-life history.

## Selected books

- *Catherine the Great and Potemkin*.
- *Stalin: The Court of the Red Tsar*.
- *Young Stalin*.
- *Jerusalem: The Biography*.
- *The Romanovs: 1613–1918*.
- *The World: A Family History of Humanity*.

Author site: https://simonsebagmontefiore.com/

## Blackball use

Use his court and family material to map who actually had access to rulers and consequential decisions. Then deliberately add the missing lower layers: conscripts, peasants, servants, coerced labor, taxpayers and colonized populations who bore the policies made in those rooms.
