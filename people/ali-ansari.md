# Ali Ansari

## Empire connection

Historian Ali Ansari appears repeatedly in Empire's Iran coverage, including the Pahlavi period and a 2026 episode on Iranian political/religious authority.

## Blackball use

Ansari is useful for institutional histories of Iranian nationalism, monarchy, constitutionalism and the Islamic Republic. Treat "Iran" as changing state institutions and social coalitions, not a unitary actor with a single will across a century.
