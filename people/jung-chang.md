## Blackball use

*Wild Swans* is especially useful as intergenerational family testimony through successive political regimes. Preserve its memoir/testimony status separately from aggregate claims about famine, persecution or mortality.

## Source discipline

*Mao: The Unknown Story* generated substantial scholarly controversy. Do not inherit its strongest claims merely because they are vivid; track citations and the specific responses of China historians.
