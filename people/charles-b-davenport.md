# Charles B. Davenport

Charles Benedict Davenport (1866–1944) was an American biologist and a major organizer of the American and international eugenics movement.

## Harvard connection

- Harvard AB, 1889.
- Harvard AM, 1890.
- Harvard PhD, 1892.
- Remained at Harvard as an instructor until 1899, when he joined the University of Chicago faculty.
- Harvard's own *Harvard & the Legacy of Slavery* research identifies Davenport as a key eugenics leader who asserted biological racial differences, white racial superiority, and opposition to racial mixing.
- In 1910 Davenport and Harry H. Laughlin established the Eugenics Record Office at Cold Spring Harbor.

This is a substantive Harvard institutional connection, not merely an honorary or incidental association.

## Blackball relevance

Davenport belongs in the Harvard institutional file as part of the history of elite universities producing, credentialing, legitimating, and circulating racial hierarchy as scholarship. The case should be handled historically rather than collapsed into a generic claim that Harvard as a whole held one position.

The Harvard report is especially useful because it documents the connection in Harvard's own institutional historical accounting.

## Do not confuse with John A. Davenport

Charles B. Davenport is **not** John Alfred Davenport, the Yale graduate who later edited *Barron's* and *Fortune* and defended white-minority governments in Rhodesia and South Africa. Keep separate dossiers for the two men.
