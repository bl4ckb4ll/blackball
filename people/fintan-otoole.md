# Fintan O'Toole

## Empire connection

Fintan O'Toole appears near the end of Empire's Ireland series to discuss modern Ireland and the social transformation that followed independence.

## Selected books

- *We Don't Know Ourselves: A Personal History of Modern Ireland*.
- *Heroic Failure: Brexit and the Politics of Pain*.
- *Ship of Fools: How Stupidity and Corruption Sank the Celtic Tiger*.

Author page: https://www.fintanotoole.com/

## Blackball use

O'Toole is useful for the long aftermath question: formal independence does not itself answer what happened to class hierarchy, migration, church authority, housing, wages, public services or access to institutions. Use the political chronology as a scaffold for those measurable social outcomes.
