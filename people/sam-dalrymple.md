# Sam Dalrymple

## Empire connection

Sam Dalrymple joins Empire for the 2025/2026 sequence on partitions across British-ruled Asia.

## Book

- *Shattered Lands: Five Partitions and the Making of Modern Asia*.

Author site: https://samdalrymple.com/

## Blackball use

The book widens "Partition" beyond the India-Pakistan border. That is useful for measuring how imperial administrative units were broken apart across Burma/Myanmar, the Gulf, India/Pakistan and later Bangladesh. Track who drew borders, citizenship rules, forced/voluntary migration, property loss, violence, and the institutions inherited by successor states.
