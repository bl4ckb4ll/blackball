# Karen Hao

## Empire connection

Journalist Karen Hao is the guest in Empire episode 382, where the show uses her reporting on large AI companies — especially OpenAI — to discuss private technological power through an imperial analogy.

## Book

- *Empire of AI: Dreams and Nightmares in Sam Altman's OpenAI*.

Publisher: https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/743569/empire-of-ai-by-karen-hao/

## Blackball use

Hao's work is useful for turning a vague story about "AI" into institutions and inputs: finance, chips/compute, electricity, water, data, contractor labor, management, government relationships and competitive strategy.

The important Blackball move is not to accept "empire" as a label. Measure concentration and bargaining power dimension by dimension, and compare those measurements with historical chartered companies.

See: [Empire AI notes](../history/empire-podcast/episode-notes/tech-ai-381-382.md).
