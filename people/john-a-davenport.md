# John A. Davenport

John Alfred Davenport (1904–1987) was an American economic journalist and editor.

## Yale connection

Davenport was a graduate of Yale University.

## Barron's and Fortune

- Editor of *Barron's National Business and Financial Weekly*, 1949–1956.
- Later returned to *Fortune*, where he became assistant managing editor and a member of the editorial board.

## Southern Africa

Davenport later became a prominent American defender of Rhodesia under Ian Smith and an opponent of sanctions against the white-minority governments of Rhodesia and South Africa. His writing argued against immediate universal majority rule and treated property or educational restrictions on the franchise as preferable alternatives.

This makes him relevant to the Blackball investigation of a recurring pro-white-minority-rule current around *Barron's* editorial history. The claim should remain specific: Davenport's own published positions and institutional role are documented; broader claims about the magazine require separate evidence about other editors and editorial policy.

## Do not confuse with Charles B. Davenport

John A. Davenport is **not** Charles Benedict Davenport, the Harvard-trained biologist and eugenics organizer. Keep separate dossiers for the two men.
