# Lyse Doucet

## Empire connection

BBC journalist Lyse Doucet appears in a subscriber bonus listed on 2 September 2026 about Afghanistan across Soviet, civil-war, U.S./NATO and Taliban periods.

## Book

- *The Finest Hotel in Kabul: A People's History of Afghanistan*.

Publisher: https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/691100/the-finest-hotel-in-kabul-by-lyse-doucet/

## Blackball use

The book's hotel-centered structure is useful for Blackball because it can keep the same workplace/location in view while regimes change around it. That allows comparison of what war and political transition mean for workers, guests, women, supply chains, security and ordinary daily life rather than only for presidents, generals and insurgent leaders.
