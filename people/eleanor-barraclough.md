## Blackball use

Barraclough is especially useful for ordinary-object and material-history methods. Small surviving objects can recover lives that elite chronicles barely record. The Viking material is also a reminder to separate occupation (raider/trader/farmer), language, political allegiance and later ethnic labels instead of treating "Viking" as a single biological people.
