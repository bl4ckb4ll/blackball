# Francesca Stavrakopoulou

## Empire connection

Biblical scholar Francesca Stavrakopoulou appears in Empire's Ancient Egypt/religion sequence when the show turns from Egyptian and Levantine religion toward the origins of later monotheistic traditions.

## Selected book

- *God: An Anatomy*.

University of Exeter profile: https://politics.exeter.ac.uk/staff/stavrakopoulou/

## Blackball use

Her work is useful for separating later theological abstraction from the embodied language and material cult practices present in ancient texts. For historical claims, distinguish what a text says, when its layers were composed, archaeological evidence, and later religious interpretation.
