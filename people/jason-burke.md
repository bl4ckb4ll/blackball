## Blackball use

Burke is useful for separating ideology from organization. The episodes should be mined for specific institutions: prisons, religious networks, funding, charities/offices, travel routes, training camps, armed groups and state sponsors/opponents.

A central source-audit question is the common claim that the CIA "funded bin Laden." Break it into separate money/organizational links and require evidence for each.
