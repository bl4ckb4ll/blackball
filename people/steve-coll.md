## Blackball use

Coll's bin Laden work supplies an important institutional and class context: the family fortune came from construction contracting tied to Saudi state development. That puts Osama bin Laden's biography inside elite wealth, state-business relationships and transnational networks rather than treating terrorism as an ideology floating free of material institutions.

*Private Empire* is separately useful for Blackball's company-power comparisons.
