# Alex von Tunzelmann

## Empire connection

Alex von Tunzelmann appears in Empire's Cuba material and in the 2026 bonus series on Mountbatten, Nehru and Partition.

## Selected books

- *Indian Summer: The Secret History of the End of an Empire*.
- *Red Heat: Conspiracy, Murder and the Cold War in the Caribbean*.
- *Blood and Sand: Suez, Hungary and Eisenhower's Campaign for Peace*.
- *Fallen Idols: Twelve Statues That Made History*.

Author site: https://www.alexvontunzelmann.com/

## Blackball use

Her books sit at the intersection of political biography and imperial breakdown. For Partition especially, do not let elite relationships substitute for the administrative and material mechanisms of division: boundary drawing, police/military capacity, transport, refugee movement, property loss and mass violence.
