# Catherine Mayer

## Empire connection

Journalist and author Catherine Mayer appears in Empire bonus material on royal women and empire.

## Selected books

- *Born to Be King: Prince Charles on Planet Windsor*.
- *Attack of the 50 Ft. Women*.
- *Good Grief: Embracing Life at a Time of Death* (with Anne Mayer Bird).
- *Divide and Rule: Royal Women and their Battles*.

Author site: https://catherine-mayer.com/

## Blackball use

The royal-family material is useful for access-to-power analysis: who obtains proximity to rulers, who converts family status into institutional influence, and how gender changes the formal versus informal channels available to elite actors.
