# Lloyd Llewellyn-Jones

## Empire connection

Ancient historian Lloyd Llewellyn-Jones appears in Empire's Persian and Egyptian material, including the Cyrus/Persian sequence.

## Selected books

- *Aphrodite's Tortoise: The Veiled Woman of Ancient Greece*.
- *King and Court in Ancient Persia 559 to 331 BCE*.
- *Designs on the Past*.
- *Persians: The Age of the Great Kings*.
- *Ancient Persia and the Book of Esther*.
- *The Cleopatras: The Forgotten Queens of Egypt*.

## Blackball use

His court-history work is useful for reconstructing imperial administration and elite ideology in periods where modern categories such as nation-state, race and bureaucracy do not map cleanly onto the evidence. Keep court sources separate from claims about the daily experience of ordinary subjects.
