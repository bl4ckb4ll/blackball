# Eugene Rogan

## Empire connection

Eugene Rogan appears in Empire's Ottoman/Gaza and Arab-Israeli-history material.

## Major books

- *The Arabs: A History*.
- *The Fall of the Ottomans: The Great War in the Middle East*.
- *Frontiers of the State in the Late Ottoman Empire*.

University of Oxford profile: https://www.sant.ox.ac.uk/people/eugene-rogan

## Blackball use

Rogan is useful for tracing the institutional transition from Ottoman rule through wartime occupation, mandates and modern states. That transition should be measured administratively: who collects taxes, who polices, which legal system applies, how borders and citizenship are defined, and what military power backs the arrangement.
