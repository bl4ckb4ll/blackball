# William Dalrymple

## Why Blackball is recording him

William Dalrymple is a historian, writer and co-host of **Empire: World History**. He is unusually important to the podcast because many of its recurring subjects — the East India Company, Mughal India, Afghanistan, the Koh-i-Noor and Indian cultural influence — overlap directly with his own books.

That makes him both a source and a framing influence. Blackball should preserve the distinction between an episode summarizing Dalrymple's own historical argument and an episode independently triangulating that argument with other scholarship.

## Blackball use

Dalrymple is especially useful for studying the East India Company as an institution rather than treating British India as if it were always administered by a unified British state. Follow company ownership, armies, tax collection, Indian financiers and rulers, commodity flows and the transition from Company to Crown rule.

## Source discipline

His narrative histories are substantial secondary sources, not primary evidence. When a strong causal claim matters, follow his notes/bibliography into the archival material and compare with competing historians.
