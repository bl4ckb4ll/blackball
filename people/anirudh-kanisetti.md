# Anirudh Kanisetti

## Empire connection

Anirudh Kanisetti joins Empire's Chola series on southern India and Indian Ocean power.

## Books

- *Lords of the Deccan: Southern India from the Chalukyas to the Cholas*.
- *Lords of Earth and Sea: A History of the Chola Empire*.

Author site: https://anirudhkanisetti.com/

## Blackball use

Kanisetti is useful for breaking the implicit equation "empire = European overseas colonialism." The Chola material offers a different institutional form for comparing taxation, temple wealth, armies/navies, agrarian production, merchant networks and political legitimacy.
