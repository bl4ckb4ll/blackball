# Anthony Faulkner Jr.

## Why Blackball is recording him

Anthony Faulkner Jr. belongs in the January 9, 2021 shooting-spree comparison because he was killed in the same sequence of violence as Yiran Fan and Aisha Johnson, but public coverage available so far contains much less institutional biography about him.

That asymmetry should be documented rather than silently filled with assumptions.

## Verified public facts

- **Age:** 20.
- **January 9, 2021:** shot and killed at the AK Food Mart at 9307 S. Halsted Street during the same shooting spree that killed Yiran Fan and Aisha Johnson.
- Contemporary news reports identify him as one of the three people killed in the spree.

## Institutional position

No source reviewed so far identifies Faulkner as a University of Chicago student, alumnus, employee, or other affiliate. He should therefore remain outside the UChicago-affiliate category unless evidence is found.

## Same-event comparison

See [Yiran Fan](yiran-fan.md) and [Aisha Johnson](aisha-johnson.md).

The comparison is useful because all three deaths occurred in one spree, but the victims entered public narratives with very different quantities of institutional information. Fan's academic record and future career were extensively described. Johnson was publicly identified through her work and family. Faulkner's biography is currently much thinner in the sources reviewed.

The correct research response is not to infer that Fan's life contained more "potential." It is to ask why one future was institutionally documented in advance while another was not.

## Questions for investigation

1. What was Faulkner doing in 2020–2021: work, school, training, caregiving, or something else?
2. What biographical information did his family make public?
3. Which outlets produced standalone stories about him rather than grouping him with the shooting spree?
4. Did any employer, school, church, neighborhood group, or other institution issue a memorial or statement?
5. How often was his future discussed compared with Fan's?
6. How did coverage duration and word count compare across the three victims?

## Source discipline

Do not interpret the absence of elite credentials in news coverage as evidence of low ability, low ambition, or low social value. It is evidence only that the reviewed public record contains fewer institutional markers.
