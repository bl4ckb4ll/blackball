# Stephen Fry

## Empire connection

Stephen Fry appears as a guest in Empire's Bronze Age / Greek-myth material. He is primarily a writer, performer and popularizer rather than an academic ancient historian.

## Blackball use

Use Fry as a popular-literary source for how Greek myth is retold to a mass audience, not as the sole authority for archaeological or historical claims. Where the podcast crosses from myth into Bronze Age history, follow the specialist guests and archaeological literature.
