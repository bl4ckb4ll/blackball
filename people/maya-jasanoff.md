# Maya Jasanoff

## Empire connection

Maya Jasanoff joins Empire for the Joseph Conrad episode in the literature-and-empire sequence.

## Books

- *Edge of Empire: Lives, Culture, and Conquest in the East, 1750–1850*.
- *Liberty's Exiles: American Loyalists in the Revolutionary World*.
- *The Dawn Watch: Joseph Conrad in a Global World*.

Harvard profile: https://history.fas.harvard.edu/people/maya-jasanoff

## Blackball use

Jasanoff is useful for following people displaced by imperial transitions rather than treating a revolution's official victor as the entire story. *Liberty's Exiles* in particular opens questions about property, migration, compensation and what happened to Loyalists and enslaved people after U.S. independence.
