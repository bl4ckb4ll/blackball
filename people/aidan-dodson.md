# Aidan Dodson

## Empire connection

Egyptologist Aidan Dodson appears in Empire's Ancient Egypt material, especially the Amarna/Tutankhamun period.

## Selected books

Dodson has written or co-written many books. Relevant selected titles include:

- *Amarna Sunset: Nefertiti, Tutankhamun, Ay, Horemheb, and the Egyptian Counter-Reformation*.
- *Monarchs of the Nile*.
- With Dyan Hilton, *The Complete Royal Families of Ancient Egypt*.
- *Tutankhamun, King of Egypt: His Life and Afterlife*.
- *The Canopic Equipment of the Kings of Egypt*.

University of Bristol profile: https://research-information.bris.ac.uk/en/persons/aidan-dodson

## Blackball use

Dodson is useful for reconstructing succession, court institutions, tombs and material evidence. Keep archaeological inference separate from later dramatic narratives about personalities where the surviving evidence is thin.
