## Corroborating mechanisms already independently documented

These items are **not** being attributed to Flat Rate Master without a transcript.

### Flat-rate hours are not clock hours

The U.S. Department of Labor says explicitly that a dealership mechanic's “flat rate” hour is not an actual clock hour. A technician may be physically at work for one number of hours while receiving credit for a different number of flat-rate hours depending on the jobs completed.

Source:
https://www.dol.gov/agencies/whd/field-operations-handbook/Chapter-21

### Wage distribution is far below the six-figure headline for most workers

BLS May 2025:

- national median: $50,620;
- 90th percentile threshold: $81,790;
- motor-vehicle-and-parts-dealer median: $58,140.

Sources:
https://www.bls.gov/ooh/installation-maintenance-and-repair/automotive-service-technicians-and-mechanics.htm
https://www.bls.gov/iag/tgs/iag441.htm

### Technician-owned tools are real worker capital

BLS says service technicians typically own their common hand tools themselves. Ford's ASSET FAQ says students typically need a basic automotive hand-tool set.

Sources:
https://www.bls.gov/ooh/installation-maintenance-and-repair/automotive-service-technicians-and-mechanics.htm
https://www-newfordtech.app.ford.com/Home/FAQ/ASSET

### Physical cost is not rhetorical

BLS says the occupation involves prolonged standing, uncomfortable positions, heavy objects, fumes/chemicals/noise and one of the highest rates of occupational injuries and illnesses.

Source:
https://www.bls.gov/ooh/installation-maintenance-and-repair/automotive-service-technicians-and-mechanics.htm

## Transcript-recovery acceptance test

A future transcript pass is complete only if it records:

- transcript source and whether human captions, creator captions or auto-captions;
- exact video duration;
- timestamp for every retained claim;
- speaker's own distinction, if any, among flat-rate rate, effective clock-hour earnings and annual earnings;
- every numerical example exactly as stated;
- any distinction among independent shops, dealerships, warranty work and customer-pay work;
- any claims about tools, debt, schooling, certifications, benefits or schedule;
- any claims about technician exit/retention;
- any proposed remedy;
- enough surrounding context to prevent a clipped sentence from reversing or overstating the speaker's meaning.

- Do not infer the speaker's personal earnings from the title.
- Do not treat one creator as representative of all mechanics.
- Do not use dissatisfaction as proof that wages are objectively low without a denominator.
