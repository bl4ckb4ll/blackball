# Angus Maddison — historical statistics on world GDP

## Scope

Angus Maddison reconstructed population, GDP, and GDP per capita over very long historical periods so that economic performance could be compared across countries and centuries. This note preserves a compact set of his published world-GDP benchmarks and points to both his original data and the later Maddison Project continuation.

The benchmark table below comes from Angus Maddison, *The World Economy: Historical Statistics* (OECD Development Centre, 2003), Table 8b, “World GDP, 20 Countries and Regional Totals, 1–2001 AD.”

**Unit:** millions of 1990 international Geary-Khamis dollars.

## Final original Maddison database

The Groningen Growth and Development Centre preserves the final database version supplied by Maddison. It covers world population, GDP, and GDP per capita from AD 1 through 2008. Groningen also preserves Maddison's archived original site.

- Final Maddison Database 2010 release page: https://www.rug.nl/ggdc/historicaldevelopment/maddison/releases/maddison-database-2010?lang=en
- Original Maddison material and archive links: https://www.rug.nl/ggdc/historicaldevelopment/maddison/original-maddison?lang=en

For a question specifically about **Maddison's own estimates**, prefer the original publication or final original Maddison database rather than silently substituting a later revision.

## Later continuation: Maddison Project Database

After Maddison's death in 2010, colleagues continued and revised the series as the Maddison Project Database. The University of Groningen explicitly describes this as a continuation of Maddison's work, with original estimates retained unless newer or better evidence supports revisions.

The current release located during this source pass is **Maddison Project Database 2023**, covering 169 countries and aggregate regions from AD 1 through 2022.

- Project overview: https://www.rug.nl/ggdc/historicaldevelopment/maddison/?lang=en
- Release history, including the original 2010 database and MPD 2013/2018/2020/2023: https://www.rug.nl/ggdc/historicaldevelopment/maddison/releases/?lang=en
- MPD 2023 release page: https://www.rug.nl/ggdc/historicaldevelopment/maddison/releases/maddison-project-database-2023?lang=en
- Dataset DOI: https://doi.org/10.34894/INZBF2
- Update paper DOI: https://doi.org/10.1111/joes.12618

The MPD release page gives the citation as Bolt and van Zanden (2024), “Maddison style estimates of the evolution of the world economy: A new 2023 update.” The final journal bibliographic record is Jutta Bolt and Jan Luiten van Zanden (2025), *Journal of Economic Surveys* 39(2), 631–671. Both point to the same DOI above.

The MPD 2023 dataset is licensed CC BY 4.0. Its release page also gives additional attribution requirements when graphing the data or using subsets of fewer than twelve countries.

## Evidence cautions

- These early historical GDP numbers are reconstructions, not measurements from modern national-accounts systems. Uncertainty generally becomes much greater farther back in time.
- “1990 international Geary-Khamis dollars” are a purchasing-power-comparison unit. Do not read the values as current US dollars.
- Maddison Project revisions can differ materially from Maddison's original figures because methods and historical evidence have changed. Record the data vintage whenever quoting a number.
- Do not splice figures from different Maddison/MPD releases into one series without documenting the methodological break.
