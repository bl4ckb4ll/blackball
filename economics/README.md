# Economics

Evidence-led notes on economic thought, economic history, institutions, measurement, and claims that benefit from being read against their original sources.

This folder is not restricted to texts that would now be shelved in an economics department. Eighteenth-century political economy grew partly inside moral philosophy, jurisprudence, history, and theology, before economics became a separate university discipline.

## Adam Smith

- [*The Theory of Moral Sentiments* (1759)](adam-smith-theory-of-moral-sentiments.md) — Smith's account of sympathy, moral judgment, the impartial spectator, justice, virtue, status, and the corruption of judgment by admiration of wealth and rank; includes its relation to *The Wealth of Nations* and an edition/source note.
- [Adam Smith people dossier](../people/adam-smith.md) — career chronology and a scale check on what it meant to be a professor in Scotland in 1759, including a cautious reconstruction of the Scottish professoriate and comparison with colonial North America.
