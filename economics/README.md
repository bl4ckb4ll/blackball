## Political economy and primary texts

- [Marx and Engels — the “second page” of *The Communist Manifesto*](marx-engels-second-page-of-the-communist-manifesto.md) — the Part I passage where Marx and Engels catalogue the bourgeoisie's historically revolutionary achievements: destruction of feudal relations, continual technical change, the world market, urbanization, modern communications and transport, political centralization, and unprecedented productive capacity.
