# BME-01: original-paper audit

The original advising reconstruction identifies a decision-order failure: program ranking preceded checking whether the degree would serve the student's occupational aim. This audit makes the paper usable for that question without turning an interview study into an employment survey. It preserves adverse evidence and favorable accounts under the same evidentiary rules.

## What the design permits

This section is the audit's analysis, not a paraphrase of the authors' discussion.

A volunteered account can establish the presence of a concern or reasoning pattern. It cannot supply a population rate without a sampling and measurement argument. Repeated interviews can illuminate persistence or change within a participant; treating them as independent people would manufacture evidence. Likewise, a within-sample mention count measures coded speech under the interview conditions, not the number obtaining jobs or even the number privately holding a view.

Selection through co-curricular engagement leaves out an especially relevant comparison: students unable to obtain such engagement. Saturation addresses whether further interviews add themes in the selected context. It cannot repair coverage of people outside the recruitment channels. Nor can a sample of persisting third-year students represent students who already left the degree. The appropriate follow-up is to recruit or observe those groups explicitly, rather than assign them the views of the available participants.

The changing interview protocol creates an interpretation problem for longitudinal frequency. Before treating a later mention as a changed belief, inspect whether the interviewer newly prompted the topic. Attrition and pandemic timing are additional rival explanations for a changed count. The protocol and transcript coverage needed to test these explanations remain unresolved.

Researcher consensus is a declared interpretive procedure. This audit has not reproduced the codes, independently coded transcripts, or calculated inter-rater agreement. No such result should be inferred from the fact that the authors discussed their decisions. The paper's participant confidentiality boundary also prevents casual republication of a full underlying dataset.

The institutional description and author affiliations are separate records. Identifying a study site from a recognizable combination of details would go beyond the paper's explicit anonymized designation. This audit therefore does not substitute Michigan for the anonymous site. Public author affiliation does establish who published the work; it does not establish what every departmental officer knew or what applicants were told.

## Material interpretation tests

The positive and adverse claims in `claims.tsv` are paired with transformations that must be rejected. A reader can use a favorable account to challenge a blanket impossibility claim while still requiring demonstrated openings, hiring requirements and net costs for advice to a new student. Equally, failure to prove deliberate deception does not erase a documented adverse concern or make it irrelevant to disclosure.

The central unresolved mechanism is whether favorable reinterpretation changes access to paid work. A persuasive account of one's education could affect an interview; it does not itself create a vacancy, remove a hiring screen, finance an unpaid opportunity, or pay a relocation deposit. Testing that mechanism requires an intervention or a credible comparative design with actual outcomes. No hiring-effect estimate is admitted from this paper.

For institutional accountability, distinguish a public notice date, a particular person's knowledge, an institution's communications and an applicant's exposure. BME-02 can compare dated prospectuses or advising materials with the public record and ask whether relevant costs and obstacles were disclosed. A later webpage cannot establish what an earlier applicant received. A person's continued enrollment also cannot establish informed consent without the information available when that decision was made.

The contextual alumni figures 26/45/29 are source assertions in the institutional-description paragraph. The paragraph does not identify the survey instrument, cohort, respondent denominator, response rate or outcome-observation interval. They must not become the interview sample's placement rate, a national rate, or a bachelor-only industry rate. `unresolved-sources.tsv` retains the missing provenance rather than manufacturing it.

Background comparisons belong to the cited studies. `background-trace.tsv` records the limited upstream checks performed for this contribution. No current labor-market conclusion is inferred from a historical occupational-stock comparison. BME-05A retains ownership of the later Ohio State 2026 extraction and its numerical reconciliation.

## Interchange mapping

All rows in this directory inherit `envelope.tsv`. In `claims.tsv`, `observation_id` is the stable observation identity, `claim_id` identifies the analytical claim, `source_id` joins `sources.tsv`, and `locator` supplies `source_span`. The joined source row supplies `source_locator`, publication/observation time and precision, source digest and access boundary. No URL alias is an independent source. `passage-map.tsv` is the coverage index, not another observation of each person. `background-trace.tsv` is a separate contribution to source review, with its own explicit extent.

The fine-grained evidence kinds map as follows: `method_report`, `contextual_source_assertion`, `participant_perception`, `author_summary`, `author_framing` and `cited_background` map to common `source_assertion`; `author_interpretation` and `author_recommendation` map to common `interpretation`; `publication_metadata` maps to `checked_observation` for metadata only. The paper's method report being read is not proof that the method was performed as reported. Supported/unsupported conclusions are this audit's interpretation, not additional source assertions.

Publication date is not event time. A year-range describing interviews remains a range within the claim's scope; it is not silently copied to a precise date. Unknown external byte digests reflect retrieval through extracted-text representations and are not zero-length hashes. The numerical count fields in the shared envelope remain unknown because this contribution is a finite reading audit, not a vacancy-retrieval result. No failed read becomes an empty population.
