# UVA’s residential data-science master’s and Capital One

The residential program advertises faculty with industry experience and access to employer-sponsored capstones. UVA profiled Miriam Friedel in October 2026 after she joined the school as associate professor of practice. She had worked at Capital One since 2020 and left as vice president of machine learning engineering. Her move went from the firm to the university. In April 2026 Capital One staff spoke with graduate students through a university industry event. These records establish real corporate access for an experienced faculty member and direct contact for some students. ## Paying for the 2026–27 residential program

UVA lists **$44,827 for tuition and fees** plus **$36,960 in estimated living expenses** for a Virginia resident over the three terms. The full estimate is **$81,787**. The nonresident estimate is $97,562. The $500 deposit credits the first bill. UVA lists a need-based fellowship program and describes typical awards as up to 10% of total attendance cost. Awards credit the student account contingent on enrollment and standing. [UVA price and term schedule](https://datascience.virginia.edu/degrees/info/tuition-and-fees) · [Financial aid and payment options](https://datascience.virginia.edu/student-services/financial-aid)

The example uses an $8,000 fellowship within that stated range, $5,000 of family support actually received and $8,000 of savings from wages. These are illustrative amounts applied to UVA’s published price. For a new eligible graduate borrower in 2026–27, the federal annual Direct Unsubsidized limit is $20,500, subject to eligibility and other limits. Most new graduate borrowers lack access to Grad PLUS under the July 2026 rules. A $20,500 Direct Unsubsidized loan at a 1.057% origination fee nets about $20,283.32 in usable cash before any per-disbursement rounding. The stated graduate interest rate for loans first disbursed in 2026–27 is 8.07%. [Loan caps and exceptions](https://fsapartners.ed.gov/sites/default/files/2026-05/FrequentlyAskedQuestionsLoanLimits.pdf) · [FY 2027 loan fee](https://fsapartners.ed.gov/knowledge-center/library/electronic-announcements/2026-05-13/fy27-sequester-required-changes-title-iv-student-aid-programs) · [2026–27 rate table](https://fsapartners.ed.gov/fsa-print/publication/1007224)

At $500 of disposable earnings saved per month, the billed $44,827 alone represents almost 90 months of saving from zero at a constant price. Private earnings foregone while enrolled would increase the cost relative to continuing to work. Loan proceeds can cover authorized attendance expenses. The school can apply them to tuition and fees before any job outcome. The student’s repayment obligation attaches to the principal, and the published admissions and price material does not provide a contract promising a Capital One job. The credit increases purchasing power for a particular purchase. The job risk remains with the student.
