# Alexandre Laumonier

Laumonier is a smaller Blackball shelf for now, but an interesting one: his work treats markets and information systems as physical and historical constructions rather than abstract price equations.

## Books / projects verified in the first pass

- ***6/5*** (Zones Sensibles, 2013–2014) — high-frequency trading, market microstructure, algorithms, exchanges, and the infrastructure beneath apparently instantaneous finance.
- ***4*** (2019) — the radio/microwave communications networks built to gain tiny latency advantages in high-frequency trading.
- ***Notæ*** (2020) — a technological prehistory of encoding and organizing information in the West, roughly the twelfth through seventeenth centuries.
- ***La nature des échanges / The Nature of Exchanges*** (2021) — an anthropological history of market microstructure in the West.

Primary bibliographic source for this seed: Alexandre Laumonier's public CV and publisher/secondary references.
