# Openly redistributable sources in Roger L. Geiger's citation graph

Most of the books located through Internet Archive in the Geiger source graph are controlled lending or preview copies. This file is the opposite list: works Geiger actually cites for which Blackball has verified an affirmative license permitting redistribution.

The standard is not “I found a PDF.” The standard is **an explicit license**.

### Blackball rights decision

`redistribute_here = yes`

Blackball may lawfully keep a copy of the licensed PDF or EPUB provided attribution and the license information stay with it. This is materially different from the `printdisabled`, controlled-borrowing, and preview-only Internet Archive items elsewhere in this directory.

The preferred provenance for any local mirror is the publisher's Luminos edition or the UCL institutional copy, not an arbitrary third-party upload.

## Rule for adding another work here

A work enters this file only when all three conditions are met:

1. the edge from Geiger to the work is directly verified;
2. the exact edition or open version is identified;
3. an explicit license or public-domain basis permits redistribution.

“Open access,” “read online,” an Internet Archive item, a university-hosted PDF, and a Google Books preview are **not by themselves** enough.
