# Ha Jin short-fiction catalog

This file indexes the contents of Ha Jin's four major story collections. It is
bibliographic metadata, not a copy of the stories.

The purpose is to make each story independently retrievable for later Blackball
work: syllabus appearances, lawful online publication, reviews, criticism,
themes, provenance, and claims can attach to a title without collapsing the
whole collection into one record.
