# Lawful access and publication routes for Ha Jin

The goal is to make Ha Jin easy to retrieve without turning Blackball into a
copyright-infringing mirror.

Keep access classes explicit:

- **publisher / magazine story page** — an authorized publication page; current
  paywall behavior can change;
- **excerpt** — only part of a longer work;
- **library borrow** — access depends on participating library/account;
- **controlled digital lending / access restricted** — link only, do not mirror;
- **course reserve** — evidence that enrolled students had a route, not public
  access;
- **metadata / table of contents** — bibliographic evidence only.

## Copyright rule for this dossier

Do not copy a copyrighted story into Blackball because a random full-text site
exists. Prefer the author/publisher/journal page, licensed library service,
controlled borrowing record, or bibliographic metadata. If a third-party page's
authorization is unclear, record it as a discovery lead or omit it.

If a public story page later disappears behind a paywall or is removed, change
the access class; do not preserve a scraped copy merely to keep the link
"working."
