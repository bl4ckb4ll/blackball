# Ha Jin in syllabi and courses

This is an evidence catalog, not a recommendation list and not a claim that the
sample measures Ha Jin's frequency in higher education.

A row enters the **confirmed** table only when an institutional course page,
syllabus, or schedule was inspected and it actually names the Ha Jin work.
Course-blog evidence and search-discovered documents are kept separately.

### Catholic University story-level detail

The MHU 365 schedule is unusually useful because it identifies individual
stories rather than only the collection:

- Aug. 27: "The Bane of the Internet"; "A Composer and His Parakeets"
- Sept. 10: "The Beauty"; "Children as Enemies"; "Choice"; "Shame"
- Sept. 17: "In the Crossfire"; "An English Professor"; "Temporary Love";
  "The House Behind a Weeping Cherry"
- Sept. 24: first writing assignment on Ha Jin's short stories

The PDF itself says "Behind a Weeping Cherry"; the collection title is *The
House Behind a Weeping Cherry*. Preserve the syllabus wording when quoting or
auditing it.

## What not to infer

This catalog does **not** establish:

- how often Ha Jin is taught overall;
- whether a work remained on a syllabus after the archived term;
- student reception or learning outcomes;
- a ranking of Ha Jin against other authors;
- whether a text was freely available to the public merely because students had
  course-reserve or Canvas access.

Those require separate evidence.
