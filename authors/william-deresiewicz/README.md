# William Deresiewicz

## Excellent Sheep: The Miseducation of the American Elite and the Way to a Meaningful Life — 2014

A polemic about elite American higher education and the students produced by its admissions and achievement machinery. Deresiewicz argues that highly selective institutions can train students to become exceptionally good at satisfying institutional expectations while leaving them poorly equipped to decide what they themselves value.

The book moves from the student type (“sheep”), to the problem of building a self, to what colleges and teachers should be for, and finally to the elite university as a mechanism of social reproduction and hereditary meritocracy.
