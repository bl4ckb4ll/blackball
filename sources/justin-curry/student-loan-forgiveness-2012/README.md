# Justin Curry — Student Loan Forgiveness Act post, 18 March 2012

## Claim supported

A user-supplied screenshot of an X/Twitter search result shows a post by Justin Curry (@currying), dated **18 Mar 12**, reading:

> “Support the Student Loan Forgiveness Act of 2012”

The post includes a signon.org petition link and the hashtag `#signon`.

This supports the claim that Curry publicly promoted the Student Loan Forgiveness Act of 2012 during his graduate-school period.

## Claim not supplied by this single image alone

The screenshot does not identify:

- the amount of Curry’s debt;
- which institution generated the debt;
- repayment status;
- whether Curry used the literal word “surprised”;
- Curry’s complete reasons for supporting the act.

The image nevertheless corroborates the substantive recollection that student-loan forgiveness was publicly salient to him during the long academic route. Do not impose a requirement that the screenshot reproduce the user’s later paraphrase word for word before acknowledging what it plainly verifies.
