# After the JD — 2004 first report

**Full source:** [Read the complete publisher-hosted PDF](https://www.americanbarfoundation.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/12/After_JD_Phase-I_2004.pdf).

**Blackball annotations:** [Detailed notes on every numbered section, the front matter, methods appendix, and sponsors](section-notes.md).

## Citation and exact locator

Ronit Dinovitzer, Bryant G. Garth, Richard Sander, Joyce Sterling, and Gita Z. Wilder, *After the JD: First Results of a National Study of Legal Careers*. NALP Foundation for Law Career Research and Education / American Bar Foundation, 2004. 96 PDF pages.

**The 69% finding is on printed page 20, also PDF-viewer page 20**, under “Socioeconomic Background”: respondents' fathers working as managers or professionals. It is not the percentage with lawyer fathers. [Open page 20](https://www.americanbarfoundation.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/12/After_JD_Phase-I_2004.pdf#page=20).

The report contrasts that figure with 20% of the general workforce. Its early-2000s admitted-lawyer sample is not a sample of everyone who paid for law school. Preliminary results are unweighted. See the [methods notes](section-notes.md#methods).
