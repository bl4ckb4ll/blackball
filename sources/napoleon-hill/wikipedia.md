## Snapshot summary

Oliver Napoleon Hill (1883–1970) was an American self-help writer best known for *Think and Grow Rich* (1937) and *The Law of Success* (1928). The Wikipedia article treats Hill as a controversial figure and emphasizes the gap between his public success narrative and the documentary record.

It recounts repeated failed ventures and fraud accusations, including the Acree-Hill Lumber Company, the Automobile College of Washington, the George Washington Institute of Advertising, *Hill's Golden Rule*, and later success-course businesses. It also notes that historians and biographers have found no supporting evidence for Hill's claimed 1908 meeting and collaboration with Andrew Carnegie, and little evidence for several other claimed relationships with famous political and business figures.

The article covers the commercial success of *Think and Grow Rich*, Rosa Lee Beeland's substantial contribution to the book, Hill's later Philosophy of Achievement, his claims about invisible counselors and spirit visitations, his association with W. Clement Stone, and the later Napoleon Hill Foundation.
