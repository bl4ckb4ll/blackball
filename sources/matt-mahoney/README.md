# Matt Mahoney / Ocarina Networks source ledger

Source trail for [`people/matt-mahoney.md`](../../people/matt-mahoney.md).

The immediate question is not merely “who is Matt Mahoney?” It is a claim-reconstruction problem:

1. Where did he teach and study?
2. What was his role at Ocarina Networks?
3. Did he found Ocarina?
4. What did Ocarina actually build and finance?
5. What exactly did Dell acquire, when, and for how much?
6. Is there evidence that Mahoney personally received $1 million?
7. Where might that remembered $1 million figure have come from?
8. What documents can be preserved or rediscovered if the live web disappears?

## Bottom line

The source record supports the following reconstruction:

- Mahoney earned a PhD at **Florida Institute of Technology**, not the University of South Florida, in 2003 and says he taught classes at Florida Tech through 2009.
- Ocarina Networks was already operating before Mahoney joined it.
- Ocarina announced Mahoney's hire as **chief scientist on 2 September 2008**.
- Contemporary and later sources identify other people as Ocarina founders/co-founders; Mahoney is not identified as a founder in the sources found here.
- On **10 September 2008**, Ocarina announced a compression-research prize pool of up to **$1 million**. That amount belonged to the contest budget, not to Mahoney personally.
- Dell announced an agreement to acquire Ocarina on **19 July 2010**, explicitly saying the terms were not disclosed.
- Dell's SEC filing says the acquisition closed on **29 July 2010**.
- Dell reported approximately **$275 million total cash consideration for KACE + Ocarina + Scalent together**. This does not reveal Ocarina's standalone price and cannot establish Mahoney's proceeds.
- No source found in this pass establishes Mahoney's equity ownership or a personal $1 million acquisition payout.

## Preservation / rights rule

A source being publicly reachable does **not** by itself mean Blackball may republish it.

This ledger therefore uses three dispositions:

- **mirror permitted** — an inspected source affirmatively grants redistribution under stated conditions;
- **open-access / inspect license before mirroring** — public full text is available, but a repository copy should retain the exact license/attribution terms;
- **link only** — no affirmative redistribution license was established in this pass.

For historical web pages, a `Wayback capture index` below is an archive-discovery URL of the form `https://web.archive.org/web/*/<original-url>`. It is not a claim that every listed URL currently has a usable archived capture.
