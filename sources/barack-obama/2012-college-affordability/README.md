# Barack Obama: the April 2012 “best investment” college speeches

Primary-source dossier for a **three-campus** presidential student-loan affordability tour. These records establish what was said and in what setting; they do not independently establish the empirical return on college.

## Tour and wording

| Date | Venue (start, local time) | Wording | Detailed record |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 24 April 2012 | UNC–Chapel Hill (1:13 p.m. EDT) | Higher education was called the single most important investment, and college was described as **one of** the best investments for the person and the country. | [Official UNC transcript/video](https://obamawhitehouse.archives.gov/realitycheck/photos-and-video/video/2012/04/24/president-obama-speaks-student-loan-interest-rates-north-carolina/index__q_page%3D458.html) |
| 24 April 2012 | University of Colorado Boulder (7:20 p.m. MDT) | College as **the best** private and national-future investment. | [Colorado speech](2012-04-24-university-of-colorado-boulder.md) |
| 25 April 2012 | University of Iowa, Iowa City (1:28 p.m. CDT) | Repeated **the best** formulation; additional assurance about students' lifetime earnings. | [Iowa speech](2012-04-25-university-of-iowa.md) |

The remembered phrase “best investment you'll ever make” is a rough paraphrase, **not** the verbatim form in these transcripts. A [25 April White House blog post titled “President Obama: College Is the Best Investment You Can Make”](https://obamawhitehouse.archives.gov/blog/2012/04/25/president-obama-college-best-investment-you-can-make/) describes **Colorado's 24 April appearance**; its publication date is not a second Colorado event.

## Common historical context

All three events advocated congressional action to prevent the **1 July 2012 scheduled interest-rate increase on affected federal Stafford student loans**. Obama combined national unemployment and income comparisons by education with the Obamas' student-debt story, defense of grants and lower college costs, criticism of Republican budget proposals, and calls for student activism under **#DontDoubleMyRate**. This was legislative persuasion in an election year, not individualized financial advice.

An important additional statement appears in the [Iowa speech](2012-04-25-university-of-iowa.md): Obama recounts telling two students facing family job loss and borrowing costs that their own lifetimes' earnings would repay their college investment. Record that prospective assurance separately from his statistical claims about *graduates as a group*.

## Evidence boundaries

- **Verified directly in White House transcripts:** speaker, date, venue, formulations, policy objective, figures Obama asserted, and the Iowa students' account as related by Obama.
- **Not proven by the speeches:** whether particular majors, institutions, prices, noncompleters, or students have positive returns; whether college was the *best* investment relative to alternative paths; whether the two students recovered their costs.
- **Methodological caution:** national associations between credentials and income are neither an individualized earnings guarantee nor, without further analysis, a causal effect of enrolling. Completion, selection, borrowing, opportunity costs, and occupational conversion matter.
- **Preservation status:** live archived White House transcript/video URLs inspected; no local video or full-text transcript copied here; independent audio/text collation and original data-method verification remain open.
