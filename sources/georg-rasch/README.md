# Georg Rasch — journals, intellectual network, and claims of measurement

Status: **initial, selective source investigation**, 2026-10-16. Not an exhaustive literature review, population-level citation audit, verdict that a discipline is pseudoscientific, or assessment of every article in the publications below. Source grades refer to what was actually inspected.

## Question

Does mathematical calibration in Rasch psychometrics establish measurement of a genuine psychological quantity, or can a one-variable model impose a preferred numerical representation on heterogeneous responses? Does the professional publication network produce substantive independent testing, or mostly reinforce its premises through mutual citations, professional authority, and methodological refinement?

**Do not conflate four separate assertions:**
1. A given collection of responses is reasonably summarized by a one-parameter item-response model.
2. A one-dimensional quantity exists independently of the test and predicts other observations.
3. The quantity can justifiably be interpreted as a named psychological construct, such as reading ability, self-esteem, or intelligence.
4. A single general intelligence quantity exists and is measured by all intelligence tests.

None follows merely from writing, fitting, or exponentiating an equation. Rasch's 1960 book includes intelligence and attainment tests, but use of Rasch analysis does **not** itself commit every application to one universal intelligence variable. Do not attribute that stronger claim without a source.

### The 2008 debate: papers explicitly answering one another

All four appeared in **Theory & Psychology 18(1), February 2008** and therefore cannot be honestly dismissed, without reading, as merely a closed Rasch journal publishing mutual praise.

1. **Andrew Kyngdon**, ["The Rasch Model from the Perspective of the Representational Theory of Measurement"](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/0959354307086924), pp. 89–109. Challenges the claimed equivalence between Rasch modeling and representational/conjoint measurement. **Bibliographic/abstract level** pending full text.
2. **Denny Borsboom and Annemarie Zand Scholten**, ["The Rasch Model and Conjoint Measurement Theory from the Perspective of Psychometrics"](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/0959354307086925), pp. 111–117. Explicit reply: fitting Rasch does **not automatically produce** a conjoint measurement representation, but the authors resist saying it is impossible *in principle*. **Abstract inspected**.
3. **Joel Michell**, ["Conjoint Measurement and the Rasch Paradox: A Response to Kyngdon"](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/0959354307086926), pp. 119–124. Explicit reply: attributes must be shown quantitative independently of the model's convenient numerical parametrization. **Abstract inspected**.
4. **Kyngdon**, ["Conjoint Measurement, Error and the Rasch Model: A Reply to Michell, and Borsboom and Zand Scholten"](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/0959354307086927), pp. 125–131. Explicit rejoinder to both replies. **Abstract inspected**.

This exchange is the clearest starting point for the actual intellectual disagreement. The references list links authors and arguments; **a citation is not an endorsement**. See [seed citation graph](citation-edges.tsv).

## People, infrastructure, incentives: not accusations

- John Michael Linacre's [own profile](https://www.winsteps.com/linacre.htm) says he edited RMT from 1989 through 2012 and developed/commercializes the Winsteps and Facets Rasch software. This is a **verified overlap of editor, methodological advocate, software vendor**; determine author and referee independence before inferring any publication distortion.
- AERA Rasch SIG, IOMW workshops, PROMS events, books, awards, and publisher announcements circulate through RMT. Map who writes, edits, reviews, teaches, markets instruments, and sells tools, across specific dates. Do not count announcements as research papers.
- JAM's former editor Richard M. Smith retired in December 2020; NTNU became its publisher with Hak Ping Tam editing starting volume 22. [Publisher primary statement](https://jamntnu.net/index.html). Do not assume the 2000 editorial structure remained intact through 2026.
- The **commercial interests disclosed in Stenner et al. (2013)** are primary-source facts. Examine independent replications and economic incentives; no conclusion of fraud or editorial capture follows merely from disclosure.

## What the first directed graph establishes, and what it does not

The accompanying TSV is a **purposefully chosen seed graph**, not a complete bibliography or statistically representative sample. It distinguishes direct responses, same-outlet references, cross-outlet citations, and a 2013 paper's explicit bibliographic connections. A path from one article to another establishes a textual relationship, **not** an exchange of empty content, agreement, or collusion.

To test "these papers only cite one another": enumerate **all research-content articles** for fixed publication years, identify distinct authors and institutions, extract reference lists, label citations as internal/cross-journal/outside-psychometrics, normalize by field and date, annotate intellectual contribution and independent data, and audit a preregistered random sample **including negative evidence**. Without denominator or external controls, the claim remains untested.

## Provisional findings and falsifiers

- **Supported**: Rasch formalism and acceptable fit do not by themselves prove a psychological latent attribute is unidimensional or causal. Even Rasch-community authors acknowledge this.
- **Supported**: some advocacy papers explicitly choose one-number reporting for practical reasons rather than claiming all observed dimensions collapse mathematically.
- **Supported**: critical arguments appear **inside** JAM and RMT and **outside** both venues, with replies. The literature is not wholly uncritical.
- **Open**: whether particular applied papers illegitimately promote model assumptions to claims about reality; if so, which papers, populations, instruments, and claims?
- **Open**: whether there is excessive insular citation, insufficient substantive novelty, weak peer review, or systematic disregard of unfavorable data. No systematic audit has yet been completed.
- **Falsifier for strong criticism**: predeclared construct, independently held-out or intervention data, clearly falsifiable predictions, replicable comparisons with multidimensional alternatives, and correct generalization across groups/tasks. Such work can justify particular operational uses without proving universal intelligence.
- **Falsifier for unconditional defense**: model passes fit by item deletion or changed tolerance but fails independent test, group invariance, stable cross-task interpretation, or credible manipulation.
