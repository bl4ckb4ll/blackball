# Mike Lynch — court and legal-document inventory

Primary-source map for the Autonomy civil litigation, Lynch’s extradition litigation, and the U.S. criminal prosecution.

## Important evidentiary warning

Do not merge the legal outcomes into one binary label.

- An **indictment** records allegations, not guilt.
- The **English civil litigation** produced findings substantially favorable to the HP-related claimants under the applicable civil law and standard.
- The **U.S. criminal trial** ended with Lynch acquitted on every count submitted to the jury; the prosecution therefore did not establish those criminal charges beyond a reasonable doubt.
- Different proceedings can reach different results without either result being fictional or logically inconsistent.

## U.S. criminal case

### Case identity

- Court: U.S. District Court for the Northern District of California.
- Case: *United States v. Michael Richard Lynch and Stephen Keith Chamberlain*.
- Docket: **3:18-cr-00577-CRB**.
- Judge: Charles R. Breyer.

### 29 November 2018 — initial charging announcement

- Source owner: U.S. Attorney’s Office, Northern District of California.
- Official page: https://www.justice.gov/usao-ndca/pr/former-autonomy-ceo-charged-wire-fraud
- At that stage DOJ described one conspiracy count and thirteen wire-fraud counts against Lynch and Chamberlain.
- Status: official government source; link record only.
- Evidence rule: allegations only. The DOJ page itself states the presumption of innocence.

### 21 March 2019 — superseding indictment, docket 21

- Case: 3:18-cr-00577-CRB.
- DOJ later described this as a 17-count superseding indictment.
- Lynch was charged with conspiracy to commit wire fraud, wire-fraud counts, securities fraud, and a further conspiracy count; Chamberlain faced the overlapping non-securities counts.
- DOJ description: https://www.justice.gov/usao-ndca/pr/michael-richard-lynch-former-ceo-autonomy-corporation-makes-appearance-federal-court
- Public PDF copy discovered at the UK Financial Reporting Council: https://media.frc.org.uk/documents/Stephen_Chamberlain_Annex_2.pdf
- Status: **candidate for local mirroring**. The FRC copy is a public secondary host of the federal filing. Before committing binary bytes, verify that it is byte-identical to a PACER/RECAP or court-issued copy if one is available.

### Extradition evidentiary declaration

- Document: Declaration of Assistant U.S. Attorney Robert S. Leach in support of the U.S. extradition request.
- Case number printed in document: CR 18-00577 CRB.
- Public copy: https://regmedia.co.uk/2021/07/27/autonomy_leach_declaration_extradition_us_doj.pdf
- Status: third-party mirror; useful because it lays out the prosecution’s extradition theory in detail.
- Evidence rule: prosecution declaration, not neutral findings.

### 11 May 2023 — bail / release order

- Document: order granting bail and imposing conditions on release.
- Citation: *United States v. Lynch*, 18-cr-00577-CRB-1 (N.D. Cal. May 11, 2023).
- Public text mirror: https://app.midpage.ai/document/united-states-v-lynch-1000327565583
- DOJ account of appearance and conditions: https://www.justice.gov/usao-ndca/pr/michael-richard-lynch-former-ceo-autonomy-corporation-makes-appearance-federal-court
- The court treated Lynch as a substantial flight risk but constructed unusually restrictive release conditions; DOJ reports a $100 million bond and home confinement in San Francisco.
- Status: text mirror + official DOJ summary; search for a free RECAP PDF remains open.

### 2023–2024 — pretrial orders

A number of trial/pretrial orders are indexed by public legal mirrors. Example:

- 9 April 2024 order concerning HP’s motion to quash: https://app.midpage.ai/document/united-states-v-lynch-1000392173241

These should be collected selectively according to whether they bear on contested factual theories, admissibility, expert evidence, or what the jury actually heard. Do not bulk-ingest procedural noise merely because it is public.

### 6 June 2024 — verdict

- Result: federal jury acquitted Michael Lynch on **all 15 counts submitted against him** and also acquitted Stephen Chamberlain on all counts submitted against him.
- Reliable contemporaneous report: Reuters, https://www.reuters.com/legal/british-tech-pioneer-mike-lynch-acquitted-us-fraud-trial-2024-06-06/
- A same-day court order modifying conditions of pretrial release is publicly indexed as docket 561: https://trellis.law/doc/district/14123037/
- Status: **verdict form itself remains a retrieval target**. Do not substitute a news story for the verdict form once the primary filing is located.

## U.S. related case: Sushovan Hussain

Former Autonomy CFO Sushovan Hussain was separately tried and convicted in the Northern District of California before Lynch’s trial.

- DOJ conviction announcement, 30 April 2018: https://www.justice.gov/usao-ndca/pr/former-autonomy-cfo-convicted-wire-fraud

This is relevant background but must not be used as guilt-by-association evidence against Lynch. It does matter for understanding the prosecution theory, the English litigation, and the unusually divergent outcomes among Autonomy executives.

- Matter: U.S. request for extradition of Michael Richard Lynch.
- Public judgment copy: https://regmedia.co.uk/2021/07/27/usa_v_lynch_judgment_westminster_mags.pdf
- Status: third-party public mirror; candidate for local copy after rights/integrity check.

- Neutral citation: **[2022] EWHC 142 (Admin)**.
- Case: CO/4080/2021.
- Official Judiciary page: https://www.judiciary.uk/judgments/149342-2/
- Subject: judicial-review challenge concerning extradition procedure/timing.
- Status: official public judgment; **high-priority mirroring candidate**.

### April 2023 — *Lynch v Government of the United States of America*

- Case: CO/468/2022.
- Court: Divisional Court, King’s Bench Division.
- Judgment dated 12 April 2023; Judiciary page published 21 April 2023.
- Official Judiciary page: https://www.judiciary.uk/judgments/lynch-v-usa/
- Result: Lynch’s attempt to prevent extradition failed.
- Importance: useful judicial summary of the U.S. allegations, the Autonomy technology/business background, the forum arguments, and the extradition history.

- DOJ official account: https://www.justice.gov/usao-ndca/pr/michael-richard-lynch-former-ceo-autonomy-corporation-makes-appearance-federal-court
- DOJ states that Lynch was extradited from the UK and appeared in federal court on 11 May 2023.

## English civil litigation: Autonomy / HP v Lynch and Hussain

### Case identity

- High Court case: **HC-2015-001324**.
- Court: Business and Property Courts of England and Wales, Business List (Chancery Division).
- Claimants included Autonomy/HP-related entities.
- Defendants originally included Michael Richard Lynch and Sushovan Tareque Hussain.

### 28 January 2022 — Summary of Conclusions

- Official Judiciary page: https://www.judiciary.uk/judgments/autonomy-v-lynch/
- Judge: Mr Justice Hildyard.
- Document: summary of conclusions following the extremely long civil trial.
- Importance: concise primary route into the holdings, but **not a substitute for the full judgment**.
- Status: official public judgment; high-priority local-copy candidate.

### 17 May 2022 — main judgment

- Official Judiciary page: https://www.judiciary.uk/judgments/autonomy-and-others-v-michael-richard-lynch-and-another/
- Neutral citation commonly given as **[2022] EWHC 1178 (Ch)**.
- Judge: Mr Justice Hildyard.
- Importance: central primary source for the civil fraud findings. The judgment is exceptionally long; later answers should cite specific paragraphs rather than press summaries whenever feasible.
- Result at high level: the HP-related claimants substantially succeeded on liability, although not every allegation or theory succeeded and the eventual damages question required separate proceedings.
- Status: official public judgment; **highest-priority civil document to mirror/index**.

### 14 May 2025 — related companies-court judgment

- Neutral citation: **[2025] EWHC 1171 (Ch)**.
- Official page: https://www.judiciary.uk/judgments/acl-netherlands-b-v-and-others-v-michael-richard-lynch-and-another/
- Case number remains HC-2015-001324.
- Status: official public source; relationship to the quantum sequence should be described precisely after full reading.

### 22 July 2025 — quantum judgment

- Neutral citation: **[2025] EWHC 1877 (Ch)**.
- Official landing page: https://www.judiciary.uk/judgments/autonomy-corporation-and-others-v-lynch-and-others/
- Official PDF: https://www.judiciary.uk/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/Autonomy-Corporation-and-others-v-Lynch-and-others.pdf
- Lynch had died by this stage; his estate was represented by administrator Jeremy Vaughan Sandelson.
- Importance: damages/quantum rather than the original liability determination.
- Status: official public PDF; high-priority local-copy candidate.

### 24 March 2026 — consequential judgment

- Neutral citation: **[2026] EWHC 691 (Ch)**.
- Official page: https://www.judiciary.uk/judgments/acl-netherlands-b-v-and-others-v-jeremy-vaughan-sandelson/
- Subject includes consequential monetary issues following the quantum determination, including interest.
- Status: official public judgment; candidate for local mirroring.

## Local-hosting / rights status

No binary court document has been copied into this branch yet.

Reasons:

1. the repository-writing connector in this pass is optimized for UTF-8 text, not bulk binary PDF ingestion;
2. a public URL does not automatically mean every attached party filing can be redistributed without checking provenance and rights;
3. for third-party docket mirrors, integrity should be checked against the court/PACER/RECAP or official Judiciary copy where possible;
4. Blackball should preserve a hash and source-owner record when it does mirror a legal document.
