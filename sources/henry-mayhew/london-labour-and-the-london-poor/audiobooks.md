# Public-domain audiobook routes

LibriVox recordings are public domain in the United States. The YouTube links below are LibriVox Audiobooks' uploads; they are useful as a low-friction listening interface, while the LibriVox catalog pages are the durable source records and expose downloadable audio.

## Volume I

- LibriVox catalog: https://librivox.org/london-labour-and-the-london-poor-volume-i-by-henry-mayhew/
- YouTube, Part 1/8: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=O_d0Fh9aNCI
- Reader: **Peter Yearsley**
- Running time: about 53 h 22 m

## Authorship warning for Volume IV

The LibriVox record correctly notes that volume IV is not solely Mayhew's work: it includes material by **Bracebridge Hemyng, John Binny, and Andrew Halliday**, and the printed volume identifies **William Tuckniss** as author of the introductory essay on agencies for suppressing vice and crime. Retrieval from the audio should preserve section authorship where it can be established.
