# FTX / Bankman-Fried federal case — atomic claim ledger

This file decomposes the dossier into retrieval-sized claims. The labels describe the **kind of support**, not a numerical probability.

- **JUDICIAL:** stated in a published court opinion or judgment.
- **DOJ:** stated in an official Justice Department case record or release.
- **TRIAL-EVIDENCE:** witness/document evidence summarized by a court; do not silently convert every witness proposition into a separate judicial finding.
- **GOV-FILING:** government characterization in an adversarial filed submission.
- **INSTITUTIONAL:** university or other institutional biographical source.
- **REPORTING:** contemporaneous reporting; preserve attribution and seek transcript confirmation for consequential use.
