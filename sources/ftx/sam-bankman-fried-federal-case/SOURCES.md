# FTX / Bankman-Fried federal case — source map and provenance

This file records **where each kind of proposition should be sourced**. It is deliberately redundant with the narrative dossier so later retrieval does not have to rediscover the evidentiary hierarchy.

## Tier 1 — judicial sources

### Second Circuit merits opinion — 12 June 2026

**Source:** United States Court of Appeals for the Second Circuit, `United States v. Bankman-Fried`, No. 24-961-cr.

**URL:** https://ww3.ca2.uscourts.gov/decisions/OPN/24-961_opn.pdf

**Use for:**
- case posture;
- seven convictions;
- appellate disposition;
- compact reconstruction of trial evidence;
- Caroline Ellison testimony as summarized on appeal;
- Gary Wang testimony and Alameda account privileges;
- Nishad Singh testimony;
- Can Sun testimony;
- Christian Drappi / Alameda meeting recording;
- Bankman-Fried's own testimony and defense theory;
- later-recovery / fraud legal reasoning;
- appeal issues and holdings.

**Strength:** Best compact source in this dossier. It is a neutral published judicial opinion written after full appellate briefing and argument.

**Limitation:** It is not a complete trial transcript. The court selected facts relevant to the appellate issues.

### District-court docket / judgment

**Case:** `United States v. Samuel Bankman-Fried`, No. 22 Cr. 673 (LAK), SDNY.

**Use for:** exact charging documents, minute entries, judgments, transcript references, evidentiary rulings and sentencing record.

**Retrieval note:** DOJ's case page links many filings, but for exact transcript quotations the official docket copy is preferable to press reproductions.

## Source conflicts and reconciliation notes

### Chinese-bribery dollar figures

Different government records describe different dollar amounts in connection with the alleged Chinese-official bribery scheme. The DOJ FCPA page and the government's Ellison sentencing memorandum should not be mechanically collapsed into one number.

**Rule:** attach each amount to the precise transfer, document and wording used by its source. If the question is “how much was the alleged bribe?”, explain the source discrepancy instead of choosing a number by intuition.

### FTX victim-loss / recovery figures

The criminal trial, sentencing, bankruptcy estate and later asset distributions use different concepts and different valuation dates.

**Do not silently equate:**
- customer account shortfall;
- offense loss;
- restitution;
- criminal forfeiture;
- bankruptcy claims;
- dollar-value distributions;
- recovered/appreciated assets;
- foregone crypto appreciation.

A later answer about whether customers were “made whole” must specify **made whole in what unit, at what date, and under what legal/accounting definition**.

### Caroline Ellison's MIT connection

The safe formulation is:

> Caroline Ellison attended Stanford, not MIT; both of her parents are MIT economists, and she participated in MIT PRIMES while in high school.

Do not shorten that to “Ellison was an MIT student.”

### MIT network

The safe formulation is:

> Bankman-Fried and Wang were MIT graduates; Yedidia and Trabucco also had substantial MIT affiliations; Ellison had strong MIT family/program ties but was a Stanford undergraduate.

The existence of that network is not evidence that MIT as an institution participated in the fraud.
