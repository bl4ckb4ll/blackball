## People

There are **185 unique guest dossiers** across the completed 1998–2000 corpus. The 2000 tranche contains 89 credited guest appearances by 89 distinct people: 74 first appear in 2000 and 15 return from earlier years. Every 2000 topic links to each guest dossier, and every dossier links back to all of that person's ingested appearances.

## Counting note

Public archive counts differ depending on whether repeats and archive extras are included. This corpus counts original topic episodes, identified by BBC programme ID plus original broadcast date, and does not add a new topic for a repeat or a later archive wrapper. A guest appearance is one credited guest on one topic; the presenter is not counted.
