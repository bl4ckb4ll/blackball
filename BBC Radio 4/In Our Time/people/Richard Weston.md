# Richard Weston

## Summary

Richard Weston was an architect, architectural writer and academic known especially for work on modern architecture and figures such as Alvar Aalto. On *Architecture in the 20th Century* he provides the historical and critical counterpoint to Daniel Libeskind's practice, placing spectacular late-century buildings inside the longer development of modernism and architectural institutions.
