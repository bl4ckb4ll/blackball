# John Allen Paulos

## Summary

John Allen Paulos is an American mathematician and writer best known for books such as *Innumeracy*, which examine how weak quantitative reasoning distorts judgments about probability, risk and public claims. On *Maths and Storytelling* he explores both genuine mathematical structures in narrative and the human tendency to perceive patterns where none have been demonstrated. That makes him useful for Blackball whenever a persuasive story is doing work that should be done by a calculation or explicit comparison.
