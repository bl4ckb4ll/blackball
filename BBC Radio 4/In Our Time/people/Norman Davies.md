# Norman Davies

## Summary

Norman Davies is a historian best known for broad histories of Poland, Europe and the British Isles. His work often challenges national histories that treat current borders and political units as natural or inevitable. On *The Nation State* he brings that comparative historical perspective to Britain and the United Kingdom, emphasizing that states, names and identities have changed repeatedly and therefore require historical explanation.
