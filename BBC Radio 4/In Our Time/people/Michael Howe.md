# Michael Howe

## Summary

Michael J. A. Howe was a psychologist whose research examined learning, expertise, exceptional achievement and the development of ability. He argued against treating outstanding performance as adequately explained by an undefined inborn gift, emphasizing sustained development and environmental opportunity. On *Inspiration and Genius* his work is directly relevant to Blackball's attempt to separate demonstrated accomplishment from the conditions that allowed someone to acquire skill and enter a field where accomplishment could be recognized.
