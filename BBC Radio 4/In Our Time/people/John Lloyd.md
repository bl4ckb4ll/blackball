# John Lloyd

## Summary

John Lloyd is a British journalist and editor whose work has concentrated on politics, Russia, Eastern Europe, international affairs and the institutions of journalism. His reporting and editorial career gave him direct experience of both Soviet/post-Soviet politics and Western media institutions.

In *The American Century* he helps place US power in an international rather than purely domestic frame. For Blackball, his contribution is useful when comparing the ideals used to describe a political order with how that order is perceived from countries subject to its economic, military or diplomatic power.
