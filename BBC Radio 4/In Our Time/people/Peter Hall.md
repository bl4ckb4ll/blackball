# Sir Peter Hall

## Summary

Peter Hall was a geographer, planner and one of the most influential scholars of modern cities and urban planning. His work ranged from the history of planning to world cities, transport, regional development and the way dense urban environments generate innovation.

In *The City in the 20th Century* he provides the planning and institutional side of the discussion: cities can be deliberately reshaped, but the success of a plan depends on much more than architectural form. For Blackball this makes him useful when comparing official planning goals with who actually gets housing, transport, work and access.
