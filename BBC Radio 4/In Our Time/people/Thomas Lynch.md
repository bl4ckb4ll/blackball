# Thomas Lynch

## Summary

Thomas Lynch is an American poet, essayist and funeral director. His writing, including *The Undertaking*, draws on decades of practical work caring for the dead and for bereaved families. On *Death* his occupational experience makes him distinct from literary scholars discussing death symbolically: he can describe the material labor, routines, bodies and institutions that public culture often keeps out of view.
