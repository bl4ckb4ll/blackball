# Ted Wragg

## Summary

Ted Wragg was a British education scholar, teacher educator and public commentator known for research on classroom teaching and for criticism of education policy imposed without sufficient knowledge of classroom practice. He became Professor of Education at the University of Exeter. On *Education* he brings the practical and institutional side of schooling to a philosophical debate about what education ought to accomplish.
