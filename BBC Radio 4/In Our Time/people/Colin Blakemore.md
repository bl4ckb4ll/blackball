# Colin Blakemore

## Summary

Colin Blakemore was a British neuroscientist known for research on vision and brain development and for prominent public defenses of animal experimentation in biomedical research. Because his own work involved animal experiments, his role in *Animal Experiments and Rights* is not detached commentary: he argues from inside a contested research practice about its scientific value and necessity.
