# Martin Amis

## Summary

Martin Amis was a British novelist and essayist whose fiction often examined status, masculinity, money, violence and late-twentieth-century social anxiety through satire and highly stylized narration. His books include *Money* and *London Fields*. On *Masculinity in Literature* he speaks as a novelist whose work creates and interrogates male characters rather than as a social scientist measuring men as a population.
