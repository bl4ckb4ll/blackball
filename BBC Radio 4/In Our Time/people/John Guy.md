# John Guy

## Summary

John Guy is a historian of Tudor England whose work has covered monarchy, government, law, Thomas More and Mary Queen of Scots. His scholarship has been central to debates over whether Tudor government represented a major administrative revolution or developed more gradually from medieval institutions. On *The Tudor State* he brings political and legal evidence to that state-formation question.
