# Brian Butterworth

## Summary

Brian Butterworth is a cognitive neuroscientist known for research on numerical cognition, dyscalculia and the mental representation of number. His work asks which basic numerical capacities emerge early and how they differ from later formal mathematical education. On *Mathematics* he helps separate elementary quantity processing, learned symbolic skills, advanced mathematics and performance under particular educational conditions rather than treating them as one thing called “mathematical ability.”
