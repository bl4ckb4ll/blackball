# Valentine Cunningham

## Summary

On *Literary Modernism*, Valentine Cunningham places modernist works within literary history and tests claims of an absolute break with earlier writing. On *Oscar Wilde*, Valentine Cunningham places Wilde within late-Victorian literary culture and the later canon. The broadcast-era identification below is retained for provenance; it should not be read as a current affiliation.
