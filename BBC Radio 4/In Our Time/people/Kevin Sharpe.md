# Kevin Sharpe

## Summary

Kevin Sharpe was a historian of early modern England whose work covered politics, monarchy, print, representation and reading. He paid particular attention to the ways political authority was communicated and interpreted through texts and images. On *Reading* he brings the history of literacy and reading practices into contact with power: access to texts, censorship and ways of reading are themselves historically organized.
