# Gillian Beer

## Summary

Gillian Beer is a literary scholar known especially for work on the relation between nineteenth-century literature and science, including Darwin's influence on narrative and metaphor. Her major books include *Darwin's Plots*. On *The Novel* she brings literary history to questions about why the novel became such a flexible form for representing social and individual life and how literary forms respond to changes in knowledge and technology.
