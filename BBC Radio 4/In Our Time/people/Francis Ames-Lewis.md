# Francis Ames-Lewis

## Summary

Francis Ames-Lewis is an art historian specializing in the Italian Renaissance, especially drawing, workshops, patronage and artists' working practices. On *The Renaissance* he brings material evidence about how art was actually commissioned and produced, complicating a story that describes the period only through a few celebrated geniuses and a sudden discovery of individualism.
