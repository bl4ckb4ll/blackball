# Carole Hillenbrand

## Summary

On *Third Crusade*, Carole Hillenbrand uses Arabic sources to reconstruct Saladin’s world and Muslim responses to the crusade. The broadcast-era identification below is retained for provenance; it should not be read as a current affiliation.
