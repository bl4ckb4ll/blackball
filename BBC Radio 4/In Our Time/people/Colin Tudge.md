# Colin Tudge

## Summary

Colin Tudge is a British biologist and science writer whose books have covered evolution, genetics, agriculture, conservation and the future of food. He has often worked at the boundary between specialist science and its social implications. On *Evolution* he helps translate evolutionary biology into broader questions about progress and future technology while providing a useful point at which to watch for analogies that move beyond what the biology itself establishes.
