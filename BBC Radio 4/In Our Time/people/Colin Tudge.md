# Colin Tudge

## Summary

Colin Tudge is a British biologist and science writer whose books have covered evolution, genetics, agriculture, conservation and the future of food. He has often worked at the boundary between specialist science and its social implications. On *Evolution* he translates evolutionary biology into broader questions about progress and future technology; on *The Natural Order* he turns to taxonomy and the evidence behind biological classification. Both appearances provide useful points at which to separate biological findings from larger social analogies.
