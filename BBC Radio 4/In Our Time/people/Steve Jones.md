# Steve Jones

## Summary

Steve Jones is a British geneticist and science writer who has worked on population genetics and evolution and has written extensively against simplistic genetic determinism. He has been a prominent public interpreter of Darwin and modern genetics. On *Genetic Determinism* he helps distinguish genes that contribute causally to a trait from the much stronger claim that a complex outcome is fixed, predictable or independent of environment and development.
