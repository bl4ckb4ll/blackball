# Steve Jones

## Summary

Steve Jones is a British geneticist and science writer who has worked on population genetics and evolution and has written extensively against simplistic genetic determinism. On *Genetic Determinism* he distinguished genes that contribute causally to a trait from the much stronger claim that a complex outcome is fixed or independent of environment and development.

On *Genetics* he places Mendelian and population genetics within evolutionary history and again resists simple gene-for-trait stories. The recurring analytical rule is to state the causal claim precisely rather than sliding from heritability or association to inevitability.
