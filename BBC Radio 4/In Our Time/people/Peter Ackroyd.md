# Peter Ackroyd

## Summary

Peter Ackroyd is a British novelist, biographer and cultural historian whose work is strongly associated with London. His books include biographies of Dickens and Blake and the large cultural history *London: The Biography*. On *London* he treats the city almost as a historical character with recurring places, stories and patterns. For Blackball this literary-historical vision should be cross-checked against material measures of housing, work, migration, transport and municipal change.
