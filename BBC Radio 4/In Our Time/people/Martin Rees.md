# Martin Rees

## Summary

Martin Rees is a British astrophysicist and cosmologist whose research has covered quasars, galaxy formation, black holes and large-scale cosmology. He became Astronomer Royal and has held major scientific leadership roles. On *The Universe's Origins* he supplies the observational and theoretical cosmology needed to distinguish well-supported inferences about cosmic history from more speculative claims about beginnings, fine-tuning and other possible worlds.
