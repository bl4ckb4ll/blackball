# Martin Rees

## Summary

Martin Rees is a British astrophysicist and cosmologist whose research has covered quasars, galaxy formation, black holes and large-scale cosmology. On *The Universe's Origins* he distinguished well-supported inferences about cosmic history from more speculative claims about beginnings; on *Grand Unified Theory* he brought cosmological constraints to attempts to reconcile quantum theory and gravitation.

On *Black Holes* he places the objects within relativistic astrophysics, cosmology and galaxy formation. Across the appearances, observational consequence is kept separate from mathematical possibility and from broader metaphysical extrapolation.
