# Richard Holmes

## Summary

Richard Holmes is a British biographer known especially for lives of Romantic-era writers and scientists and for writing about the practice of biography itself. His books include major studies of Shelley and Coleridge. On *Biography* he brings long experience reconstructing lives from letters, journals, places and archives, while also making visible the interpretive choices through which fragments become a continuous narrative.
