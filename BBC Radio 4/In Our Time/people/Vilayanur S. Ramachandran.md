# Vilayanur S. Ramachandran

## Summary

Vilayanur S. Ramachandran is a neuroscientist known for behavioral neurology and for using unusual neurological cases to study perception, body representation and consciousness. His work on phantom limbs, neglect, visual perception and related disorders became especially influential because apparently bizarre symptoms can reveal components of ordinary cognition that normally operate invisibly.

In *Neuroscience in the 20th Century* his examples function like natural experiments. They are powerful evidence about mechanisms, but the inferential lesson is also one of restraint: discovering a mechanism involved in a mental capacity does not automatically settle every philosophical or social question about the person who has it.
