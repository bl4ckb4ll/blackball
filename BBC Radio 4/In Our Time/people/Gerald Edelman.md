# Gerald Edelman

## Summary

Gerald Edelman was an American scientist who received the Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine for work on the structure of antibodies and later developed theories of brain organization and consciousness known as neural Darwinism. On *Imagination and Consciousness* he brings neuroscience and a selection-based account of brain development to questions about subjective experience and creativity. His immunology Nobel does not itself validate the later consciousness theory; those evidential domains should remain separate.
