# Evelyn Welch

## Summary

Evelyn Welch is an art historian and historian of material culture whose research has examined Renaissance Italy through patronage, shopping, clothing, medicine and the circulation of objects. On *The Renaissance* she supplies evidence that cultural change is embedded in markets, workshops, households and institutions rather than generated only by famous artists or abstract ideas.
