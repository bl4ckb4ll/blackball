# Nancy Cartwright

## Summary

Nancy Cartwright is a philosopher of science known for challenging overly simple accounts of universal scientific laws and for emphasizing models, causal capacities and the restricted conditions under which scientific regularities hold. Her books include *How the Laws of Physics Lie*. On *Laws of Nature* she supplies the strongest methodological warning against assuming that a successful equation in one domain must describe all systems at all scales.
