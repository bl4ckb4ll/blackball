# Nigel Hamilton

## Summary

Nigel Hamilton is a British-American biographer and scholar of biography whose subjects have included military, political and cultural figures. He has also written explicitly about biography as a research method and literary form. On *Biography* he helps expose the institutional side of life-writing: archive access, source selection, authorization, reputation and the biographer's own framing all affect the finished account.
