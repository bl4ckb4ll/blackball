# Margaret Wertheim

## Summary

Margaret Wertheim is a science writer and cultural historian whose work examines how scientific concepts enter public imagination. On *Space in Religion and Science* she traced the cultural meanings attached to changing ideas of physical and spiritual space.

On *Mathematics and Platonism* she supplies the cultural history of Pythagorean and Platonic claims about number, order and the cosmos. Her contribution helps distinguish mathematics as a technical practice from the religious and metaphysical meanings historically attached to it.
