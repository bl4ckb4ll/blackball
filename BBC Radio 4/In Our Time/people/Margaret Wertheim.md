# Margaret Wertheim

## Summary

Margaret Wertheim is a science writer and cultural historian whose work examines how scientific concepts enter public imagination. Her book *The Pearly Gates of Cyberspace* traced changing Western ideas of space from medieval cosmology through modern physics to digital culture. On *Space in Religion and Science* she supplies the historical argument that scientific and technological concepts acquire cultural meanings that exceed their technical definitions.
