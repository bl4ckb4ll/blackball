# Mary Archer

## Summary

Mary Archer is a British scientist with a background in physical chemistry and solar-energy research. She has held academic and institutional roles connecting chemistry, energy and public science. On *Chemical Elements* she supplies practicing chemical expertise to a historical discussion of elements and the periodic table, helping distinguish the modern scientific concept of an element from earlier philosophical schemes of matter.
