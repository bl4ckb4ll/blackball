# Amartya Sen

## Summary

Amartya Sen is an economist and philosopher whose work transformed welfare economics, social choice, famine analysis and the measurement of well-being. He received the 1998 Nobel Memorial Prize in Economic Sciences. His capability approach asks what people are actually able to be and do rather than treating income, utility or formal rights as complete measures of freedom. On *Economic Rights* this supplies a direct framework for Blackball's distinction between being legally allowed to try and possessing the material means to reach the arena at all.
