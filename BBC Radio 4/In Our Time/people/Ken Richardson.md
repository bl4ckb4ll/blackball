# Ken Richardson

## Summary

Ken Richardson is a psychologist and writer known for criticism of simplistic models of intelligence, IQ testing and genetic explanations of cognitive differences. His work emphasizes development, social context and the difficulty of inferring a single fixed mental quantity from test performance. On *Intelligence* he is directly relevant to Blackball's IQ material because he attacks the movement from an operational test score to claims about innate hierarchy.
