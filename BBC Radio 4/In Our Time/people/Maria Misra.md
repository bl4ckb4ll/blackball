# Maria Misra

## Summary

On *Imperial Science*, Maria Misra connects scientific authority to the practical governance and political economy of empire, especially in India. On *The British Empire*, Maria Misra brings the history of British rule in India and the practical compromises of imperial governance. The broadcast-era identification below is retained for provenance; it should not be read as a current affiliation.
