# Richard J. Evans

## Summary

Richard J. Evans is a British historian specializing in modern Germany and historical method. On *History as Science* he emphasized documentary evidence, contingency, institutions and human action; on *History and Understanding the Past* he examined whether historical knowledge can explain the present without turning patterns into inevitabilities.

On *Money* he supplies modern European cases in which inflation, monetary institutions and political legitimacy interact. Later appearances should continue to normalize source variants such as “Richard Evans” and “Richard J Evans” into this dossier.
