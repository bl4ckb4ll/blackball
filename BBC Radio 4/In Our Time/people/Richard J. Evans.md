# Richard J. Evans

## Summary

Richard J. Evans is a British historian specializing in modern Germany, especially the nineteenth century, Nazism and the Third Reich. He has also written extensively on historical method and the defense of evidence-based historical scholarship. On *History as Science* he resists reducing historical outcomes to one general environmental model, emphasizing documentary evidence, contingency, institutions and human action. On *History and Understanding the Past* he addresses whether historical knowledge can explain the present or predict the future without turning patterns into inevitabilities.
