# Richard J. Evans

## Summary

Richard J. Evans is a British historian specializing in modern Germany, especially the nineteenth century, Nazism and the Third Reich. He has also written extensively on historical method and the defense of evidence-based historical scholarship. On *History as Science* he provides the professional historian's resistance to reducing historical outcomes to one general environmental model, emphasizing documentary evidence, contingency, institutions and human action.
