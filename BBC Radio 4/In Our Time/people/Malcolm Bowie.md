# Malcolm Bowie

## Summary

Malcolm Bowie was a British scholar of French literature and intellectual history known for work on Proust, Mallarmé, Lacan and psychoanalysis. On *Memory and Culture* he treated memory as an activity shaped by narrative, omission and later reinterpretation; on *Psychoanalysis and Literature* he addressed how psychoanalytic concepts travel into literary writing and criticism.

On *Surrealism* he connects the movement's literature to French intellectual history and psychoanalytic interpretation. The three appearances trace a recurring problem: concepts of memory and the unconscious change when they move among clinical, literary and artistic institutions.
