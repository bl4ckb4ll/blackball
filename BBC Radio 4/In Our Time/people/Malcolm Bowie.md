# Malcolm Bowie

## Summary

Malcolm Bowie was a British scholar of French literature and intellectual history known for work on Proust, Mallarmé, Lacan and psychoanalysis. His scholarship repeatedly addressed memory, interpretation and the relation between literary form and mental life. On *Memory and Culture* he contributes the literary and psychoanalytic side of collective remembrance: memory is not a passive storage device but an activity shaped by narrative, omission and later reinterpretation. On *Psychoanalysis and Literature* he addresses more directly how psychoanalytic concepts travel into literary writing and criticism.
