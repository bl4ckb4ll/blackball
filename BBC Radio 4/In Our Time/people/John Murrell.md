# John Murrell

## Summary

John Murrell was a British theoretical chemist whose research included molecular electronic structure and chemical bonding. He spent much of his academic career at the University of Sussex. On *Chemical Elements* he brings technical chemistry to the history of the periodic table, including the distinction between descriptive regularities and the later atomic theory that explains much of their structure.
