# John Gribbin

## Summary

John Gribbin is a science writer with a background in astrophysics who has written extensively for general audiences about cosmology, physics, astronomy and the history of science. On *Science in the 20th Century* he represented the explanatory achievements and internal development of modern science.

On *Quantum Gravity* he sets out the physical problem of reconciling gravitation with quantum theory and translates competing approaches for a general audience. His role is explanatory rather than evidence that any one research programme has already succeeded.
