# John Gribbin

## Summary

John Gribbin is a science writer with a background in astrophysics who has written extensively for general audiences about cosmology, physics, astronomy and the history of science. He has also been associated with the University of Sussex and *New Scientist*.

On *Science in the 20th Century* he represents the explanatory achievements and internal development of modern science. Paired with Mary Midgley, his contribution helps expose a useful boundary: successful physical explanation does not automatically license every philosophical or social claim attached to it.
