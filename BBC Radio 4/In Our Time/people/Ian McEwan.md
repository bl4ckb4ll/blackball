# Ian McEwan

## Summary

Ian McEwan is a British novelist whose fiction frequently uses problems from science, psychology, law and ethics as pressure points for character and moral judgment. His participation in *Science's Revelations* is not as a scientific authority but as a literary counterweight to Richard Dawkins.

He helps the episode ask what forms of understanding remain after a mechanism has been explained. That makes his contribution useful for distinguishing empirical explanation from the description of lived experience rather than treating literature as a rival laboratory science.
