# Steven Pinker

## Summary

Steven Pinker is a cognitive psychologist and psycholinguist known for work on language acquisition and for popular books including *The Language Instinct*. His research and public writing emphasize evolved cognitive capacities and the idea that human language depends on specialized mental structure. On *Language and the Mind* his claims are useful when kept at the right level: evidence for an evolved capacity does not by itself establish that particular languages, social meanings or inequalities are biologically fixed.
