# Nicholas Humphrey

## Summary

Nicholas Humphrey is a psychologist known for work on consciousness, social intelligence and the evolutionary development of mind. His research has included the idea that primate intelligence was shaped by the demands of social life. On *Evolutionary Psychology* he represents the constructive evolutionary-psychological side of the discussion, making his hypotheses useful to audit for distinctive predictions rather than merely plausible retrospective stories.
