# David Cannadine

## Summary

David Cannadine is a British historian whose work has examined class, aristocracy, empire, public ritual and the social construction of prestige. His books include *The Decline and Fall of the British Aristocracy* and *Ornamentalism*. On *The Monarchy* he treats royal ceremony historically, showing that rituals presented as immemorial traditions are often redesigned in response to political and social change.
