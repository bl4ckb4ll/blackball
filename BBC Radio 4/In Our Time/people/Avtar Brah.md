# Avtar Brah

## Summary

Avtar Brah is a sociologist and feminist scholar whose work focuses on diaspora, race, gender, class and migration. Her book *Cartographies of Diaspora* developed an influential account of how identities are made at intersections of migration histories and social power rather than inherited as isolated cultural packages. On *Multiculturalism* she brings that framework to the practical question of how migrants and their descendants negotiate belonging under unequal institutions.
