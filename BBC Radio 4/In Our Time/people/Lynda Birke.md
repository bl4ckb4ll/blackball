# Lynda Birke

## Summary

Lynda Birke is a biologist and scholar of human-animal relations whose work has crossed feminist science studies, animal behavior and ethics. She has examined how scientific institutions construct animals both as living beings and as research objects. On *Animal Experiments and Rights* she brings biological knowledge without accepting that scientific usefulness alone settles the moral status of experimentation.
