# Catherine Bates

## Summary

Catherine Bates is a literary scholar specializing in Renaissance and early modern literature, including lyric, court culture, masculinity and literary forms of self-representation. On *Metamorphosis* she provides literary-historical analysis of transformation as a recurring device and inherited classical form, complementing A. S. Byatt's perspective as a novelist.
