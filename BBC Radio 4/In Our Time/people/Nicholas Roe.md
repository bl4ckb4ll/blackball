# Nicholas Roe

## Summary

Nicholas Roe is a literary scholar and biographer specializing in Romantic-period writing, particularly John Keats, political radicalism and the historical geography of Romanticism. On *The Romantics* he contributes evidence about specific writers, circles and political contexts that complicate any single definition of the movement.
