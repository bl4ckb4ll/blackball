# Alan Clark

## Summary

Alan Clark was a British Conservative politician, military historian and diarist. Before and alongside his parliamentary career he wrote military history, including *The Donkeys*, and later a history of the Conservative Party. His diaries became another important source on the culture and personal relationships of late-twentieth-century British politics.

On *Politics in the 20th Century* he supplies a nationalist and Conservative view of the nation-state and political authority. For Blackball he is also a useful case where an intellectual account of political institutions comes from someone who directly occupied those institutions.
