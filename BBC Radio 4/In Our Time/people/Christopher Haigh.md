# Christopher Haigh

## Summary

Christopher Haigh is a historian of Tudor and Reformation England known for emphasizing local evidence, religious practice and the uneven reception of reform. His work challenges stories in which central policy immediately transforms society everywhere. On *The Tudor State* this makes him particularly useful for Blackball's distinction between a rule announced by authorities and the actual capacity to enforce or implement it across local institutions.
