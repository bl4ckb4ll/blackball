# Michael Howard

## Summary

Michael Howard was one of the major British historians of war and strategy in the twentieth century. He held the Regius Professorship of Modern History at Oxford and helped establish military history as a field that connects armies and battles to politics, institutions and society rather than treating combat as an isolated technical subject.

That breadth is what he brings to *War in the 20th Century*: war is not just weapons or tactics but a political and historical institution whose restraints, aims and social consequences change over time. In *New Wars* he applies the longer history of strategy to post-Cold War conflicts involving militias, international forces and civilian populations. The BBC credits him as “Sir Michael Howard”; the honorific is retained here as a source-name variant rather than as part of the dossier's stable name.
