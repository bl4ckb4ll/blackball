# A. N. Wilson

## Summary

A. N. Wilson is a British novelist, biographer and essayist whose nonfiction has included religious history, Victorian Britain and biographies of major literary and political figures. On *The Age of Doubt* he discussed the nineteenth-century crisis of Christian belief and its cultural consequences.

On *Gothic* he places supernatural fiction within those wider changes in religion and nineteenth-century culture. His contribution helps explain why apparently fantastic literary machinery could carry historically specific fears about faith, reason and social order.
