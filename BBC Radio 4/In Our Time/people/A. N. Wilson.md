# A. N. Wilson

## Summary

A. N. Wilson is a British novelist, biographer and essayist whose nonfiction has included religious history, Victorian Britain and biographies of major literary and political figures. His book *God's Funeral* examined the nineteenth-century crisis of Christian belief. On *The Age of Doubt* he brings that intellectual and literary history to the changing place of faith in British culture.
