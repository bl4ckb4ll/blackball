# Stephen Mulhall

## Summary

On *Evil*, Stephen Mulhall brings moral philosophy to questions of agency, intelligibility and whether some wrongdoing resists ordinary explanation. The broadcast-era identification below is retained for provenance; it should not be read as a current affiliation.
