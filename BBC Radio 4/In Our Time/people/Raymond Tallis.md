# Raymond Tallis

## Summary

Raymond Tallis is a British physician, neuroscientific critic, philosopher and writer. Trained in medicine and geriatric practice, he has argued against reducing human meaning, agency and culture to simplified accounts of brain activity or evolutionary adaptation. On *Nihilism* he brings both medical and philosophical perspectives to questions about meaning and naturalism, while his broader claims remain arguments to be evaluated rather than consequences of medical expertise.
