# Russell Stannard

## Summary

Russell Stannard was a British particle physicist and writer who also became known for explaining science to general audiences and for discussing Christian belief in relation to physics. On *Prayer* he represents a religious scientist's attempt to distinguish physical causation from religious practice rather than treating prayer as a simple rival to scientific explanation.
