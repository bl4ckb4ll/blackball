# Doreen Massey

## Summary

Doreen Massey was a geographer and social theorist whose work changed how scholars think about space and place. She argued against treating places as sealed local containers: a neighbourhood, city or region is made through relations extending outward through migration, labour, capital, politics and culture. Her work also examined uneven development and the geography of economic power.

That perspective makes her contribution to *The City in the 20th Century* particularly useful for Blackball. A city cannot be assessed only by its skyline or aggregate prosperity; one must ask which flows of labour and money sustain it, who can enter its valuable spaces, and who is displaced or immobilized.
