# Will Hutton

## Summary

Will Hutton is a British writer, journalist and political-economic commentator known especially for *The State We're In*, which criticized short-termism, inequality and weaknesses in British economic institutions. On *Economic Rights* he complements Amartya Sen's formal welfare framework with institutional arguments about ownership, employment, investment and the distribution of economic security. His claims should be assessed through concrete outcomes rather than treated as economic authority merely because the subject is the economy.
