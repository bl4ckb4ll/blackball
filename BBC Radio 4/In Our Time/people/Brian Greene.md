# Brian Greene

## Summary

Brian Greene is a theoretical physicist and science writer known for work on string theory and for explaining attempts to unify quantum theory and gravitation to general audiences. His book *The Elegant Universe* appeared shortly before his *In Our Time* appearance. On *Grand Unified Theory* he represents string theory at a period of high theoretical confidence, making the episode useful for tracking the difference between mathematical development, explanatory promise and direct empirical confirmation.
