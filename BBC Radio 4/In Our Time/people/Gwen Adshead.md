# Gwen Adshead

## Summary

Gwen Adshead is a forensic psychiatrist and psychotherapist whose career has included clinical work with people who committed serious violence, including at Broadmoor Hospital. Her work examines violence, attachment, responsibility and the psychological treatment of offenders. On *Atrocity in the 20th Century* she contributes direct clinical knowledge of violent behavior, providing an important distinction between explaining how violence becomes possible and excusing the person or institution that carries it out.
