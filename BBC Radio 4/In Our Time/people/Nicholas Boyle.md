# Nicholas Boyle

## Summary

Nicholas Boyle is a scholar of German literature and intellectual history best known for his multi-volume biography of Goethe. His work places Goethe's writing, politics, religion and scientific interests inside the institutions of eighteenth- and nineteenth-century German life. On *Goethe and the Science of the Enlightenment* he supplies the biographical and literary context needed to understand Goethe's scientific work without treating it as a detached curiosity.
