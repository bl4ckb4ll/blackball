# Paul Strathern

## Summary

Paul Strathern is a British writer whose books for general audiences have covered philosophy, science, scientists and intellectual history. On *Chemical Elements* he contributes the historical narrative of changing ideas about matter alongside two practicing chemists. His role is best treated as synthesis and exposition rather than primary chemical authority.
