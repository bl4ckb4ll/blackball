# Homi K. Bhabha

## Summary

Homi K. Bhabha is a literary and cultural theorist associated with postcolonial studies. His influential concepts include hybridity, mimicry, ambivalence and the “third space”: ways of describing colonial and postcolonial identities that do not fit a simple opposition between two self-contained cultures.

On *Cultural Rights in the 20th Century* this matters because the phrase “cultural rights” can falsely imply that every person belongs to one fixed culture with one authoritative spokesman. Bhabha's work complicates that picture and asks how identities are formed under unequal power and through contact, migration and translation.
