# Gore Vidal

## Summary

Gore Vidal was an American novelist, essayist and political critic. Much of his fiction and nonfiction returned to the history of the United States, elite political families, empire, constitutional government and the gap between American republican language and American power.

On *Politics in the 20th Century* he is useful less as a neutral historian than as an articulate critic of the political story the United States tells about itself. His contribution should therefore be read as argument and interpretation, not as uncontested empirical description.
