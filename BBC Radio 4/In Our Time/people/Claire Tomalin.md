# Claire Tomalin

## Summary

Claire Tomalin is a British biographer whose subjects include Mary Wollstonecraft, Samuel Pepys, Jane Austen, Charles Dickens and Thomas Hardy. Her work is especially attentive to private records, family relationships and the material circumstances surrounding literary lives. On *London* she contributes biographical history of people living inside the city rather than treating London only as architecture or metropolitan myth.
