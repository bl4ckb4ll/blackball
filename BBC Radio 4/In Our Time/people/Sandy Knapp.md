# Sandy Knapp

## Summary

Sandy Knapp is a botanist and taxonomist whose research has focused especially on the nightshade family and on the description and classification of plant diversity. She has held senior scientific roles at the Natural History Museum in London. On *The Natural Order* she supplies direct taxonomic practice: biological categories are revised through specimens, comparison and hypotheses about evolutionary relationships rather than inherited as fixed names.
