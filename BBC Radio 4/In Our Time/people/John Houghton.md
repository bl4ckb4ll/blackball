# John Houghton

## Summary

John Houghton was a British atmospheric physicist who led the UK Met Office and co-chaired the scientific assessment working group of the Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change. His career put him at the junction of atmospheric measurement, climate modeling and international scientific assessment. On *Climate Change* he supplies the physical-science basis for distinguishing established greenhouse mechanisms from the uncertainty ranges attached to particular forecasts.
