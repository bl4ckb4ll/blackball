# Marilyn Butler

## Summary

Marilyn Butler was a British literary scholar specializing in Romantic-period literature and the historical contexts in which literary movements are formed. Her work included studies of Jane Austen, Maria Edgeworth and Romanticism. On *Death* she brings literary-historical evidence about changing representations of mortality and mourning, complementing Thomas Lynch's direct experience of funeral work and Jonathan Dollimore's cultural theory.
