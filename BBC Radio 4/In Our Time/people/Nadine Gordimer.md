# Nadine Gordimer

## Summary

Nadine Gordimer was a South African novelist and Nobel laureate whose fiction and essays confronted apartheid, censorship, race, political violence and the moral compromises produced by unequal societies. Several of her works were banned under apartheid. On *Writing and Political Oppression* she therefore speaks from direct experience of writing inside a repressive state rather than treating censorship as an abstract literary problem.
