# Jonathan Glover

## Summary

Jonathan Glover is a British moral philosopher whose work has addressed war, violence, bioethics and the psychological conditions of cruelty. His book *Humanity: A Moral History of the Twentieth Century* examines how moral restraints break down in war, genocide and political terror. On *Atrocity in the 20th Century* he links philosophical responsibility to the institutional and psychological mechanisms through which large-scale violence becomes possible.
