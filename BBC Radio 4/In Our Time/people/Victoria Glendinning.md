# Victoria Glendinning

## Summary

Victoria Glendinning is a British biographer, novelist and critic whose biographies have covered writers and public figures including Anthony Trollope, Vita Sackville-West and Leonard Woolf. On *The Age of Doubt* she brings biographical and literary evidence to the history of religious belief and unbelief, grounding broad intellectual change in particular lives and texts.
