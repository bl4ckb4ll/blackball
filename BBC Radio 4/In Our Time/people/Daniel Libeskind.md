# Daniel Libeskind

## Summary

Daniel Libeskind is an architect known for buildings that make historical memory and symbolic form explicit, including the Jewish Museum Berlin and later the master plan for the World Trade Center site. On *Architecture in the 20th Century* he speaks as a practicing architect working on major cultural institutions. His appearance is useful for examining architecture simultaneously as art, public expenditure, memorial practice and the exercise of commissioning power.
