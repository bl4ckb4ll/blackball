# Edward Luttwak

## Summary

Edward Luttwak is a strategist and writer on military affairs, geopolitics and political economy. His book *Turbo-Capitalism* criticized forms of global market competition that increase insecurity and social disruption even while generating economic dynamism. On *Capitalism* he is therefore a critic from within broadly pro-market politics rather than a Marxist opponent of markets, making the distribution of risk and insecurity central to his contribution.
