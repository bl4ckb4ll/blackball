# Francis Fukuyama

## Summary

Francis Fukuyama is an American political scientist best known for *The End of History and the Last Man* and later *The Great Disruption*. His work has ranged from regime types and state capacity to social trust and institutional development. On *The Great Disruption* he argues that the transition to an information economy coincided with broad social disorder. For Blackball his large claims are most useful when decomposed into separate measurable outcomes rather than accepted as a single index of social health.
