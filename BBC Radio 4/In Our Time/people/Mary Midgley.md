# Mary Midgley

## Summary

Mary Midgley was a British moral philosopher known for writing against reductionism, scientism and simplistic accounts of human beings derived from a single discipline. She wrote about ethics, animals, evolution, human nature and the metaphors through which scientific ideas enter public argument.

Her role in *Science in the 20th Century* is therefore methodological as much as philosophical. She asks when a scientific finding is being used legitimately and when it has been inflated into a complete account of value, motive or society. This distinction is directly relevant to Blackball's treatment of IQ, biological explanation and expert claims.
