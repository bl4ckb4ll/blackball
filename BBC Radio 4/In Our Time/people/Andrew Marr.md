# Andrew Marr

## Summary

Andrew Marr is a British journalist, broadcaster and political writer who edited *The Independent* before becoming a prominent BBC political presenter. His work has focused on British government, political parties and the media. On *The Nation State* he supplies a contemporary political-journalistic view of devolution and British identity alongside Norman Davies's longer historical account.
