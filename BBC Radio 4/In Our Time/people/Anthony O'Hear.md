# Anthony O'Hear

## Summary

Anthony O'Hear is a British philosopher whose work has covered philosophy of science, education, culture and political thought. He has been skeptical of strong narratives of social and moral progress and has written on the philosophical implications of Darwinism. On *Progress* he supplies the explicit critique of the assumption that more science, technology or material production necessarily means a better society.
