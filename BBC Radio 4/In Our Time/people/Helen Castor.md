# Helen Castor

## Summary

Helen Castor is a historian of medieval England whose work has focused on monarchy, political power and the lives of prominent women in the Middle Ages. Her later books include *Blood and Roses* and *She-Wolves*. On *The Wars of the Roses* she brings documentary political history to a subject heavily shaped in popular memory by Tudor narratives and Shakespeare.
