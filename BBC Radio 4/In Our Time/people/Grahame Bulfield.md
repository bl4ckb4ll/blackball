# Grahame Bulfield

## Summary

Grahame Bulfield is a geneticist and research administrator who led the Roslin Institute during the period immediately following the cloning of Dolly the sheep. His career places him close to the institutional development of animal genetics and biotechnology. On *Genetic Engineering* he contributes the research-practice perspective: what genetic manipulation could actually do in 1999, how scientists understood its limits, and how experimental capability differed from public speculation about engineered humans.
