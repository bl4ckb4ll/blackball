# Jones Irwin

## Summary

On *Evil*, Jones Irwin uses Nietzsche and continental philosophy to examine the genealogy of evil and the possibility of affirming life beyond inherited moral categories. The broadcast-era identification below is retained for provenance; it should not be read as a current affiliation.

## Broadcast-era identification

- 3 May 2001 — *Evil*: Lecturer in Philosophy at the University of Limerick.

## Name normalization

The episode credit is rendered as “Jones Erwin” in the source table. The broadcast-era University of Limerick philosopher is Jones Irwin; the erroneous form is retained here as provenance rather than made into a second person.
