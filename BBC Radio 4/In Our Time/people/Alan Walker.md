# Alan Walker

## Summary

Alan Walker is a social policy scholar and social gerontologist whose research has focused on ageing, pensions, inequality, employment and the social conditions of later life. He has also advised international work on ageing. On *Ageing* he supplies the institutional half of the subject: biological ageing does not determine income, retirement rules, access to care or social participation; those are produced by policy and labour-market arrangements.
