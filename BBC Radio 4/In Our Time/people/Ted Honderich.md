# Ted Honderich

## Summary

Ted Honderich is a philosopher known for work on consciousness, determinism, political philosophy and the relation between mind and brain. He held the Grote Professorship at University College London. On *Consciousness* he frames the philosophical problem that remains after neural correlates are identified: what exactly a conscious state is, and what kind of explanation would count as explaining subjective experience rather than merely its physical conditions.
