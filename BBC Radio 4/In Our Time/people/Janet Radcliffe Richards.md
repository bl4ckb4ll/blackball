# Janet Radcliffe Richards

## Summary

Janet Radcliffe Richards is a philosopher and bioethicist whose work has addressed feminism, equality, genetics, organ transplantation and the logic of moral argument. On *Evolutionary Psychology* she provides a philosophical check on moves from claims about evolved behavior to conclusions about what social arrangements are natural, desirable or morally justified. Even a correct evolutionary explanation would not by itself establish a normative rule.
