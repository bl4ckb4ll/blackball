# Christina Hardyment

## Summary

Christina Hardyment is a British social historian and writer whose books have examined domestic life, family, childhood, child-rearing and the material organization of households. Her work often uses ordinary practices and objects to recover forms of social history missed by institutional narratives. On *Childhood* she brings that historical evidence to the claim that childhood changed materially as work, schooling, household size and family expectations changed.
