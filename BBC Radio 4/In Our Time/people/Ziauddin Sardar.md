# Ziauddin Sardar

## Summary

On *Imperial Science*, Ziauddin Sardar provides a postcolonial critique of who defines valid knowledge and who bears the consequences of imperial science. The broadcast-era identification below is retained for provenance; it should not be read as a current affiliation.
