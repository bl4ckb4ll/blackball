# Adam Phillips

## Summary

Adam Phillips is a British psychoanalyst, essayist and former child psychotherapist known for literary writing about psychoanalysis rather than for presenting it as a laboratory science. His books include *On Kissing, Tickling and Being Bored* and *Darwin's Worms*. Across his two 1999 appearances he discusses both Freud's interpretive legacy and the broader idea of progress, making him useful for examining how psychological theories become cultural vocabularies as well as clinical claims.
