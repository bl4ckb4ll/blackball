# Adam Phillips

## Summary

Adam Phillips is a British psychoanalyst, essayist and former child psychotherapist known for literary writing about psychoanalysis rather than for presenting it as a laboratory science. His books include *On Kissing, Tickling and Being Bored* and *Darwin's Worms*. Across his three appearances through 2000 he discusses Freud's intellectual and clinical legacy, the broader idea of progress, and psychoanalysis as a vocabulary for writing and reading literature. Together the episodes make him useful for examining how a psychological theory becomes a cultural and literary framework as well as a clinical claim.
