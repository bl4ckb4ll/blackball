# Helena Cronin

## Summary

Helena Cronin is a philosopher of biology whose work focuses on Darwinian explanation, sex differences, cooperation and competition. She co-founded the Centre for Philosophy of Natural and Social Sciences at the London School of Economics and wrote *The Ant and the Peacock* on altruism and sexual selection. Her *In Our Time* contribution brings evolutionary theory into a debate about feminism, making the distinction between claims about biological variation and claims about rights or social roles especially important.
