# John Searle

## Summary

John Searle is an American philosopher known for work on language, intentionality, social ontology and philosophy of mind. His “Chinese room” argument challenges the claim that executing a formal program is sufficient for genuine understanding. On *Artificial Intelligence* he supplies the strongest distinction between successful symbol manipulation or outward performance and consciousness or semantic understanding, a distinction that remains directly relevant to modern language-model claims.
