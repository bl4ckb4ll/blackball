# Matt Ridley

## Summary

Matt Ridley is a British science writer whose books have popularized genetics, evolution and later arguments about markets and technological innovation. *Genome* appeared in 1999 and organized discussion of human genetics around the chromosomes. On *Genetic Determinism* he represents a gene-centered explanatory style, but the episode also gives a basis for separating useful genetic explanation from claims that genes uniquely determine complex behavior or social outcomes.
