# Eric Hobsbawm

## Summary

Eric Hobsbawm was a Marxist historian whose large-scale histories of the nineteenth and twentieth centuries emphasized capitalism, class, revolution, nationalism and social transformation. His books include *The Age of Revolution*, *The Age of Capital*, *The Age of Empire* and *Age of Extremes*. On the avant-garde episode he turns that social-historical method toward modern art, judging artistic movements partly through their relationship to the society and institutions around them.
