# Stuart Hall

## Summary

Stuart Hall was a Jamaican-born British cultural theorist and a central figure in cultural studies, especially through the Centre for Contemporary Cultural Studies and the Open University. His work examined race, media, ideology, identity, class and the political construction of common sense. On *Multiculturalism* he argues against treating national or cultural identities as fixed essences, emphasizing their production through history, migration and unequal power.
