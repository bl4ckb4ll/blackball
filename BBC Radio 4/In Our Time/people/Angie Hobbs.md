# Angie Hobbs

## Summary

On *The Philosophy of Love*, Angie Hobbs brings ancient Greek treatments of eros, friendship and the good life to the discussion. The broadcast-era identification below is retained for provenance; it should not be read as a current affiliation.

One source renders the name as “Angie Hobbes”; the philosopher's documented name is Angie Hobbs.
