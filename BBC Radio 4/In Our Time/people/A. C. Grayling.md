# A. C. Grayling

## Summary

A. C. Grayling is a British philosopher and public writer whose work has covered ethics, humanism, epistemology, liberty and the history of philosophy. He has written extensively for general audiences as well as in academic philosophy. On *Utopia* he brings a liberal philosophical perspective to the problem of ideal societies: political imagination can expose defects in existing institutions, but a single perfected blueprint can become coercive when disagreement is treated as error.
