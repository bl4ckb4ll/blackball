# A. C. Grayling

## Summary

A. C. Grayling is a British philosopher and public writer whose work has covered ethics, humanism, epistemology, liberty and the history of philosophy. On *Utopia* he brought a liberal philosophical perspective to ideal societies, including the danger that a single perfected blueprint can become coercive when disagreement is treated as error.

On *Existentialism* he sets out the movement's central claims and tests them from a broader analytic perspective. The two appearances connect political ideals to questions of freedom, responsibility and the conditions under which a person gives a life meaning.
