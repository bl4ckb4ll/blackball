# Charlotte Roueché

## Summary

On *The Roman Empire's Collapse in the 5th century*, Charlotte Roueché uses late-antique evidence, including inscriptions and the eastern Mediterranean, to show institutional continuity as well as rupture. On *Byzantium*, Charlotte Roueché uses inscriptions, texts and the social history of late antiquity to connect Byzantium to the Roman world. The broadcast-era identification below is retained for provenance; it should not be read as a current affiliation.
