# Henry Gee

## Summary

Henry Gee is a paleontologist, evolutionary writer and long-time editor at *Nature*. His work has emphasized cladistics and the limits of reconstructing smooth ancestor-to-descendant stories from a fragmentary fossil record. On *The Natural Order* he brings evolutionary classification and paleontological evidence to the problem of biological ordering, making him especially useful against ladder-like narratives that turn branching evolution into a hierarchy of progress.
