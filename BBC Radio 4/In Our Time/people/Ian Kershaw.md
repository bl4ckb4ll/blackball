# Ian Kershaw

## Summary

Ian Kershaw is a historian of Nazi Germany whose major biography of Hitler and work on popular opinion, dictatorship and the Nazi state made him one of the central historians of the field. His formulation of officials “working towards the Führer” describes a system in which subordinates radicalized policy by anticipating a leader's wishes rather than waiting for a detailed order for every act. On *Hitler in History* this is directly relevant to Blackball's delegated-coercion framework.
