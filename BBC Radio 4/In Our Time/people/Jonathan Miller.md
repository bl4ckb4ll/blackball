# Jonathan Miller

## Summary

Jonathan Miller was a British physician, theatre and opera director, writer and broadcaster. His unusual career crossed medicine, cognitive questions and the performing arts, and he repeatedly made technical ideas accessible without staying inside a single academic discipline. On *Language and the Mind* he provides a medical and humanistic counterpoint to Steven Pinker's cognitive-scientific account of language.
