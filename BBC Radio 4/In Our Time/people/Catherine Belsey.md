# Catherine Belsey

## Summary

Catherine Belsey was a British literary theorist associated with poststructuralist criticism and with work on Shakespeare, desire, culture and the history of the subject. Her influential *Critical Practice* challenged approaches that treat literary meaning as transparent or independent of ideology and language. On *Tragedy* she offers a historical and theoretical counterpoint to George Steiner's account of the genre; on *Nihilism* she helps distinguish challenges to inherited foundations of truth and morality from the claim that nothing matters.
