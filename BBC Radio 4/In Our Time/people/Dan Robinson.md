# Dan Robinson

## Summary

Daniel N. Robinson was a philosopher and historian of psychology whose work ranged across philosophy of mind, ethics, law and the intellectual history of psychology. He taught at Georgetown and Oxford and was particularly interested in how modern psychological concepts grew out of older philosophical disputes.

On *The Brain and Consciousness* he provides the philosophical and historical complement to Steven Rose's neurobiology. His role is useful whenever a neuroscientific mechanism is being asked to carry conclusions about personhood, agency or responsibility that require additional premises.
