# Mary Fulbrook

## Summary

Mary Fulbrook is a historian of modern Germany whose work has covered the German Democratic Republic, Nazi society, dictatorship, memory and the everyday participation of ordinary people in political systems. On *Hitler in History* she helps shift attention from Hitler alone to German institutions and social life under Nazism, including the difficult question of how large numbers of people accommodated, benefited from, resisted or participated in the regime.
