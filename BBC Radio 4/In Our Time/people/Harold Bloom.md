# Harold Bloom

## Summary

Harold Bloom was an American literary critic known for *The Anxiety of Influence*, *The Western Canon* and a strongly author-centered defense of major literary works. Shakespeare occupied a particularly central place in his account of literary value and human character. On *Shakespeare and Literary Criticism* he gives a forceful universalist defense of Shakespeare, making his claims useful to compare against institutional histories of canon formation and against other critical methods.
