# Lisa Appignanesi

## Summary

Lisa Appignanesi is a writer, critic and cultural historian whose work has covered literature, psychoanalysis, women, memory and intellectual history. With John Forrester she wrote *Freud's Women*, examining women as patients, thinkers and participants in the history of psychoanalysis. On *Psychoanalysis and Literature* she brings that historical context to the movement of psychoanalytic concepts between clinic, biography and literary interpretation.
