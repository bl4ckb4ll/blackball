# Christopher Hitchens

## Summary

Christopher Hitchens was a British-American essayist and journalist whose work ranged across politics, war, religion, literature and American foreign policy. He was a polemical writer rather than a neutral historian, and his political positions changed substantially over his career. On *The American Ideal* he contributes an argument about American republican ideals and power that should be treated as interpretive political criticism and checked against institutional evidence.
