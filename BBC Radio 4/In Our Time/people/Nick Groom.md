# Nick Groom

## Summary

Nick Groom is a scholar of English literature and cultural history whose work has included textual editing, literary forgery, authenticity and the history of the English imagination. On *Truth, Lies and Fiction* he helps place the Wilkomirski controversy inside a longer history of forged and falsely attributed texts. That makes his contribution useful for distinguishing textual value from provenance: a text can be powerful while its false source claim remains an evidential failure.
