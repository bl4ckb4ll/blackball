# Mark Buchanan

## Summary

Mark Buchanan is a physicist and science writer whose work has popularized ideas from complex systems, statistical physics and network science. His book *Ubiquity* used concepts such as criticality to discuss recurring patterns across physical and social systems. On *Laws of Nature* he contributes the complex-systems side of the debate. For Blackball, analogies from physics to society should be treated as hypotheses requiring independent social evidence, not as laws transferred by metaphor.
