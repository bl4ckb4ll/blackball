# Henry Louis Gates Jr.

## Summary

Henry Louis Gates Jr. is an American literary scholar and historian of African and African-American culture. His work has ranged from African-American literary traditions and archival recovery to public history and genealogy, and he became a major institution-builder in Black studies at Harvard. On *Africa* he challenges inherited Western representations of the continent and brings attention to African intellectual and cultural histories obscured by colonial categories.
