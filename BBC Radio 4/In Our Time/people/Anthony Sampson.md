# Anthony Sampson

## Summary

Anthony Sampson was a British journalist and author best known for *Anatomy of Britain* and for extensive reporting on institutions of power. He had a long connection with South Africa, including work on *Drum* magazine in Johannesburg and a biography of Nelson Mandela. On *Africa* he brings journalistic experience of colonial and apartheid-era institutions while also representing the outsider perspective the episode itself asks listeners to scrutinize.
