# Richard Wollheim

## Summary

Richard Wollheim was a British philosopher known for work on philosophy of mind, psychoanalysis, aesthetics and painting. He treated persons as psychologically complex agents whose inner lives cannot be reduced to simple behavioral descriptions. On *The Individual* he contributes philosophical history to the question of whether modern individuality is a timeless human fact or a concept shaped by changing cultural and intellectual institutions.
