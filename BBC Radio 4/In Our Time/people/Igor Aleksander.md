# Igor Aleksander

## Summary

Igor Aleksander is an engineer and researcher in artificial intelligence and neural systems, particularly machine consciousness and neural-network architectures. He held a chair at Imperial College London and developed systems intended to model aspects of perception and internal representation. In both *Artificial Intelligence* and *Imagination and Consciousness* he represents the constructive engineering side of the debate: instead of asking only what machines cannot do, he asks what architectures might instantiate capacities associated with consciousness. The two appearances are useful for keeping novel output, internal representation and subjective experience as separate claims.
