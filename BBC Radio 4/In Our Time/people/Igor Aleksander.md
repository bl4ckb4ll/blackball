# Igor Aleksander

## Summary

Igor Aleksander is an engineer and researcher in artificial intelligence and neural systems, particularly machine consciousness and neural-network architectures. He held a chair at Imperial College London and developed systems intended to model aspects of perception and internal representation. On the 1999 *Artificial Intelligence* episode he represents the constructive engineering side of the debate: instead of asking only what machines cannot do, he asks what architectures might instantiate capacities associated with consciousness.
