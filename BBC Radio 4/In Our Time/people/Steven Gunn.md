# Steven Gunn

## Summary

Steven Gunn is a historian of late-medieval and Tudor England whose work has examined court politics, warfare, state formation and the transition from the fifteenth to the sixteenth century. On *The Wars of the Roses* he supplies evidence across the conventional boundary between Yorkist conflict and Tudor rule, making him useful for testing claims that a new dynasty produced an immediate new state.
