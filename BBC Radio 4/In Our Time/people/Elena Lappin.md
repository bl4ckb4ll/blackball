# Elena Lappin

## Summary

Elena Lappin is a writer, editor and journalist whose 1999 investigative essay in *Granta* played an important role in examining the truth claims surrounding Binjamin Wilkomirski's supposed Holocaust memoir *Fragments*. On *Truth, Lies and Fiction* she therefore brings reporting and source-checking directly into a literary discussion. Her role is important for Blackball's provenance rule: the emotional force of a narrative is not evidence that its claimed autobiographical source is genuine.
