# Jared Diamond

## Summary

Jared Diamond is a geographer and physiologist whose book *Guns, Germs, and Steel* argued that large differences in historical development can be explained substantially through geography, domesticable plants and animals, disease environments and diffusion rather than through innate differences among peoples. On *History as Science* he presents the case for broad comparative causal models. The Blackball value is double: his framework rejects racial-biological explanations, but any structural model still needs auditing for determinism and omitted institutions.
