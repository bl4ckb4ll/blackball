# Arthur I. Miller

## Summary

Arthur I. Miller is a historian and philosopher of science whose books examine creativity in physics, mathematics and art, including work on Einstein, Picasso and twentieth-century scientific imagination. On *Inspiration and Genius* he uses historical cases to ask how exceptional ideas arise. For Blackball, famous-case evidence must be paired with selection analysis: studying people who became canonical cannot reveal how many comparably capable people lacked training, resources or entry to the relevant institutions.
