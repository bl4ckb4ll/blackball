# John Carey

## Summary

John Carey is a British literary critic and scholar known for work on English literature and for arguments about high culture, mass culture and literary value. On *Utopia* he contributed the literary history of imagined ideal societies and skepticism toward elite schemes that define improvement on behalf of other people.

On *Literary Modernism* he brings his critique of modernist hostility toward mass culture and ordinary readers. The appearance is useful for distinguishing formal innovation from the social claims made by writers and critics about who is competent to judge culture.
