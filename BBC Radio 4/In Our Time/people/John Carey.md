# John Carey

## Summary

John Carey is a British literary critic and scholar known for work on English literature and for arguments about high culture, mass culture and literary value. His books include *The Intellectuals and the Masses*. On *Utopia* he contributes the literary history of imagined ideal societies while also bringing a critic's skepticism toward elite schemes that define improvement on behalf of other people.
