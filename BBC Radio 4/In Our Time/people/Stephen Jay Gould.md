# Stephen Jay Gould

## Summary

On *Science and Religion*, Stephen Jay Gould argues from evolutionary science and considers the proposal that science and religion occupy non-overlapping domains. The broadcast-era identification below is retained for provenance; it should not be read as a current affiliation.
