# Leslie Aiello

## Summary

Leslie Aiello is a biological anthropologist known for research on human evolution, anatomy and energetics. Her work has examined changes in body size, brain size, diet and life history across hominin evolution. On *Human Origins* she supplies anatomical and evolutionary evidence that can be combined with archaeology without collapsing distinct forms of evidence into one smooth story of inevitable human progress.
