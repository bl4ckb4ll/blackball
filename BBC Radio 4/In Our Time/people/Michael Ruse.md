# Michael Ruse

## Summary

Michael Ruse is a philosopher and historian of biology whose work has focused on Darwinism, evolutionary theory, creationism and the philosophical implications of biology. He has written both defenses of evolutionary science and critiques of attempts to make evolution carry conclusions it does not establish. On *Intelligence* he brings historical knowledge of heredity, eugenics and evolutionary arguments into the debate over IQ.
