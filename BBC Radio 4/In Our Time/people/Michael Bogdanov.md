# Michael Bogdanov

## Summary

Michael Bogdanov was a British theatre director known especially for Shakespeare and for co-founding the English Shakespeare Company. His productions often emphasized the political and social force of the plays in performance. On *Shakespeare's Work* he supplies the practical theatre perspective: written text becomes timing, bodies, staging, labor, interpretation and audience response rather than remaining only an object of literary scholarship.
