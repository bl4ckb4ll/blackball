# Nancy Wood

## Summary

Nancy Wood is a scholar of memory, media and cultural studies whose work has examined how societies remember political violence and historical trauma. She has written on memory in relation to France, the Holocaust and public commemoration. On *Memory and Culture* she focuses attention on institutions and media that stabilize some memories and marginalize others, making collective memory something that can be studied through archives, ceremonies, images and public narratives.
