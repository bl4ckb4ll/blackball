# Linda Colley

## Summary

Linda Colley is a historian of Britain, nationalism, empire and state formation. Her work examines how British national identity was made historically through war, Protestantism, empire and relations with continental Europe rather than simply inherited.

In *The British Empire's Legacy* she supplied a long view of state formation and imperial expansion. On *The Enlightenment in Britain* she connects Enlightenment thought to the same institutions of war, religion, state power and empire, preventing intellectual history from floating free of its material and political setting.
