# Linda Colley

## Summary

Linda Colley is a historian of Britain, nationalism, empire and state formation. Her best-known work, *Britons: Forging the Nation 1707–1837*, examines how a British national identity was made historically rather than simply inherited, with war, Protestantism, empire and relations with continental Europe playing major roles.

In *The British Empire's Legacy* she supplies a long view of how British identity and the British state were built alongside imperial expansion. For Blackball this helps prevent “empire” from becoming a morality tale detached from institutions: the relevant questions include taxation, military power, trade, political incorporation, national identity and the material arrangements that survive formal decolonization.
