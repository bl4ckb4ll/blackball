# John Keane

## Summary

John Keane is a political theorist known for work on democracy, civil society, violence and the history of representative institutions. He founded the Centre for the Study of Democracy at the University of Westminster and later developed the idea of “monitory democracy.” On *Just War* he approaches war through political institutions and democratic accountability: who possesses legitimate authority to initiate violence and what mechanisms can actually constrain that authority?
