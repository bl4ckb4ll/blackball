# John Keane

## Summary

John Keane is a political theorist known for work on democracy, civil society, violence and the history of representative institutions. He founded the Centre for the Study of Democracy at the University of Westminster and later developed the idea of “monitory democracy.” On *Just War* he asks who possesses legitimate authority to initiate violence and what mechanisms can constrain it. On *The American Ideal* he examines the gap between democratic national self-description and the institutions through which American power is exercised.
