# Sheila McLean

## Summary

Sheila McLean was a Scottish scholar of medical law and ethics who founded and directed the Institute of Law and Ethics in Medicine at the University of Glasgow. Her work covered consent, reproductive medicine, genetics, death and professional responsibility. On *Medical Ethics* she brings the legal and ethical constraints that sit outside technical medical competence: knowing how to perform an intervention does not answer whether a patient has consented or whether the intervention is justified.
