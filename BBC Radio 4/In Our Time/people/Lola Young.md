# Lola Young

## Summary

Lola Young is a British cultural scholar and public figure whose work has addressed race, representation, museums, archives and the histories of slavery and empire. She later entered the House of Lords as Baroness Young of Hornsey. On *Englishness* she provides a necessary challenge to national histories that allow a narrow part of the population to stand for the whole, bringing migration, Black British history and imperial connections into the discussion.
