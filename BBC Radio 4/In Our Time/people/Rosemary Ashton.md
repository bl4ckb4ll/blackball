# Rosemary Ashton

## Summary

Rosemary Ashton is a scholar of nineteenth-century literature and intellectual history whose work has examined German influence on British culture, George Eliot and the literary life of London. On *The Romantics* she placed British Romantic writing within wider European intellectual networks.

On *Dickens* she situates the novelist within nineteenth-century intellectual life, reform debates and metropolitan culture. Her appearances resist literary histories that isolate texts from international exchange and urban institutions.
