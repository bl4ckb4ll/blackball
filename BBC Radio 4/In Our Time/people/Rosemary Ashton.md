# Rosemary Ashton

## Summary

Rosemary Ashton is a scholar of nineteenth-century literature and intellectual history whose work has examined German influence on British culture, George Eliot and the literary life of London. On *The Romantics* she places British Romantic writing within wider European intellectual networks, useful for countering national literary histories that make movements appear to arise inside sealed countries.
