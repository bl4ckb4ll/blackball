# Charles Leadbeater

## Summary

Charles Leadbeater is a British writer and policy adviser known for work on innovation, the knowledge economy and social entrepreneurship. In the late 1990s he was closely associated with arguments that networked information and knowledge work would transform firms and public institutions. His *Information Technology* appearance is useful as a dated forecast source: claims about empowerment, flexible work and a new economy can be compared directly with later labor-market and institutional outcomes.
