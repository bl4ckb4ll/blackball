# John Polkinghorne

## Summary

John Polkinghorne was a British mathematical physicist who later became an Anglican priest and one of the best-known writers on relations between science and religion. His physics career involved particle theory before he turned to theology. On *Space in Religion and Science* his significance lies precisely in crossing those domains while still requiring their claims to be distinguished: a physical theory of space and a theological interpretation of creation answer different kinds of questions.
