# Peter Burke

## Summary

Peter Burke is a cultural historian whose work has ranged across the Renaissance, popular culture, knowledge, language and the history of communication. He is known for comparing social practices across Europe rather than treating elite texts alone as the history of culture. On *The Renaissance* he helps test the classic idea of a sharp birth of modernity against regional variation and continuities with medieval institutions.
