# Vitali Vitaliev

## Summary

Vitali Vitaliev was a Ukrainian-born journalist and writer who worked in the Soviet Union before emigrating and writing for British and international publications. His journalism and memoiristic writing gave him direct experience of late Soviet institutions distinct from Robert Service's archival historical perspective. On *Lenin* he helps connect arguments about revolutionary ideology to the later society built under institutions that claimed Lenin's authority.
