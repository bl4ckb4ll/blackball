# Michael Ignatieff

## Summary

Michael Ignatieff is a historian, writer and political thinker whose work has concentrated on nationalism, human rights, liberalism, political violence and the obligations created by war. He also wrote a major biography of Isaiah Berlin. His later career crossed academia and electoral politics, making him useful for Blackball as someone who has written about political responsibility from both intellectual and institutional positions.

In the first *In Our Time*, his role is primarily moral and political: what does knowledge of distant suffering require of people who are not themselves on the battlefield, and how did twentieth-century human-rights language change the way war could be defended or condemned?
