# Neil Johnson

## Summary

Neil Johnson is a theoretical physicist whose research has ranged across complex systems and collective behavior. At the time of *Time* he was working at Oxford's Clarendon Laboratory. On the episode he contributes the modern physics needed to show why everyday intuitions about universal time fail under relativity and why a precisely measured quantity can acquire a different theoretical meaning as physical theory changes.
