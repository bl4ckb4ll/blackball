# Andrew Roberts

## Summary

Andrew Roberts is a British historian and biographer whose work has concentrated on British political and military history, including Churchill, Napoleon and the Second World War. On *Republicanism* he contributed a political-historical perspective on monarchy and the seventeenth-century republican experiment.

In 2001 he returned twice: *The French Revolution's Legacy* connects revolution to war, Napoleon, conservative reaction and the nineteenth-century European order, while *Napoleon and Wellington* compares command, personality and the later construction of military reputation.
