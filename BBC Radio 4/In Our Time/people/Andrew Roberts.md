# Andrew Roberts

## Summary

Andrew Roberts is a British historian and biographer whose work has concentrated on British political and military history, including Churchill, Napoleon and the Second World War. On *Republicanism* he brings a broader political-historical perspective to the British monarchy and the seventeenth-century republican experiment.
