# Bea Campbell

## Summary

Bea Campbell is a British writer, journalist and feminist political activist whose work has addressed class, gender, violence, local government and the British state. She has often analyzed institutions from the standpoint of people affected by their policies rather than from ceremonial or constitutional self-description. On *The Monarchy* she provides a critical political and feminist counterpoint to David Cannadine's institutional history of royal tradition.
