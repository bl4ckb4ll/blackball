# Harold “Harry” Evans

## Summary

Harold Evans, commonly known as Harry Evans, was a British-American journalist and editor best known for his editorship of *The Sunday Times*, where he became associated with investigative reporting and major public-interest campaigns. He later worked in publishing and American journalism.

On *The American Century* he brings a media and institutional perspective to American power. His background is useful for Blackball because national myths are not produced only by politicians and historians; editors and large media institutions help decide which abuses become visible and which achievements define a country's public story.
