# Will Self

## Summary

Will Self is a British novelist, essayist and cultural commentator known for satirical and formally experimental fiction. His writing frequently attacks conventional hierarchies of taste, class and respectability. On *Modern Culture* he serves as a practicing writer rather than an academic theorist, challenging Roger Scruton's account of cultural decline and helping make visible the institutional question of who gets to define serious culture.
