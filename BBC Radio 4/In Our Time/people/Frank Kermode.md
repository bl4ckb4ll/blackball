# Frank Kermode

## Summary

Frank Kermode was a major British literary critic whose work ranged across Shakespeare, modern literature, interpretation and the ways readers impose meaningful structures on texts and history. His books include *The Sense of an Ending*. On *Shakespeare's Work* he represents close literary criticism and the long scholarly institution around Shakespeare, providing one side of a discussion that also includes performance and feminist criticism.
