# Frank Kermode

## Summary

Frank Kermode was a major British literary critic whose work ranged across Shakespeare, modern literature, interpretation and the ways readers impose meaningful structures on texts and history. On *Shakespeare's Work* he represented close literary criticism and the long scholarly institution around Shakespeare.

On *The Sonnet* he places the form within Shakespearean criticism and the longer history of interpretation. His two appearances keep textual form, historical reception and later canon-making in the same frame.
