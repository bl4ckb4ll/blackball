# Ian Angell

## Summary

Ian Angell was a professor of information systems at the London School of Economics whose public writing examined the disruptive effects of computing and communications on firms, governments, privacy and employment. He was notably more skeptical than simple information-age boosterism. On *Information Technology* he helps turn the subject from a catalogue of devices into a question about who acquires leverage when information systems reorganize institutions.
