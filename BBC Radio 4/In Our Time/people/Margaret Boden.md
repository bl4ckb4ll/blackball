# Margaret Boden

## Summary

Margaret Boden is a cognitive scientist and philosopher whose work has examined artificial intelligence, creativity, computation and the conceptual foundations of cognitive science. Her books include *The Creative Mind*. On *Imagination and Consciousness* she brings a framework for asking what kinds of novelty computational systems can generate and whether computational explanation of creativity requires any claim that the system has human-like subjective experience.
