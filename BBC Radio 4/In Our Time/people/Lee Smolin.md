# Lee Smolin

## Summary

Lee Smolin is a theoretical physicist known for work on quantum gravity and for books arguing about the foundations and development of physics. He has been associated with loop quantum gravity and has written critically about the direction of fundamental physics. On *Time* he brings cosmology and foundational physics to the question of whether time is fundamental, emergent or represented differently by competing theories.
