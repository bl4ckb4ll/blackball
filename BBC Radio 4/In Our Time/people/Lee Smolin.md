# Lee Smolin

## Summary

Lee Smolin is a theoretical physicist known for work on quantum gravity and for books arguing about the foundations and development of physics. On *Time* he brought cosmology and foundational physics to the question of whether time is fundamental or emergent.

On *Quantum Gravity* he presents loop quantum gravity and the possibility that spacetime has a discrete underlying structure. The dossier should preserve the distinction between a live research programme and a settled empirical result.
