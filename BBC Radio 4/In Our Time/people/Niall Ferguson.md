# Niall Ferguson

## Summary

Niall Ferguson is a historian whose work has covered empire, finance, war, economic history and international power. On *Just War* he supplied historical cases against which moral arguments about warfare could be tested; on *Hitler in History* he addressed individual responsibility, state structures and counterfactual explanation.

On *Money* he traces public debt, banking and financial institutions alongside war and state power. The appearance is useful for treating money not as a neutral token but as an institutional relation involving enforceable obligations, political authority and unequal exposure to loss.
