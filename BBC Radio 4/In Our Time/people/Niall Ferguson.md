# Niall Ferguson

## Summary

Niall Ferguson is a historian whose work has covered empire, finance, war, economic history and international power. His books include *The Pity of War*, *Empire* and histories of money and financial institutions. On *Just War* he provides a historian's counterweight to normative political theory, asking how states invoke moral rules and whether a war's stated justification corresponds to its causes and conduct. On *Hitler in History* he contributes comparative arguments about individual responsibility, state structures and counterfactual explanation alongside two specialists in Nazi Germany.
