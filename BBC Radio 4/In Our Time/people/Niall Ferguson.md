# Niall Ferguson

## Summary

Niall Ferguson is a historian whose work has covered empire, finance, war, economic history and international power. His books include *The Pity of War*, *Empire* and histories of money and financial institutions. On *Just War* he provides a historian's counterweight to normative political theory, asking how states actually invoke moral rules in conflicts and whether the stated justification of a war corresponds to its causes and conduct.
