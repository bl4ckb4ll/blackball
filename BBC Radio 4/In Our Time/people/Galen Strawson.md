# Galen Strawson

## Summary

Galen Strawson is a philosopher known for work on consciousness, free will, personal identity and metaphysics. He has argued forcefully against the idea that people can be ultimately morally responsible for the way they are in the deepest sense. On *Good and Evil* he brings analytic questions about agency and responsibility into a discussion otherwise dominated by religious and historical conceptions of morality.
