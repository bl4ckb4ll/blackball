# Iain Sinclair

## Summary

Iain Sinclair is a British writer, poet and filmmaker whose work is closely associated with London, walking, redevelopment and psychogeography. Books such as *Lights Out for the Territory* examine neglected or rapidly changing urban spaces through observation, history and literary association. On *London* he contributes a deliberately subjective street-level counterpoint to conventional civic history; it is valuable testimony about perception and place, not a substitute for representative urban data.
