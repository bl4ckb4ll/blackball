# Mary Warnock

## Summary

Mary Warnock was a British philosopher, educationalist and public-policy figure whose work ranged across ethics, existentialism, education, special educational needs and bioethics. The Warnock Report on special education had major influence on British policy, and she later chaired the inquiry that shaped regulation of human fertilization and embryology. On *Education* she brings both philosophical arguments about the purpose of education and experience translating those arguments into institutions.
