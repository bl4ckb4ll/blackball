# Leszek Kołakowski

## Summary

Leszek Kołakowski was a Polish philosopher and historian of ideas who began as a Marxist intellectual, became a major critic of communist orthodoxy and wrote the three-volume *Main Currents of Marxism*. He also wrote extensively on religion, skepticism and the persistence of moral and metaphysical questions in secular societies. On *Good and Evil* he brings that long engagement with both secular ideology and religion to the question of moral authority.
