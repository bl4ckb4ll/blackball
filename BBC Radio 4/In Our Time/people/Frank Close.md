# Frank Close

## Summary

Frank Close is a British particle physicist and science writer whose work has covered the structure of matter, symmetry and the history of modern physics. He has held research and public-understanding roles at major UK laboratories and universities. On *Laws of Nature* he provides the practicing physicist's account of why compact physical laws are powerful while also confronting the question of how far that style of explanation extends beyond fundamental physics.
