# Rob Hopkins

## Summary

Rob Hopkins is the philosopher who appeared on the 2000 *Nihilism* episode, then teaching philosophy at the University of Birmingham. His work is in analytic philosophy rather than the later environmental “Transition Towns” movement associated with another Rob Hopkins. The dossier keeps that identity distinction explicit. On *Nihilism* he contributes philosophical analysis of truth, value and the consequences often attributed to Nietzschean skepticism.
