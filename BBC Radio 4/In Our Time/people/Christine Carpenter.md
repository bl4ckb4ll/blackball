# Christine Carpenter

## Summary

Christine Carpenter is a historian of late-medieval England whose work has examined the gentry, local politics, kingship and the political society from which Tudor government emerged. She has been associated with Christ Church, Oxford. On *The Tudor State* she supplies the medieval continuity side of the debate, testing claims of Tudor novelty against the institutions and local power structures already present before 1485.
