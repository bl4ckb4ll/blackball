# John Maynard Smith

## Summary

John Maynard Smith was a major British evolutionary biologist who helped bring game theory into evolutionary biology and developed influential work on sex, signaling and evolutionary stability. Trained originally as an engineer, he became one of the central theorists of twentieth-century population genetics. On *Evolution* he is particularly valuable for rejecting the popular habit of equating natural selection with a universal march toward higher forms.
