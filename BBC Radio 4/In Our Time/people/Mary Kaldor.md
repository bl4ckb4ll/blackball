# Mary Kaldor

## Summary

Mary Kaldor is a scholar of war, security and global civil society best known for the “new wars” thesis: the claim that many late-twentieth-century conflicts differ from classic interstate war through mixtures of state forces, militias, identity politics, criminal financing and violence directed at civilians. On *New Wars* she provides the conceptual framework against which military historian Michael Howard and commander Michael Rose can compare actual conflict and intervention.
