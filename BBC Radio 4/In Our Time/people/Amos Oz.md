# Amos Oz

## Summary

Amos Oz was an Israeli novelist, essayist and public intellectual whose fiction and nonfiction examined family life, Israeli society, nationalism, conflict and political compromise. He was also a prominent advocate of a two-state settlement in the Israeli-Palestinian conflict. On *The Great Disruption* he provides a literary and social perspective against which Francis Fukuyama's broad quantitative and institutional thesis can be tested.
