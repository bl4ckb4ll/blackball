# Jonathan Dollimore

## Summary

Jonathan Dollimore is a literary and cultural theorist associated with cultural materialism and with work on Renaissance literature, sexuality, death and political criticism. His scholarship asks how supposedly private identities are shaped by language, institutions and power. On *The Individual* he gives the historically constructed side of the debate, complementing Richard Wollheim's philosophical focus on persons and mental life. On *Death* he brings that literary and cultural approach to the ways mortality is represented and organized socially.
