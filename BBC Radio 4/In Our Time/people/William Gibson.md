# William Gibson

## Summary

William Gibson is a science-fiction writer best known for *Neuromancer* and for helping popularize the word “cyberspace.” His fiction examines technology, corporate power, advertising, consumer objects and the way new technical systems become ordinary environments. On *Materialism and the Consumer* he contributes cultural foresight and fictional observation rather than economic measurement, making the distinction between imaginative diagnosis and empirical evidence important.
