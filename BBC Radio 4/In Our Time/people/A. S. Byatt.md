# A. S. Byatt

## Summary

A. S. Byatt was a British novelist and critic whose fiction frequently engaged with Victorian literature, myth, art and intellectual history. Her novel *Possession* is especially known for embedding scholarly and historical forms inside fiction. On *Metamorphosis* she brings the practicing novelist's perspective to a literary tradition of transformation extending from Ovid into modern writing.
