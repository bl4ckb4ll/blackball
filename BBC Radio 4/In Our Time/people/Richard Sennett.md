# Richard Sennett

## Summary

Richard Sennett is a sociologist and social theorist whose major subjects include work, cities, craftsmanship, cooperation and the personal consequences of capitalism. *The Corrosion of Character* examined what happens when institutions demand flexibility, short time horizons and repeated reinvention from workers while offering less continuity in return.

That makes his 1998 *In Our Time* appearance a core Blackball source. Sennett gives a vocabulary for measuring work beyond whether someone is technically employed: continuity, control, skill, narrative coherence, institutional loyalty and the ability to plan a life all matter.
