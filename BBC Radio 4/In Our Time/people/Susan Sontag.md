# Susan Sontag

## Summary

Susan Sontag was an American essayist, novelist and critic whose writing addressed photography, illness, war, interpretation and modern culture. Her essays repeatedly examined how representations mediate people's relation to suffering and political power. On *The American Ideal* she brings a cultural critic's perspective on the United States, useful for analyzing national self-description but not interchangeable with quantitative evidence about American institutions or populations.
