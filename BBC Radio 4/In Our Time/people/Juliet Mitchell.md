# Juliet Mitchell

## Summary

Juliet Mitchell is a British psychoanalyst, feminist theorist and scholar whose books *Woman's Estate* and *Psychoanalysis and Feminism* helped reopen debate over Freud within feminist theory. She has treated psychoanalysis less as a timeless biological account of women than as a theory of how sexual difference and family relations are represented and reproduced. On *Psychoanalysis and its Legacy* she places Freud inside twentieth-century social and intellectual history.
