# Simon Schama

## Summary

Simon Schama is a historian known for highly narrative work spanning Dutch history, the French Revolution, art, landscape, Britain and Jewish history. His writing foregrounds the fact that historians do not merely collect facts; they construct an intelligible account from uneven surviving evidence.

In *History's Relevance in the 20th Century* that strength becomes the subject of the programme itself. Schama helps examine how much imaginative reconstruction historical writing can use before it becomes fiction, and why narrative craft does not remove the historian's obligation to evidence.
