# Jonathan Bate

## Summary

Jonathan Bate is a British literary scholar and biographer known for work on Shakespeare, Romanticism, ecology and literary history. On *The Romantics* he contributed close literary scholarship and attention to the historical environments in which Romantic writing developed.

On *The Sonnet* he traces the English form through Shakespeare, Romanticism and later literary history. Together the appearances show how a named literary movement or form is reconstructed from texts, institutions and retrospective classification.
