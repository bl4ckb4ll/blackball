# Jonathan Bate

## Summary

Jonathan Bate is a British literary scholar and biographer known for work on Shakespeare, Romanticism, ecology and literary history. His books include *Romantic Ecology* and biographies of major writers. On *The Romantics* he contributes both close literary scholarship and attention to the historical environments in which Romantic writing developed, resisting a purely retrospective movement label.
