# Paul Langford

## Summary

Paul Langford was a historian of eighteenth-century Britain whose work examined politics, manners, national identity and the social order. His books include *A Polite and Commercial People*. On *Englishness* he supplies a long historical view of characteristics later presented as timelessly English, allowing the programme to ask when those traits became culturally prominent and which institutions helped define them.
