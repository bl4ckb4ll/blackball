# Michael Rose

## Summary

General Sir Michael Rose is a retired British Army officer who commanded the United Nations Protection Force in Bosnia in 1994. On *New Wars* he brings direct command experience from a conflict involving regular armies, militias, international forces, siege warfare and large civilian populations. His testimony is therefore particularly useful for comparing abstract doctrines of intervention with the constraints, chains of command and delegated coercion encountered on the ground.
