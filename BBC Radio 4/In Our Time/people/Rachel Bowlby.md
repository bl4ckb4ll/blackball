# Rachel Bowlby

## Summary

Rachel Bowlby is a literary and cultural critic whose work has examined consumer culture, shopping, supermarkets, psychoanalysis and modern literature. Her books include *Just Looking* and *Carried Away*, studies of retail and consumption as social and cultural institutions. On *Materialism and the Consumer* she provides the historical framework for understanding shopping as more than individual preference: retail forms, advertising and social meanings structure what appears as consumer choice.
