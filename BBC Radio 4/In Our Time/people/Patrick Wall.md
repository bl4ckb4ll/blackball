# Patrick Wall

## Summary

Patrick Wall was a British neuroscientist and physiologist who, with Ronald Melzack, developed the gate control theory of pain. That work transformed pain research by showing that pain is not a simple one-to-one readout of tissue damage but is modulated by neural systems and context. On *Pain* he provides the foundational physiological account that makes subjective reports scientifically important rather than dismissing them as noise.
