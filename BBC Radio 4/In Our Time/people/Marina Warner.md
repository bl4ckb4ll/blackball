# Marina Warner

## Summary

Marina Warner is a British novelist, critic and cultural historian known for work on myth, fairy tale, symbolism, gender and storytelling. Her books trace how apparently timeless stories change as they move through institutions and historical settings. On *Maths and Storytelling* she supplies the narrative side of a comparison with mathematical pattern, helping distinguish formal structure from the meanings readers and cultures attach to recurring forms.
