# Andrew Samuels

## Summary

Andrew Samuels is a British psychotherapist and Jungian analyst whose work has connected analytical psychology with politics, gender and social conflict. He became Professor of Analytical Psychology at the University of Essex. On *Prayer* he approaches religious practice psychologically, asking what prayer may do within the mental life of the person praying even when separate claims about supernatural intervention remain unestablished.
