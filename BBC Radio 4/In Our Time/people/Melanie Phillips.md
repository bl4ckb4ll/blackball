# Melanie Phillips

## Summary

Melanie Phillips is a British journalist and political commentator who has written extensively on social policy, education, family, culture and politics. Her commentary has generally taken a socially conservative direction, although particular claims need to be evaluated on their own evidence rather than inferred from that label.

In *Work in the 20th Century* she supplies a journalistic and social-policy perspective alongside Richard Sennett and Theodore Zeldin. Her presence is useful because the episode contains not only academic interpretations of labour but a contemporary argument over the norms and institutions surrounding work.
