# Frances Morris

## Summary

Frances Morris is a British curator whose career at Tate included major responsibility for modern and contemporary art and later the directorship of Tate Modern. In 1999 she was involved in the programme for the new Tate Gallery of Modern Art. On the avant-garde episode she represents the museum institution from inside: how art is selected, displayed, preserved and presented to a public, and therefore how institutional judgment helps form an artistic canon.
