# Tom Kirkwood

## Summary

Tom Kirkwood is a biologist of ageing known for the disposable-soma theory, which explains senescence through evolutionary tradeoffs in maintenance and reproduction rather than through a simple built-in death clock. He became the UK's first professor of biological gerontology. On *Ageing* he provides the biological mechanisms against which Alan Walker's social account can be compared, keeping physiological ageing distinct from the institutions that determine how older people live.
