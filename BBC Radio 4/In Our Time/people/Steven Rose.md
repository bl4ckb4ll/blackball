# Steven Rose

## Summary

Steven Rose is a biologist and neuroscientist known for research on memory and brain mechanisms and for sustained criticism of biological determinism. His public writing has repeatedly challenged attempts to infer fixed social hierarchies or political prescriptions directly from biology.

In *The Brain and Consciousness* he brings experimental biology to a discussion that could otherwise become purely philosophical. In *Evolutionary Psychology* he challenges adaptationist explanations of human behavior and arguments that slide from an evolutionary hypothesis to a fixed social conclusion. For Blackball, his importance is methodological: even when a biological measurement is real, the causal and social interpretation placed on it has to be argued separately.
