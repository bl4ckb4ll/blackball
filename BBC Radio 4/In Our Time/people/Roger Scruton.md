# Roger Scruton

## Summary

Roger Scruton was a British philosopher and writer whose work concentrated on aesthetics, conservatism, architecture, music and political philosophy. He defended inherited forms of judgment and tradition against much modernist and postmodernist culture. On *Modern Culture* he gives the strongest version of the cultural-decline argument, making his appearance useful for separating an aesthetic judgment from empirical claims about cultural participation, institutional authority or social decay.
