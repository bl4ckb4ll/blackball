# Roger Scruton

## Summary

Roger Scruton was a British philosopher and writer whose work concentrated on aesthetics, conservatism, architecture, music and political philosophy. On *Modern Culture* he gave a strong version of the cultural-decline argument, useful for separating aesthetic judgment from empirical claims about participation or social decay.

On *The Philosophy of Love* he develops an account of erotic desire, personhood and the moral meaning of sexual relations. The two appearances connect his aesthetics and conservatism to a broader theory of judgment, attachment and inherited forms.
