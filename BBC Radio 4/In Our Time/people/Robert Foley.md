# Robert Foley

## Summary

Robert Foley is a biological anthropologist and human-evolution researcher whose work applies evolutionary ecology to hominin diversity, migration and adaptation. He helped develop major research programmes on human evolutionary history at Cambridge. On *Human Origins* he brings population and ecological models to a fossil and archaeological record that is necessarily incomplete, making explicit the inferential steps between finds and larger reconstructions.
