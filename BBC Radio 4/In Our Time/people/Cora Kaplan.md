# Cora Kaplan

## Summary

Cora Kaplan is a feminist literary and cultural critic whose work has examined gender, class, race, fiction and the history of feminist thought. Her scholarship treats literary representations as part of the social production of gender rather than as transparent descriptions of how people naturally are. On *Masculinity in Literature* she provides the critical framework for asking how male identities are narrated, normalized and challenged in texts.
