# D. J. Taylor

## Summary

D. J. Taylor is a British novelist, critic and literary biographer whose work has examined twentieth-century British writing and the institutions around literary careers. His books include biographies of Thackeray and Orwell and the literary history *After the War*. On *The Novel* he speaks as both a practicing novelist and historian of literary culture, discussing the form's survival amid repeated claims that new media would displace it.
