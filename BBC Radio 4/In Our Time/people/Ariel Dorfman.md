# Ariel Dorfman

## Summary

Ariel Dorfman is an Argentine-Chilean-American writer, playwright and essayist whose work has been shaped by exile and the Pinochet dictatorship in Chile. His play *Death and the Maiden* examines torture, memory, evidence and accountability after authoritarian rule. On *Writing and Political Oppression* he contributes firsthand political and literary experience of exile, censorship and the problem of speaking for victims when official institutions suppress or deny what happened.
