# Susan Greenfield

## Summary

Susan Greenfield is a neuroscientist and science communicator whose research has involved brain physiology, pharmacology and questions about consciousness. She has also held prominent public-facing scientific roles, including leadership of the Royal Institution.

In *Neuroscience in the 20th Century* she approaches consciousness from experimental neurobiology and pharmacology. For Blackball the relevant distinction is between evidence about mechanisms in the nervous system and broader claims about identity or social worth that the evidence by itself does not establish.
