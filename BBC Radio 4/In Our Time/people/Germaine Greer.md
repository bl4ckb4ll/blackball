# Germaine Greer

## Summary

Germaine Greer is an Australian-born writer, academic and feminist critic best known for *The Female Eunuch*. Her work has addressed women's social roles, sexuality, literature and the history of women's writing. On *Feminism* she supplies a political and literary feminist counterpoint to Helena Cronin's evolutionary account, pressing on the difference between observed sex differences and the institutions that allocate freedom, status and opportunity.
