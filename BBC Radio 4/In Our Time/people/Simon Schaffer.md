# Simon Schaffer

## Summary

Simon Schaffer is a historian of science whose work has examined experimentation, instruments, astronomy, physics and the social organization of scientific credibility. On the Goethe episode he reconstructed Enlightenment science as a practice embedded in institutions and conventions rather than ranking past thinkers only by modern textbook correctness.

On *Oceanography* he examines the instruments, coordination and systems of trust required to make observations at sea authoritative. The episode makes visible the labour and organization hidden behind apparently self-contained scientific facts.
