# Simon Schaffer

## Summary

Simon Schaffer is a historian of science whose work has examined experimentation, instruments, astronomy, physics and the social organization of scientific credibility. With Steven Shapin he wrote *Leviathan and the Air-Pump*, a major study of how experimental facts become authoritative. On the Goethe episode he helps reconstruct Enlightenment science as a practice embedded in institutions and conventions rather than simply ranking past thinkers by modern textbook correctness.
