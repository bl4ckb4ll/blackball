# Sarah Barber

## Summary

Sarah Barber is a historian whose work has focused on seventeenth-century Britain, republicanism and the political culture surrounding civil war and the English Commonwealth. On *Republicanism* she supplies the specialist history needed to distinguish later republican ideals from the institutions and political conflicts of the actual seventeenth-century republic.
