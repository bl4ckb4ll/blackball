# Semir Zeki

## Summary

Semir Zeki is a neuroscientist known for research on the visual cortex and for developing neuroaesthetics, the study of neural processes involved in visual experience and art. His work maps specialized brain systems while also raising questions about how neural measurements relate to subjective experience. On *Pain* he brings a neurobiological perspective to an experience that cannot be reduced to visible injury alone.
