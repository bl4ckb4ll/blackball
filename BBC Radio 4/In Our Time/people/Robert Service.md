# Robert Service

## Summary

Robert Service is a historian of modern Russia and the Soviet Union known for biographies of Lenin, Stalin and Trotsky and for broader histories of Soviet politics. His *Lenin* biography was central to the 2000 discussion. On the episode he provides archival and institutional history for judging Lenin's role in revolution, one-party government and coercive state formation rather than treating later Soviet history as either wholly predetermined or wholly disconnected from the institutions Lenin built.
