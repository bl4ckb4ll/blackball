# Ian Stewart

## Summary

Ian Stewart is a British mathematician and prolific expositor whose research has included dynamical systems and whose books have presented advanced mathematics to broad audiences. On *Mathematics* he represented mathematics as a discipline and practice rather than only a collection of school techniques.

On *Mathematics and Platonism* he brings that practice to the question of discovery versus invention: mathematical structures constrain what can be done even though notation, questions and institutions are historically made. This helps separate internal mathematical objectivity from stronger metaphysical claims about a separate realm of forms.
