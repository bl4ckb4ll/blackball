# Ian Stewart

## Summary

Ian Stewart is a British mathematician and prolific expositor whose research has included dynamical systems and whose books have presented advanced mathematics to broad audiences. He has held a professorship at the University of Warwick and served as Gresham Professor of Geometry. On *Mathematics* he represents mathematics as a discipline and practice, complementing Brian Butterworth's work on the cognitive capacities through which people learn and perform mathematics.
