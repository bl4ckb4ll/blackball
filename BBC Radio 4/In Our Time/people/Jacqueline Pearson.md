# Jacqueline Pearson

## Summary

Jacqueline Pearson is a scholar of English literature and the history of reading whose research has examined gendered access to books and the changing meanings attached to women's reading. On *Reading* she supplies evidence that literacy cannot be understood only as the technical ability to decode print: what women were permitted, encouraged or warned not to read was shaped by social authority and access to texts.
