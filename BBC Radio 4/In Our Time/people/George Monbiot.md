# George Monbiot

## Summary

George Monbiot is a British environmental journalist, author and campaigner whose work has addressed climate change, land use, corporate power and environmental policy. On the 2000 *Climate Change* episode he brings the political and distributive side of the issue alongside John Houghton's atmospheric science: who bears the costs of environmental damage, what kinds of uncertainty justify delay, and which institutions have power to act.
