# George Steiner

## Summary

George Steiner was a literary critic and comparatist whose writing addressed tragedy, translation, language, European high culture and the relationship between civilization and twentieth-century barbarism. His books include *The Death of Tragedy* and *After Babel*. On *Tragedy* he supplies a historically expansive account of tragic form and its possible decline, while also raising the difficult question of how artistic categories relate to real political catastrophe.
