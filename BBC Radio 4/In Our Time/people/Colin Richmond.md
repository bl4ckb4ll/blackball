# Colin Richmond

## Summary

Colin Richmond was a historian of late-medieval England whose research examined the Wars of the Roses, gentry society and English Jewish history. He was known for questioning tidy political narratives and emphasizing local relationships and evidence. On *The Wars of the Roses* he helps break the retrospective label into particular conflicts, loyalties and regions rather than assuming a continuous national war with two coherent sides.
