# Barry Jackson

## Summary

Barry Jackson was a British surgeon who served as President of the Royal College of Surgeons of England. His career placed him inside the professional institutions responsible for surgical standards and the expansion of technically demanding medical treatment. On *Medical Ethics* he represents clinical practice and professional judgment in a debate about when medical capability should or should not be exercised.
