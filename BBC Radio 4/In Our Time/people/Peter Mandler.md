# Peter Mandler

## Summary

Peter Mandler is a historian of modern Britain whose work has covered national identity, culture, education, the social sciences and the uses of history. His research is particularly useful for testing claims that national character is stable across centuries. On *Englishness* he helps locate ideas of English identity in specific periods and institutions rather than treating them as natural properties of a population.
