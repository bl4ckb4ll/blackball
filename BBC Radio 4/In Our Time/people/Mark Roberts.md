# Mark Roberts

## Summary

Mark Roberts is the British archaeologist associated with the Boxgrove Paleolithic site in West Sussex, where excavations produced exceptionally important evidence of early human occupation, stone tools and hominin remains. On *Human Origins* he contributes field archaeology: stratigraphy, artifacts, animal remains and site context provide a material check on broad evolutionary narratives. Keep this dossier identified specifically as the Boxgrove archaeologist because the name is common.
