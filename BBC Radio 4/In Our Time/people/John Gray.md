# John Gray

## Summary

John Gray is a political philosopher known for work on liberalism, value pluralism and criticism of universal theories of historical progress. Over time he became increasingly skeptical of projects that treat one political or economic order as the necessary destination of every society.

In *Cultural Rights in the 20th Century* he questions simple universalism from within political philosophy. His contribution is useful for separating universal protections against domination from the stronger—and much less obvious—claim that one institutional model is universally correct.
