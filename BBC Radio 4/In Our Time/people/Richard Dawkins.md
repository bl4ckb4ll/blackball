# Richard Dawkins

## Summary

Richard Dawkins is an evolutionary biologist and science writer best known for gene-centred accounts of natural selection and for public arguments about scientific naturalism. In *Science's Revelations* he argued that causal explanation can intensify rather than destroy wonder.

On *Genetics* he explains the sense in which genes persist through differential reproduction and how that relates to natural selection. The episode still requires a boundary between the explanatory usefulness of gene-centred models and stronger claims that complex traits or social outcomes are fixed by genes.
