# Richard Dawkins

## Summary

Richard Dawkins is an evolutionary biologist and science writer best known for gene-centred accounts of natural selection and for arguing publicly for scientific naturalism. His books made evolutionary reasoning accessible far outside specialist biology and also made him a prominent participant in disputes over religion and the cultural authority of science.

In *Science's Revelations* his role is narrower than that later public reputation: he argues that causal explanation can intensify rather than destroy wonder. The episode is therefore useful for separating a scientist's empirical field from broader philosophical claims made in the name of science.
