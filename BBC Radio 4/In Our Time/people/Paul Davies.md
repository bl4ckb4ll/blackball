# Paul Davies

## Summary

Paul Davies is a theoretical physicist, cosmologist and science writer whose work has addressed quantum theory, cosmology, astrobiology and the origin of life. He is also known for philosophical writing about the implications of physical theory. On *The Universe's Origins* he helps explore where established cosmology ends and broader interpretation begins, especially in questions about why physical laws have the form they do and whether life is common in the universe.
