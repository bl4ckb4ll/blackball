# Karen Armstrong

## Summary

Karen Armstrong is a British writer on comparative religion whose books have examined Judaism, Christianity, Islam, fundamentalism and the historical development of religious ideas. A former Catholic religious sister, she became a prominent interpreter of religion for general audiences. On *Fundamentalism* she treats fundamentalist movements historically rather than as timeless expressions of a religion, emphasizing their relation to modernity, political conflict and perceived threats.
