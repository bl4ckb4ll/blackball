# Amanda Foreman

## Summary

Amanda Foreman is a historian and biographer whose first major book, *Georgiana, Duchess of Devonshire*, combined political, social and personal history and won major attention shortly before her *In Our Time* appearance. On *Biography* she represents a younger generation of biographical historians and the use of private papers to reconstruct a public figure's political and social world.
