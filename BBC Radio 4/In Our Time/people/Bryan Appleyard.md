# Bryan Appleyard

## Summary

Bryan Appleyard is a British journalist and author who has written about science, technology, religion and culture for general audiences. His book *Brave New Worlds: Genetics and the Human Experience* examined the social meaning attached to the new genetics. On *Genetic Engineering* he is not the laboratory expert; his role is to interrogate the claims made around biotechnology—especially genetic prediction, identity and the temptation to treat a genome as a complete description of a person.
