# Jacqueline Rose

## Summary

Jacqueline Rose is a literary and cultural critic whose work draws on psychoanalysis, feminism, politics and literature. She has written on sexuality, motherhood, nationalism, Israel/Palestine and the political uses of psychic life. On *Shakespeare and Literary Criticism* she represents forms of criticism that ask what canonical texts disclose about gender, desire, power and unconscious conflict rather than treating literary greatness as self-explanatory.
