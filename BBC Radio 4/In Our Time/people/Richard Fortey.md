# Richard Fortey

## Summary

On *Fossils*, Richard Fortey connects fossils, taxonomy and stratigraphy to extinction, evolution and the institutional history of palaeontology. The broadcast-era identification below is retained for provenance; it should not be read as a current affiliation.
