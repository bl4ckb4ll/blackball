# Roger Penrose

## Summary

Roger Penrose is a mathematical physicist and mathematician whose work includes singularity theorems in general relativity, twistor theory, tilings and mathematical physics. He has also argued, controversially, that human mathematical understanding and consciousness may involve processes not captured by ordinary algorithmic computation. On *Consciousness* he brings those arguments into direct contact with philosophy of mind; the empirical physics and the stronger claims about mind should be tracked separately.
