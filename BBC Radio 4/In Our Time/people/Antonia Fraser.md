# Antonia Fraser

## Summary

Antonia Fraser is a historian and biographer particularly associated with narrative histories of monarchy, women and political figures in Britain and Europe. Her work sits close to the boundary discussed in the 1998 episode: it aims for the readability and characterization of narrative writing while remaining accountable to historical evidence.

In *History's Relevance in the 20th Century* she helps test the difference between making the past vivid and inventing it. For Blackball, that is directly relevant to the use of memoir, oral testimony and first-person accounts alongside administrative or quantitative records.
