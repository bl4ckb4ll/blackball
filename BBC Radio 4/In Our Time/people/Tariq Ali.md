# Tariq Ali

## Summary

Tariq Ali is a Pakistani-British writer, editor and political activist associated with the New Left and with criticism of imperialism, war and authoritarianism. His books and journalism range across South Asian, Middle Eastern and European politics. On *Fundamentalism* he supplies a secular political analysis that connects religious movements to colonial history, state power and geopolitical conflict rather than explaining them only through doctrine.
