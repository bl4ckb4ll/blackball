# Tariq Ali

## Summary

Tariq Ali is a Pakistani-British writer, editor and political activist associated with the New Left and with criticism of imperialism, war and authoritarianism. On *Fundamentalism* he connected religious movements to colonial history, state power and geopolitical conflict rather than explaining them only through doctrine.

On *Third Crusade* he adds a political and literary counterpoint concerning Saladin's later reputation and the uses of crusading memory. His role is especially useful for distinguishing medieval actors and institutions from modern political narratives built out of them.
