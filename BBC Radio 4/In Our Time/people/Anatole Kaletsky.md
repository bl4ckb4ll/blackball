# Anatole Kaletsky

## Summary

Anatole Kaletsky is an economist and financial journalist who has written extensively on macroeconomics, markets and economic policy. In 1999 he was an economics commentator and associate editor at *The Times*. On *Capitalism* he represents a mainstream market-economy perspective in a discussion about whether capitalism's post-Cold-War dominance made older critiques obsolete. His claims are useful to compare with labor-level measures rather than only national aggregates.
