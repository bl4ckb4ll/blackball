# The Wars of the Roses

- **Broadcast:** 18 May 2000
- **BBC programme ID:** `p00546sp`
- **BBC:** https://www.bbc.co.uk/programmes/p00546sp
- **Coverage:** bbc-verified

## Summary

Helen Castor, Colin Richmond and Steven Gunn discuss the dynastic conflicts conventionally grouped as the Wars of the Roses. The episode distinguishes later literary and national memory—especially Shakespeare's dramatic version—from the documentary history of aristocratic politics, local loyalties, kingship and state power.

For Blackball this is a useful reminder that memorable labels and narratives can create a cleaner historical object than participants themselves experienced. The archive should preserve uncertainty and regional variation rather than forcing every event into the later name of a war.
