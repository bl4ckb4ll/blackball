## Summary

Francis Ames-Lewis, Peter Burke and Evelyn Welch discuss the Renaissance and the powerful nineteenth-century story that it marked the birth of modern individualism and a decisive break from the Middle Ages. All three guests bring historical or art-historical expertise, allowing the programme to test that grand periodization against patronage, workshops, trade, courts, religion and continuities across the supposed divide.

For Blackball the episode is methodological: names such as “Renaissance” are useful organizing devices, but the label should not substitute for measuring which institutions and practices actually changed and which did not.
