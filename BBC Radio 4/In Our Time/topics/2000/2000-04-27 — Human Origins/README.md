## Summary

Leslie Aiello, Robert Foley and Mark Roberts discuss evidence for human evolution from anatomy, fossils, archaeology and sites such as Boxgrove. Aiello brings biological anthropology and human anatomy; Foley brings evolutionary ecology and hominin evolution; Roberts brings field archaeology from one of Britain's major Paleolithic sites.

The episode is useful against ladder-like pictures of evolution. Hominin history involves branching populations, extinction, migration and changing environments. Fossils and artifacts are partial evidence from which relationships and behavior have to be inferred, not a complete chronological sequence of ever-superior types.
