# Psychoanalysis and Literature

- **Broadcast:** 9 November 2000
- **BBC programme ID:** `p00546y5`

- **Coverage:** cross-checked

## Summary

Adam Phillips, Malcolm Bowie and Lisa Appignanesi discuss the influence of psychoanalysis on literature and literary criticism. Phillips brings clinical and essayistic psychoanalysis; Bowie brings literary scholarship and work on Freud and Lacan; Appignanesi brings literary history, biography and writing on women in Freud's circle.

The episode is useful for separating two questions often run together: whether psychoanalysis is empirically adequate as a clinical theory and whether its vocabulary is productive as a way of reading texts. A framework can be illuminating in one role without thereby being established in the other.
