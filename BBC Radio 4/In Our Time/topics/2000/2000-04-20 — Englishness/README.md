## Summary

Paul Langford, Peter Mandler and Lola Young discuss the historical construction of English national identity. Langford and Mandler bring histories of Britain, manners, institutions and national self-description; Young brings cultural history, race and museum work. The episode asks which traits later called “English” are old, which are comparatively recent, and whose experience was excluded when national identity was narrated as homogeneous.

For Blackball the key question is evidential: a national stereotype should not be mistaken for a population description. Institutions, empire, migration and class all shape which people are allowed to stand for the nation.
