## Summary

Rob Hopkins, Raymond Tallis and Catherine Belsey discuss nihilism through Nietzsche and later debates over truth, morality, meaning and modernity. The guests bring philosophy, medicine and humanism, and literary/cultural theory respectively. The episode follows a broad intellectual lineage, but such lineages need care: disagreement with objective moral or metaphysical foundations is not automatically the claim that nothing matters or that every statement is equally true.

For Blackball the useful rule is to unpack the word “nihilism” into specific propositions rather than using it as a label for any skeptical or anti-foundational position.
