## Summary

A. N. Wilson and Victoria Glendinning discuss the erosion and transformation of Christian belief in Britain from the eighteenth century through the Victorian crisis of faith and into the twentieth century. Scientific developments matter, but the episode also examines biblical criticism, social change, intellectual fashion and personal experience.

The useful historical caution is against a single-cause story in which “science disproved religion” and everything else followed automatically. Changes in belief are institutional and cultural as well as intellectual.
