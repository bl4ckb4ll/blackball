# Imagination and Consciousness

- **Broadcast:** 29 June 2000
- **BBC programme ID:** `p00546vr`
- **BBC:** https://www.bbc.co.uk/programmes/p00546vr
- **Coverage:** cross-checked

## Summary

Gerald Edelman, Igor Aleksander and Margaret Boden discuss consciousness, imagination and whether systems capable of internal representation, novelty and selection can be built or modeled computationally. Edelman brings neuroscience and neural Darwinism; Aleksander brings machine-consciousness research; Boden brings cognitive science, philosophy and artificial intelligence.

For Blackball this is an early AI benchmark. Keep separate claims about generating novel outputs, modeling cognitive processes, possessing internal representations and having subjective experience. Success at one does not automatically establish the others.
