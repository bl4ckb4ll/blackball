## Summary

Brian Greene and Martin Rees discuss attempts to reconcile quantum theory with gravitation and to find a deeper unification of fundamental physics. Greene brings string theory; Rees brings cosmology and astrophysics. The discussion captures a period in which string theory had great theoretical prestige but limited direct empirical testing.

For Blackball the important distinction is between mathematical coherence, explanatory ambition and empirical confirmation. A beautiful framework can be intellectually productive without having the same evidential status as a theory that makes distinctive tested predictions.
