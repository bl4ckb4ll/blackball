## Summary

Janet Radcliffe Richards, Nicholas Humphrey and Steven Rose discuss attempts to explain human behavior in evolutionary terms. Humphrey brings psychology and evolutionary accounts of social cognition; Rose brings neuroscience and a longstanding criticism of biological determinism; Radcliffe Richards brings philosophy and bioethics.

For Blackball this is a core inference-audit topic. An evolutionary explanation needs more than a plausible story about how a trait might once have been adaptive. Competing hypotheses, developmental mechanisms, cross-cultural evidence and predictions that could fail are necessary if adaptationist explanation is to be more than retrospective storytelling.
