## Summary

Richard Holmes, Nigel Hamilton and Amanda Foreman discuss biography as historical research and narrative construction. Biographers work from letters, diaries, archives, interviews and other people's memories, but they still have to select events, infer motives and construct a sequence that makes a life intelligible.

For Blackball this is a direct source-method issue. A people dossier should distinguish documented facts from interpretive claims and should not let a polished life narrative erase missing records, contradictory testimony or structural opportunities unavailable to comparison cases.
