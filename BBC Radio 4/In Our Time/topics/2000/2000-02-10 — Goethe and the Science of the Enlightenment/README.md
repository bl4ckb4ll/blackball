# Goethe and the Science of the Enlightenment

- **Broadcast:** 10 February 2000
- **BBC programme ID:** `p00546n1`
- **BBC:** https://www.bbc.co.uk/programmes/p00546n1
- **Coverage:** bbc-verified

## Summary

Nicholas Boyle and Simon Schaffer discuss Goethe not only as a literary figure but as an investigator of color, plants and morphology within the scientific culture of the Enlightenment. Boyle brings biographical and literary expertise on Goethe; Schaffer brings history of science.

The episode is useful methodologically because it resists judging past scientific work only by whether later science retained its conclusions. Historical significance also lies in questions asked, methods used, institutions available and the conceptual boundaries between science, art and philosophy at the time.
