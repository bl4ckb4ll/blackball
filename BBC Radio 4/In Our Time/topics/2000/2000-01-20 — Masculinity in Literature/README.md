# Masculinity in Literature

- **Broadcast:** 20 January 2000
- **BBC programme ID:** `p00546lx`
- **BBC:** https://www.bbc.co.uk/programmes/p00546lx
- **Coverage:** cross-checked

## Summary

Martin Amis and Cora Kaplan discuss changing representations of masculinity in literature and the anxiety, authority and social expectations attached to male roles. Amis speaks as a novelist whose fiction often stages aggressive or unstable forms of masculinity; Kaplan brings feminist literary and cultural criticism.

The episode is most useful when literary representation is kept distinct from population-level social evidence. Novels can expose norms, fantasies and conflicts, but they do not by themselves establish how common a behavior is or what caused it.
