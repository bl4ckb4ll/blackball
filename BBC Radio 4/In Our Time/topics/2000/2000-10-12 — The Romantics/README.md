# The Romantics

- **Broadcast:** 12 October 2000
- **BBC programme ID:** `p00546ws`
- **BBC:** https://www.bbc.co.uk/programmes/p00546ws
- **Coverage:** cross-checked

## Summary

Jonathan Bate, Rosemary Ashton and Nicholas Roe discuss the writers retrospectively grouped as the Romantics and the political, intellectual and literary conditions surrounding them. The category joins distinct authors and positions into a movement that later criticism made more coherent than it may have appeared to participants.

For Blackball this is useful as a classification problem: a retrospective label can help organize evidence while also hiding disagreement, chronology and institutional context. The people and texts should not become instances of a definition invented after the fact.
