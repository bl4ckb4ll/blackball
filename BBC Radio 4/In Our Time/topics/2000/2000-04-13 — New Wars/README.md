# New Wars

- **Coverage:** bbc-verified

## Summary

Michael Howard, Mary Kaldor and Michael Rose discuss warfare after the Cold War, especially conflicts in which states, militias, paramilitaries, international forces and civilian populations interact differently from the classic model of two national armies. Kaldor brings the “new wars” thesis; Howard brings military history; Rose brings direct command experience from UN operations in Bosnia.

For Blackball this is a major delegated-coercion source. The chain from political objective to violence may run through regular armies, police, militias, contractors or local armed groups. Mapping those intermediary roles is necessary if responsibility is not to disappear into the word “war.”
