# Laws of Nature

## Summary

Mark Buchanan, Frank Close and Nancy Cartwright discuss what scientists mean by a “law of nature” and whether the success of compact laws in parts of physics licenses the belief that all phenomena are governed by similarly universal equations. Buchanan brings physics and complex systems; Close brings particle physics; Cartwright brings philosophy of science and a sustained critique of overly universal pictures of scientific laws.

For Blackball this is a direct warning against extrapolation. A model that works under controlled conditions may be local, approximate or dependent on auxiliary assumptions. Calling something a law does not remove the need to specify where it applies and how well it predicts.
