# The American Ideal

## Summary

Christopher Hitchens, John Keane and Susan Sontag discuss the ideals attached to the United States—liberty, democracy, individualism and self-invention—and the contradictions between those ideals and American institutions at home and abroad. Hitchens and Sontag bring essayistic and cultural criticism; Keane brings democratic theory.

For Blackball the core discipline is to measure ideals against institutional outcomes without assuming either that hypocrisy cancels every achievement or that aspirational language proves actual equality. Political rights, racial hierarchy, wealth, war and cultural power need separate evidence.
