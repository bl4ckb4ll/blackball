## Summary

Peter Ackroyd, Claire Tomalin and Iain Sinclair discuss London as both a material city and an accumulation of stories. Ackroyd and Tomalin bring biography and literary history; Sinclair brings fiction, poetry and psychogeographic attention to streets, development and neglected urban spaces.

For Blackball the episode should be read beside material urban history rather than in place of it. A city's mythology can reveal how people understand it, but wages, rents, transport, displacement, immigration, industrial change and municipal power determine who can actually live and move there.
