# The American Century

- **Broadcast:** 17 December 1998
- **BBC programme ID:** `p0054594`
- **BBC:** https://www.bbc.co.uk/programmes/p0054594
- **Coverage:** cross-checked

## Summary

Harry Evans and John Lloyd examine the claim that the twentieth century was an “American century.” The programme contrasts the international appeal of American democracy, freedom and material abundance with war, racial hierarchy, inequality and the use of American power abroad. The question is not only whether the United States became dominant but what kind of dominance it exercised and how its ideals interacted with its institutions.

The episode is useful as a reminder that national self-description and measured social outcomes are different evidence. A country can export a compelling political vocabulary while distributing power and security unequally at home and abroad.
