# The Brain and Consciousness

- **Broadcast:** 19 November 1998
- **BBC programme ID:** `p0054582`
- **BBC:** https://www.bbc.co.uk/programmes/p0054582
- **Coverage:** cross-checked

## Summary

Steven Rose and Dan Robinson ask how far knowledge of the physical brain can explain mind, memory and conscious experience. The discussion moves between biological mechanisms and philosophical questions about persons: a description of neural activity may be necessary to understand memory, but it does not automatically settle every question about agency, identity or moral responsibility.

Rose brings experimental neurobiology and a long-running criticism of crude biological determinism. Robinson brings philosophy and the history of psychology. Their pairing makes the episode particularly useful as a warning against moving silently from a measurable biological finding to a much larger social or moral conclusion.
