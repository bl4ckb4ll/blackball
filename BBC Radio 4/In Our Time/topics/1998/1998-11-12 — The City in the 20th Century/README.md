# The City in the 20th Century

- **Broadcast:** 12 November 1998
- **BBC programme ID:** `p005457r`
- **BBC:** https://www.bbc.co.uk/programmes/p005457r
- **Coverage:** cross-checked

## Summary

Peter Hall and Doreen Massey discuss the twentieth-century city as both a built environment and a social relation. Cities concentrate capital, labour, institutions, migration, culture and invention; planning can reshape those concentrations but cannot make conflicts over land, class and access disappear.

Hall brings the history and practice of urban planning and the idea of cities as centres of innovation. Massey brings a geographical account of place as something made by larger economic and social relations rather than a closed local container. For Blackball, the episode is useful whenever a claim about a city treats its visible buildings separately from the labour, movement and unequal power that produced them.
