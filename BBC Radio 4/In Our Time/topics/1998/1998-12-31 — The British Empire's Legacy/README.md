# The British Empire's Legacy

- **Broadcast:** 31 December 1998
- **BBC programme ID:** `p005459p`
- **BBC:** https://www.bbc.co.uk/programmes/p005459p
- **Coverage:** cross-checked

## Summary

Catherine Hall and Linda Colley discuss how the British Empire should be remembered inside Britain after formal empire. The episode treats imperial history not as something that happened only overseas but as a force that shaped British identities, institutions, wealth, race and political language at home.

Hall stresses the connections between metropolitan Britain and colonial power, including race and gender. Colley brings a longer history of British state formation and national identity. The useful question is not whether descendants should inherit personal guilt, but whether institutions and material arrangements produced through empire persist after the people who built them are gone.
