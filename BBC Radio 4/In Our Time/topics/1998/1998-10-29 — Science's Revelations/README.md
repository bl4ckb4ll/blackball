# Science's Revelations

- **Broadcast:** 29 October 1998
- **BBC programme ID:** `p005454c`
- **BBC:** https://www.bbc.co.uk/programmes/p005454c
- **Coverage:** cross-checked

## Summary

Richard Dawkins and Ian McEwan discuss whether scientific explanation drains the world of mystery or creates new grounds for wonder. The central issue is the familiar split between explaining **how** a phenomenon works and asking what, if anything, it **means**. Dawkins argues from evolutionary biology and scientific explanation; McEwan brings the perspective of a novelist interested in experience, imagination and moral life.

The episode is useful because it refuses the easy claim that science and poetry occupy sealed domains. Both can attend closely to the same world, but they justify statements differently. That makes the programme a source for separating empirical explanation from aesthetic or existential interpretation without assuming that one must erase the other.
