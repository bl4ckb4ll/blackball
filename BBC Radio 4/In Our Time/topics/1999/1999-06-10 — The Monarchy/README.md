# The Monarchy

- **Broadcast:** 10 June 1999
- **BBC programme ID:** `p00545k6`
- **BBC:** https://www.bbc.co.uk/programmes/p00545k6
- **Coverage:** cross-checked

## Summary

David Cannadine and Bea Campbell discuss how the British monarchy survived democratic and social change by adapting its public rituals, family image and claims to national representation. Cannadine brings a historian's work on ceremonial and “invented tradition”; Campbell brings feminist and political criticism. The episode is useful for treating prestige as something institutions actively manufacture and reproduce rather than as a self-explanatory consequence of antiquity.
