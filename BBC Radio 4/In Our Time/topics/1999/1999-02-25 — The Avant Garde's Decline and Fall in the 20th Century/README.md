# The Avant Garde's Decline and Fall in the 20th Century

- **Coverage:** cross-checked

## Summary

Eric Hobsbawm and Frances Morris discuss the twentieth-century avant-garde and the claim that modern visual art reached an institutional or aesthetic dead end. Hobsbawm brings social history and a skeptical account of modernism's historical role; Morris brings the perspective of a museum curator responsible for modern art. The disagreement is therefore also institutional: how much should judgments about artistic success depend on the art market, museums, expert critics or broader audiences?
