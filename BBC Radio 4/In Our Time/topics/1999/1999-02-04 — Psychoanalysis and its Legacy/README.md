# Psychoanalysis and its Legacy

- **Broadcast:** 4 February 1999
- **BBC programme ID:** `p00545cg`
- **BBC:** https://www.bbc.co.uk/programmes/p00545cg
- **Coverage:** cross-checked

## Summary

Juliet Mitchell and Adam Phillips discuss Freud's legacy a century after *The Interpretation of Dreams*. They consider whether psychoanalysis is best understood as science, interpretation, clinical practice or a vocabulary through which modern people learned to describe themselves. Mitchell brings psychoanalysis together with feminism and social theory; Phillips brings clinical practice and literary interpretation. The episode is especially useful for asking what kind of evidence can confirm or disconfirm an interpretive framework.
