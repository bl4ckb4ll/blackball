# Animal Experiments and Rights

- **Broadcast:** 18 March 1999
- **BBC programme ID:** `p00545f8`
- **BBC:** https://www.bbc.co.uk/programmes/p00545f8
- **Coverage:** cross-checked

## Summary

Colin Blakemore and Lynda Birke discuss the scientific use of animals and the moral status of the animals subjected to experimentation. Blakemore argues from neuroscience and the claimed benefits of experimental research; Birke combines biology with scholarship on human-animal relations. The episode's strongest analytical question is not whether research has benefits in the abstract, but what evidence of necessity, alternatives, suffering and expected benefit should be required before coercive experimentation is justified.
