# The Nation State

- **Broadcast:** 14 October 1999
- **BBC programme ID:** `p005462v`
- **BBC:** https://www.bbc.co.uk/programmes/p005462v
- **Coverage:** cross-checked

## Summary

Norman Davies and Andrew Marr discuss the historical construction of Britain, England and the United Kingdom and the pressures placed on the nation-state by devolution and changing identities at the end of the twentieth century. Davies brings European and British history; Marr brings political journalism. The episode is useful for remembering that states and national identities are institutions with histories, not natural units that explain themselves.
