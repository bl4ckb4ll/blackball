# Fundamentalism

- **Broadcast:** 22 April 1999
- **BBC programme ID:** `p00545gy`
- **BBC:** https://www.bbc.co.uk/programmes/p00545gy
- **Coverage:** cross-checked

## Summary

Karen Armstrong and Tariq Ali discuss modern religious fundamentalism across traditions and the political conditions in which absolutist movements grow. Armstrong brings comparative religious history; Ali brings political history and criticism of empire and authoritarianism. The episode treats fundamentalism as a modern political and social phenomenon rather than simply as unusually intense private belief, and asks how secular power, colonial history and social disruption interact with religious movements.
