## Summary

Neil Johnson and Lee Smolin discuss how physics changed the concept of time from Newton's universal clock through Einsteinian relativity and into quantum theory and modern cosmology. Johnson brings theoretical physics; Smolin brings quantum gravity and cosmology. The episode is a compact example of a concept that seems intuitively obvious but becomes technically different once measurement and theory improve: ordinary experience of time, clock measurement and the role of time in fundamental equations are related but not identical.
