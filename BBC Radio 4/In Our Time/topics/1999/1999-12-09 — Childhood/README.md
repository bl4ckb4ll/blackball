- **Coverage:** cross-checked

## Summary

Christina Hardyment and Theodore Zeldin discuss the historical transformation of childhood in the twentieth-century West: later entry into paid work, more years in school, smaller households and a stronger cultural separation between children and adults. The episode makes clear that “childhood” is partly biological but also institutional. School-leaving ages, labour law, family income and consumer markets change what children are allowed or required to do.
