## Summary

Mary Warnock and Ted Wragg debate what education is for: cultivated knowledge, citizenship, practical competence, occupational preparation, social mobility or some mixture. The programme considers the historical movement from elite education toward mass schooling and the persistent conflict between liberal and vocational curricula. For Blackball the episode is useful only if educational claims are followed through to outcomes: what is taught, who gets access, what students can actually do afterward, and whether credentials open consequential opportunities.
