- **BBC:** https://www.bbc.co.uk/programmes/p00545bs
- **Coverage:** cross-checked

## Summary

Will Self and Roger Scruton debate whether late-twentieth-century culture represented decline, democratization, fragmentation or expansion. Scruton approaches modern culture through aesthetics, tradition and criticism of modernism; Self approaches it as a working novelist and satirist suspicious of inherited cultural hierarchies. The episode is useful for separating claims about artistic quality from claims about who has authority to define a canon and which institutions give some tastes more prestige than others.
