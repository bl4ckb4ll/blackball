## Summary

Anthony O'Hear and Adam Phillips discuss the Enlightenment idea of progress and whether scientific and technological advance can be assumed to produce moral or social improvement. Darwin and Freud complicate older pictures of rational mastery, while twentieth-century history makes simple upward narratives difficult to sustain. This is a core Blackball topic: “progress” should be disaggregated. A technology may improve output, speed or knowledge while worsening coercion, bargaining power, environmental exposure or distribution.
