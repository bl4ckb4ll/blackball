## Summary

Patrick Wall and Semir Zeki discuss pain as a biological signal and a subjective experience. Wall brings physiology and major work on pain mechanisms; Zeki brings neurobiology and visual neuroscience. The episode illustrates a recurring measurement problem: neural activity and observable injury are relevant evidence, but the thing clinicians ultimately need to explain and treat is experienced by the person. A measure can correlate with suffering without exhausting what suffering is.
