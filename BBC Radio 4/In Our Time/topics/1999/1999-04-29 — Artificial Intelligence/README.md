# Artificial Intelligence

- **Broadcast:** 29 April 1999
- **BBC programme ID:** `p00545h7`
- **BBC:** https://www.bbc.co.uk/programmes/p00545h7
- **Coverage:** cross-checked

## Summary

Igor Aleksander and John Searle debate whether a machine can genuinely think or be conscious rather than merely produce behavior interpreted as intelligent. Aleksander approaches the question through artificial neural systems and machine consciousness; Searle brings philosophy of mind and his critique of computational accounts of understanding. The episode is a useful historical checkpoint: claims now made about contemporary machine learning can be compared with older distinctions among performance, internal mechanism, understanding and consciousness.
