# Writing and Political Oppression

- **Broadcast:** 8 April 1999
- **BBC programme ID:** `p00545g8`
- **BBC:** https://www.bbc.co.uk/programmes/p00545g8
- **Coverage:** cross-checked

## Summary

Nadine Gordimer and Ariel Dorfman discuss writing under censorship, dictatorship, apartheid, exile and political violence. Both guests wrote from direct engagement with repressive political systems, so the discussion concerns not merely fictional representations of oppression but the costs and responsibilities of public speech. The episode asks what writers can document that official institutions suppress and what happens when political urgency competes with artistic independence.
