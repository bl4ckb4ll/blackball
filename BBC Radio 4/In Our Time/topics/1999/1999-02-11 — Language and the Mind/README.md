# Language and the Mind

- **Broadcast:** 11 February 1999
- **BBC programme ID:** `p00545cr`
- **BBC:** https://www.bbc.co.uk/programmes/p00545cr
- **Coverage:** cross-checked

## Summary

Jonathan Miller and Steven Pinker discuss how language is acquired, represented in the mind and related to human biology. The episode draws on the Chomskyan claim that language depends on innate structure while also asking what learning, culture and development contribute. Pinker brings cognitive science and evolutionary psychology; Miller brings medicine, theatre and intellectual history. A recurring issue is how evidence for biological capacity should be distinguished from claims that particular languages or social uses of language are biologically fixed.
