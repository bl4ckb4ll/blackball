# Shakespeare and Literary Criticism

- **Broadcast:** 4 March 1999
- **BBC programme ID:** `p00545dp`
- **BBC:** https://www.bbc.co.uk/programmes/p00545dp
- **Coverage:** cross-checked

## Summary

Harold Bloom and Jacqueline Rose discuss why Shakespeare retains extraordinary literary and academic authority and what modern criticism does with that authority. Bloom emphasizes Shakespeare's imaginative and psychological power; Rose brings psychoanalytic, feminist and political criticism. The episode makes visible a basic question for any canon: whether prestige reflects durable achievement, institutional inheritance, or both, and how new critical methods alter what readers notice in old texts.
