# Genetic Determinism

- **Broadcast:** 23 September 1999
- **BBC programme ID:** `p005461w`
- **BBC:** https://www.bbc.co.uk/programmes/p005461w
- **Coverage:** cross-checked

## Summary

Steve Jones and Matt Ridley discuss what genes determine and, equally important, what they do not. The programme moves from Mendelian inheritance and Darwinian evolution to late-twentieth-century claims that complex behavior can be read directly from DNA. Both guests are geneticists or science writers steeped in genetics, but the discussion exposes the need to distinguish genetic contribution, heritability, developmental interaction and deterministic prediction. For Blackball this is a core source against converting a statistical association into a claim of fixed biological destiny.
