# Architecture in the 20th Century

- **Broadcast:** 25 March 1999
- **BBC programme ID:** `p00545fm`
- **BBC:** https://www.bbc.co.uk/programmes/p00545fm
- **Coverage:** cross-checked

## Summary

Daniel Libeskind and Richard Weston discuss twentieth-century architecture as art, public infrastructure and a visible expression of institutional power. The episode considers spectacular buildings, historical memory and the use of public resources. Libeskind brings the practice of designing symbolically charged buildings; Weston brings architectural history and criticism. For Blackball the useful question is who gets to commission monumental space, who pays for it, and how the benefits are evaluated against ordinary needs.
