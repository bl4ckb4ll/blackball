## Summary

Ian Stewart and Brian Butterworth discuss mathematics both as a formal body of knowledge and as a human cognitive capacity. Stewart brings research mathematics and mathematical exposition; Butterworth brings cognitive neuroscience, including work on numerical cognition. The pairing helps distinguish mathematics itself from performance on particular mathematical tasks, and acquired education from more elementary capacities for quantity and number.
