# Multiculturalism

- **Broadcast:** 13 May 1999
- **BBC programme ID:** `p00545hw`
- **BBC:** https://www.bbc.co.uk/programmes/p00545hw
- **Coverage:** cross-checked

## Summary

Stuart Hall and Avtar Brah discuss migration, race, national identity and the politics grouped under the word multiculturalism. Both reject the picture of cultures as sealed, timeless units simply transported from one place to another. Hall brings cultural studies and postcolonial theory; Brah brings sociology, feminism and work on diaspora. The episode is useful for asking whether institutions distribute rights and resources equally even when they formally recognize cultural diversity.
