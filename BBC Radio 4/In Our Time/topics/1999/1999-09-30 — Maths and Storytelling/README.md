# Maths and Storytelling

- **Broadcast:** 30 September 1999
- **BBC programme ID:** `p0054625`
- **BBC:** https://www.bbc.co.uk/programmes/p0054625
- **Coverage:** cross-checked

## Summary

John Allen Paulos and Marina Warner explore why humans find patterns in both numbers and stories and where analogies between mathematical and narrative structure are illuminating or misleading. Paulos brings mathematics, probability and work on innumeracy; Warner brings literary history, myth and storytelling. The episode is useful for distinguishing a mathematically demonstrated pattern from a pattern merely perceived in a story, market or social history.

## Blackball hooks

- demonstrated pattern versus suggestive analogy
- innumeracy and susceptibility to quantitative rhetoric
- storytelling as an aid to understanding and a source of false coherence
