# The Novel

- **Broadcast:** 11 November 1999

- **Coverage:** cross-checked

## Summary

D. J. Taylor and Gillian Beer discuss the novel's historical development, its ability to represent extended lives and social worlds, and predictions that new media would make it obsolete. Taylor brings fiction, criticism and literary biography; Beer brings literary history and scholarship on relations between literature and science. The episode offers a useful historical record of technological displacement claims that can later be compared with what actually happened to reading and publishing.
