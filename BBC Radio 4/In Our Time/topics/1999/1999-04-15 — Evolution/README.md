# Evolution

- **Broadcast:** 15 April 1999
- **BBC programme ID:** `p00545gl`
- **BBC:** https://www.bbc.co.uk/programmes/p00545gl
- **Coverage:** cross-checked

## Summary

John Maynard Smith and Colin Tudge discuss evolutionary theory, genetic mechanisms and the common mistake of equating evolution with inevitable progress. Maynard Smith brings population genetics and evolutionary game theory; Tudge brings biology and science writing. The episode is especially useful for Blackball because adaptation is local to an environment and criterion: it does not imply a universal ladder on which organisms, people or societies can be ranked from inferior to superior.
