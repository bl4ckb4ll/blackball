# The Universe's Origins

- **Broadcast:** 20 May 1999
- **BBC programme ID:** `p00545j9`
- **BBC:** https://www.bbc.co.uk/programmes/p00545j9
- **Coverage:** cross-checked

## Summary

Martin Rees and Paul Davies discuss what late-twentieth-century cosmology could infer about the origin and large-scale structure of the universe and about the conditions required for life. Rees brings observational cosmology and astrophysics; Davies brings theoretical physics and science communication. The episode is useful as a case study in inference from evidence that cannot be experimentally reproduced at full scale: models are constrained by observations, but claims about beginnings and other worlds necessarily combine measurement with theory.
