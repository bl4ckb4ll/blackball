# Space in Religion and Science

- **Broadcast:** 18 February 1999

- **BBC:** https://www.bbc.co.uk/programmes/p00545d1
- **Coverage:** cross-checked

## Summary

John Polkinghorne and Margaret Wertheim discuss how conceptions of space moved from theological and cosmological frameworks through relativity and into the late-century language of cyberspace. Polkinghorne combines physics and theology; Wertheim approaches science historically and culturally. The episode asks whether new technical spaces are genuinely new domains or metaphors built from older religious and scientific ideas.
