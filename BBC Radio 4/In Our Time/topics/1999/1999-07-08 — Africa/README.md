## Summary

Henry Louis Gates Jr. and Anthony Sampson discuss how European colonialism, missionary activity, anthropology, resource extraction and later media narratives shaped Western ideas of Africa. Gates brings African and African-American literary and cultural scholarship; Sampson brings journalism and long engagement with South Africa. The episode is useful when the category “Africa” is treated as though a continent with many histories were one social object: the first task is to identify whose description, which place, which period and which institutions produced the claim.
