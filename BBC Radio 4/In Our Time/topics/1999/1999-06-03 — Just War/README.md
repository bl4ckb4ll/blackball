## Summary

John Keane and Niall Ferguson examine the Just War tradition from Augustine and Aquinas to modern interstate war. The episode asks whether criteria such as legitimate authority, proportionality, just cause and discrimination between combatants and civilians actually constrain rulers or mainly provide a vocabulary through which wars are justified after political leaders have already chosen them. The distinction between a rule's formal existence and its enforcement is central.
