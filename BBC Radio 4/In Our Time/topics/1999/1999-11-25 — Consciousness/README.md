# Consciousness

- **Broadcast:** 25 November 1999
- **BBC programme ID:** `p005464j`
- **BBC:** https://www.bbc.co.uk/programmes/p005464j
- **Coverage:** cross-checked

## Summary

Ted Honderich and Roger Penrose discuss whether consciousness can be fully explained in physical or computational terms. Honderich brings philosophy of mind and determinism; Penrose brings mathematics and physics and his arguments that human understanding may not reduce to conventional computation. The episode is useful as a record of competing claims whose premises should be kept separate: neural dependence, computability, subjective experience and metaphysical conclusions are not interchangeable propositions.
