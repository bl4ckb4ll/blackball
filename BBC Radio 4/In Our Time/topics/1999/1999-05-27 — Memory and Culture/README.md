## Summary

Malcolm Bowie and Nancy Wood discuss the relation between individual memory and collective memory: how societies decide what to commemorate, suppress or reinterpret after conflict and political violence. The programme considers cases including Germany, South Africa, Northern Ireland and Chile, as well as the role of photographs and other media. Its central distinction is between memory as evidence and memory as an object of study; what people remember matters even when memory is partial or contested.
