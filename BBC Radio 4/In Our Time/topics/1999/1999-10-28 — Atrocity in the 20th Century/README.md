# Atrocity in the 20th Century

## Summary

Jonathan Glover and Gwen Adshead discuss the moral psychology and institutional conditions that allow ordinary people and organized states to participate in extreme cruelty. Glover brings ethics and historical analysis of twentieth-century mass violence; Adshead brings forensic psychiatry and direct clinical work with serious offenders. For Blackball the episode belongs beside the delegated-coercion material: atrocity requires not only leaders or ideologies but people, roles, routines and organizations that turn commands into acts.
