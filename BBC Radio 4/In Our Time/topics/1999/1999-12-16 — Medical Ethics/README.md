# Medical Ethics

- **Broadcast:** 16 December 1999
- **BBC programme ID:** `p005465c`
- **BBC:** https://www.bbc.co.uk/programmes/p005465c
- **Coverage:** cross-checked

## Summary

Barry Jackson and Sheila McLean discuss how rapidly expanding medical capabilities create ethical questions that technical success cannot answer by itself. Jackson brings surgical practice and professional leadership; McLean brings medical law and ethics. The episode asks who decides when an intervention is justified, how consent and professional authority should work, and whether “can be done” is being silently converted into “ought to be done.”
