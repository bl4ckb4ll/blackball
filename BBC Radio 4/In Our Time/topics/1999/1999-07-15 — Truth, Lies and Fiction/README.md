# Truth, Lies and Fiction

- **Broadcast:** 15 July 1999
- **BBC programme ID:** `p00545lr`
- **BBC:** https://www.bbc.co.uk/programmes/p00545lr
- **Coverage:** cross-checked

## Summary

Elena Lappin and Nick Groom discuss the boundary between fiction and factual testimony through the controversy over Binjamin Wilkomirski's *Fragments*, published as a Holocaust memoir and later challenged as fabricated autobiography. The case makes genre labels evidentially consequential: readers grant testimony a different authority from acknowledged fiction. The episode is therefore a useful source on verification, memory, narrative persuasion and the harm done when invented experience occupies the evidential position of a witness.
