## Summary

Grahame Bulfield and Bryan Appleyard discuss genetic engineering at a moment shaped by Dolly the sheep and the Human Genome Project. The episode asks what follows when organisms can be deliberately altered and when human genetic variation can be mapped at unprecedented scale. Bulfield supplies the research-institution perspective; Appleyard presses on personhood, prediction and the cultural consequences of treating DNA as destiny. The distinction between a technology's technical capacity and the social authority granted to its users is central.
