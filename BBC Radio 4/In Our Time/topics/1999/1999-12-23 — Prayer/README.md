# Prayer

## Summary

Russell Stannard and Andrew Samuels discuss prayer as religious petition, contemplative practice and psychological act. Stannard brings physics and Christian writing; Samuels brings Jungian analysis and psychology. The programme distinguishes several questions often conflated in debates about prayer: whether prayer changes external events, whether it changes the person praying, and what religious traditions mean when they describe an act as effective.
