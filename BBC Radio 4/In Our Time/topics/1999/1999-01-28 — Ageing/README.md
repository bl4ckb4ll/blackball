# Ageing

- **Coverage:** cross-checked

## Summary

Alan Walker and Tom Kirkwood examine ageing as both a biological process and a social institution. Longer lives create demographic and fiscal questions, but the consequences of ageing also depend on pensions, employment, health, housing and the way societies distribute care. Kirkwood brings the biology of senescence; Walker brings social gerontology. The episode therefore resists collapsing the experience of old age into biology alone.
