## Summary

George Steiner and Catherine Belsey discuss tragedy from Aristotle's account of catharsis through Shakespeare and into modernity. The episode asks whether tragedy remains a viable artistic form in societies saturated with reports and images of real catastrophe. Steiner brings comparative literature and a high-cultural account of tragedy; Belsey brings critical theory and historical approaches to literature. The programme is useful for distinguishing aesthetic representations of suffering from suffering as historical evidence.
