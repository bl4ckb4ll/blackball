# Good and Evil

- **Broadcast:** 1 April 1999
- **BBC programme ID:** `p00545g0`
- **BBC:** https://www.bbc.co.uk/programmes/p00545g0
- **Coverage:** cross-checked

## Summary

Leszek Kołakowski and Galen Strawson discuss whether the concepts of good and evil require religion and how modern philosophy, evolutionary theory and psychology altered moral explanation. Kołakowski brings the history of philosophy, Marxism and religion; Strawson brings analytic philosophy and questions about agency. The episode is useful for separating explanations of why people have moral sentiments from arguments about which actions are actually justified.
