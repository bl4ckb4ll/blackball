# Existentialism

- **Broadcast:** 28 June 2001
- **BBC programme ID:** `p00547h8`
- **BBC:** https://www.bbc.co.uk/programmes/p00547h8
- **Coverage:** cross-checked

## Summary

The programme examines a family of arguments associated with Kierkegaard, Nietzsche, Heidegger, Sartre and Beauvoir rather than a single doctrine. Human beings find themselves in a world without a ready-made personal essence, yet must choose and act under conditions they did not choose.

Freedom, anxiety, bad faith, authenticity and responsibility organize the discussion. The guests ask whether existentialism licenses arbitrary self-creation or instead makes people answerable for choices made amid other people, history and mortality.

## Guests

- **[A. C. Grayling](../../../people/A. C. Grayling.md):** sets out the central philosophical claims and tests their coherence from a broader analytic perspective.
- **[Christina Howells](../../../people/Christina Howells.md):** brings specialist knowledge of French existential and post-existential thought.
- **[Simon Critchley](../../../people/Simon Critchley.md):** connects existentialism to continental ethics, finitude and the demands made by other people.

## Analytical hooks

- freedom, responsibility, finitude and meaning
- movement label versus disagreement among named thinkers
- ethical demands made by other people versus solitary self-creation
