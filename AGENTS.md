## Sensitive-topic editorial language

In Blackball-authored writing about sexual violence, name the harm only when necessary to make a factual distinction. Use `r***` in place of the spelled-out term; otherwise prefer precise, restrained wording such as "victimization", "incident", "survey disclosure", or "sexual violence" when context requires. Avoid graphic details, sensationalism, gratuitous repetition, and casual comparisons with other atrocities. Treat affected people with respect; never imply that a model's limitations invalidate their experiences.

Keep incidence, survey disclosure, and reporting to authorities distinct. Preserve evidence and traceability: do not alter original primary documents, recorded quotations, URLs, DOI strings, or archival artifacts merely to impose editorial wording. Public-facing bibliographic titles may be redacted, with the stable DOI or source URL preserving the exact citation. State when a displayed title is editorially shortened or redacted.
