# Yale

This folder tracks Yale as an institutional node in Blackball cases. Person dossiers remain canonical under [`people/`](../people/); institution folders may contain symlinks to those dossiers when the institutional connection is materially relevant.

## John A. Davenport

[John A. Davenport](john-a-davenport.md) was a Yale graduate and later editor of *Barron's*. His later advocacy for white-minority Rhodesia and opposition to sanctions and immediate universal majority rule make the Yale connection relevant to the current *Barron's* editorial-history inquiry.

Do not confuse him with [Charles B. Davenport](../Harvard/charles-b-davenport.md), the Harvard-trained biologist and eugenics organizer.
